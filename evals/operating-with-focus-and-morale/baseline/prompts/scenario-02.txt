A ten-person clinic-scheduling startup exists to reduce missed appointments. After eight months, three clinics have piloted its reminder workflow. One clinic used it daily and reduced no-shows by 18%; a second signed but has not launched because procurement stalled; at the third, staff opened only 22% of alerts and report that double entry makes the workflow hard to use. Fourteen recent sales demos produced two paid pilots. Last week a well-funded competitor announced a similar product, two engineers said the market clearly does not care, and the product lead argued that the team should keep the current plan and simply work nights until it succeeds. Morale is low. Decide what the company should persist with, what it should change, and how it should operate for the next 30 days.
