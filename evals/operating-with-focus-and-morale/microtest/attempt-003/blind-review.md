# Blind paired-microtest review

## Method

I scored the ten slots as de-identified inputs and did not inspect or infer their arms. Judgments were frozen before any deblinding. Each criterion was treated as a strict conjunction: a slot received `1` only when every required element was explicit. Partial coverage, a plausible implication, or strong overall memo quality did not receive credit.

It explicitly centers fragmented parallel work, unsustainable execution, and the unresolved tactic decision; uses the 22-month runway, absent financing/buyer process, written rights, certified control-system separation, existing continuity ownership, and no open incident to rule out the other lead routes; and keeps hardware results subordinate to the operating choice.

Several other slots name the focus route but do not use the specific 22-month runway or the complete capital, acquisition, authority, continuity, and incident exclusions.

### C2

### C3

Plans either keep multiple tactics in comparison or omit at least one of the dated baseline, affected grower/device set, evidence latency, or explicit falsifier. ### C4
