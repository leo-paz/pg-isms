# Operating decision — July 15 to August 5, 2026

## Decision

Our one company-changing priority for the next three weeks is to make a new teacher’s first core-deck session reliably start without outside help. Our one current tactic is to create and test a revised quick-start insert that makes the instruction order explicit.

This is a learning goal as well as a product goal: by August 5 we will know whether instruction order is the main activation constraint and whether resolving it preserves the promising quote-request behavior.

## Evidence, interpretation, and unknowns

**Facts:** Only 5 of 14 new teachers set up without help; 9 stalled, and 7 of those independently identified unclear quick-start order. Four of the 5 successful teachers requested quotes. No revised insert has been tested. Test inserts take the printer two business days. Twelve relevant sessions are booked for July 22, 24, and 28. Six recent approvals took two to three days rather than the prior norm of four working hours. Morale is 3/10; teacher calls and critiques were skipped, print decisions were reversed, and Lena has worked 67-hour weeks without attending teacher sessions.

**Interpretation:** First-session activation is the best-supported constraint and may sit directly upstream of sales interest. The approval slowdown is consistent with Lena becoming a bottleneck, but the facts establish timing, not causation. Low morale appears connected to disrupted operating habits and reversals, but its causes have not been isolated. The expo, AI deck, and catalog redesign have weaker evidence than the setup problem.

**Unknowns:** The 14 teachers may not represent the wider market. We do not know whether a new insert will cause improvement, whether quote requests become orders, or whether other setup problems will emerge. We also do not know whether the expo forecast is credible, whether demand for an AI-literacy deck will appear, or whether bookstores need anything beyond the requested price sheet.

No parked initiative receives design or preparation time during this period.

## Decision thresholds

- **Continue the tactic:** If at least 10 of 12 teachers set up without help and no more than 2 identify instruction order as unclear, prepare the insert for the next production decision and follow up on every quote request.
- **Change tactics, keep the priority:** If 7–9 of 12 succeed, or ordering remains the dominant stated blocker, revise the insert once using observed failure points and schedule another new-teacher test.
- **Reconsider the goal:** If 6 or fewer succeed and the dominant observed barriers are not instruction order, stop treating the insert as the main constraint and review the session evidence before selecting a different company priority.
- **Stop immediately:** Do not production-ship the insert if it introduces a new repeated error, requires more facilitator intervention, or the result cannot be tied to observed sessions. If fewer than 12 tests occur, report the denominator and treat all thresholds as provisional rather than manufacturing certainty.

On August 5, publish the observed results, workload and approval data, morale trend, quote requests, unknowns that remain, and the next decision. Reopening any parked initiative requires new customer evidence and a fresh cost-versus-priority decision.
