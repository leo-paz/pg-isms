# Operating decision — July 15 to August 5, 2026

## Decision

Our one company-changing priority is to make a new teacher's first Mosaic Minute session independently startable. Our one tactic is to produce one minimally reordered quick-start insert and test it consistently in the 12 already-booked new-teacher sessions. The core deck does not change during this test.

This takes precedence because the closest evidence to use and revenue is the first-session bottleneck: 9 of 14 teachers stalled, 7 independently named the instruction order, and 4 of the 5 who got through setup requested quotes. That suggests, but does not prove, that setup clarity is the highest-leverage constraint.

## What we will and will not do

- **Expo:** Do not pay the $8,000 non-refundable deposit on July 17; stop this booth plan. The promised 300 leads is unsupported, while our comparable event produced 41 scans, two demos, and no orders, and this one would consume 90 team-hours.
- **AI-literacy deck:** Hold it through August 5. A competitor announcement and my fear of irrelevance are not customer evidence. Reopen only after at least five teachers or buyers independently identify this as a current need, or equivalent observed usage evidence appears.
- **Wholesale catalog:** Do not redesign it. Send the three bookstores the current price sheet they requested. Reopen only if at least three buyers explicitly say the existing sheet blocks a purchase or next step.
- No one works around these holds. New ideas go into the backlog, not into active work.

## Evidence gates

These are decision thresholds, not predictions:

- **Continue this tactic:** At least 9 of 12 teachers complete setup without help, and no one instruction-sequence stall recurs in three or more sessions. Prepare the insert for a larger validation batch; do not call the result proven from this small sample.
- **Change tactics, keep the priority:** Six to eight complete independently, or a common stall recurs at least three times. Revise the specific failing step and run another prospective test.
- **Stop this insert tactic:** Five or fewer complete independently. Do not print it broadly; return to direct observation and diagnose whether wording, facilitation, deck structure, or something else is blocking setup.
- **Reconsider the priority:** Setup succeeds for at least 9 of 12, but fewer than 3 request a quote or state a concrete next use, or another blocker appears in at least half the sessions. That would weaken the interpretation that setup is the company constraint. Choose the next priority from observed teacher behavior, not a held initiative by default.

## Facts, interpretations, and unknowns

**Facts:** The current setup success is 5/14; 9 stalled; 7 separately cited quick-start order; 4 successful teachers requested quotes. Six approvals recently took two to three days. Morale is 3/10, two calls and two critiques were skipped, two print decisions reversed, and I worked 67-hour weeks with no full day off. The three proposed initiatives have the evidence stated above.

**Interpretations:** Instruction order is probably the nearest actionable constraint. My overload probably caused the approval delay and is contributing to execution instability and morale. The expo, AI deck, and redesign are currently conviction or fear, not validated demand.

**Unknowns:** Whether the insert causes better setup; whether setup success causes quote demand; the exact reasons morale fell; the value of future expo leads; and whether meaningful demand exists for the AI deck or redesign. The next three weeks are designed to reduce those unknowns without spending runway on unsupported bets.
