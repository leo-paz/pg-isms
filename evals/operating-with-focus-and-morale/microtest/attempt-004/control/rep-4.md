# Common Chord operating order: July 15-August 24, 2026

## Decision and objective

Until renewal closes, Common Chord has one improvement objective: **learn whether making rehearsal progress visible improves members’ experience and renewal, while reliably operating the four existing choirs.**

Effective July 15:

- Do not open the fifth choir on August 20. Maya may reconsider a new location only after the August 24 review, using measured member demand, renewal results, staffing capacity, and economics.
- Freeze the rebrand and employer-membership pitch through August 24.
- Decline the produced livestream for this showcase. “Credibility” is not a measured renewal outcome, and production would add work during an overloaded period. Preserve the sponsor relationship by offering a post-renewal discussion.
- Deliver the August 29 showcase at the already-promised scope: honor deposits, accompanists, performer communications, insurance, and incident procedures. Add no production features or discretionary scope.

These are prioritization decisions, not conclusions that the frozen ideas are bad. Their demand and renewal effects are unknown.

## Member-progress test

Ellis creates the test sheet by July 17 and locks it before seeing results.

**Intervention:** at the start of a rehearsal, state one written, observable song goal; at the close, make a brief rehearsal recording that lets members hear performance against that goal. Obtain and document appropriate member consent; use the recording only for this test, share it only with that choir, and delete it on the stated schedule. If current consent or handling practices do not cover this, use a live closing playback/reflection without retaining a recording.

**Design, July 20-August 9:** all four choirs participate in a three-week crossover. Ellis randomly assigns two choirs to intervention/usual/intervention and two to usual/intervention/usual. Keep each choir’s conductor, time, venue, repertoire plan, and ordinary reminders unchanged where feasible. Conductors cannot add a substitute “progress” practice during usual weeks. Log deviations.

After every rehearsal, record:

1. members present / members expected;
2. anonymous one-question response: “I can tell whether I am improving in this choir” (1-5);
3. delivery completed, minutes added, and any consent, technical, or workload problem.

This controls for stable choir and conductor differences better than the earlier two-week observation, but four choirs, time effects, self-report, and crossover spillover still limit causal confidence.

Rowan contacts the 52 low-attendance members from July 20-31 using the same brief, non-pressuring script. Record one primary reason (progress visibility, schedule, price, other), whether they intend to renew, and any requested follow-up. Do not imply that the test is proven to work.

## Dated control points

**July 16 — capacity reset.** Maya and Ellis cut or defer every task not named in this order. Publish rehearsal, inbox, incident, and showcase coverage through August 31. Schedule at least one full day off in every seven for every person starting immediately. For the next two weeks, cap planned work at 50 hours for Maya, 48 for Ellis, and contracted hours for conductors. If mandatory coverage does not fit, Maya buys temporary administrative/showcase help within an explicit cap or reduces uncontracted showcase scope; she does not silently extend hours.

**July 17 — readiness gate.** Ellis confirms test protocol, assignment, consent/recording handling, backup method, and song-sheet two-person check. Rowan confirms one inbox owner and backup every day, with twice-daily queue review so the 24-hour response promise is met. If coverage or safe recording handling is missing, use the no-retained-recording version; do not postpone normal rehearsals.

**July 27 — first operating review (30 minutes).** Review only missed rehearsals, inbox age, song-sheet errors, hours/days off, test delivery, and member issues. Correct execution, not the test design. If the intervention adds more than 15 minutes per rehearsal, causes repeated consent/member concern, or causes a coverage miss, simplify to written goal plus closing live reflection.

**August 3 — second operating review.** Same dashboard. If any member reply exceeds 24 hours, any person misses the day-off rule, or another song sheet is wrong, remove optional showcase work and add/reassign coverage that day. Do not restart frozen projects.

**August 8 — pre-renewal decision.** Ellis analyzes intervention versus usual weeks within each choir. Roll out the practice for August 10-24 only if at least three of four choirs show (a) a higher average progress-clarity score and (b) no attendance decline greater than five percentage points, with no unresolved member or workload problem. If results are mixed, allow it only in choirs where both conditions hold and describe it as an experiment. If results are negative or execution is unreliable, stop it and run normal rehearsals. Preserve the results; do not choose only favorable comments.

**August 10 — renewal opens.** Rowan sends accurate renewal communication to all members and direct, respectful follow-up to low-attendance members. State what Common Chord is doing; do not claim improved outcomes not demonstrated. Track renewal by choir, attendance band, outreach reason, and intervention exposure. Pricing remains unchanged unless Maya records a separate evidence-based decision before launch; the existing evidence does not isolate price as the main issue.

**August 17 — renewal midpoint.** Rowan reports renewals and nonrenewal reasons; Ellis reports continued delivery and burden; Maya reports capacity and cash. Use targeted reminders, not discounts or new initiatives. Any missed core-service standard triggers immediate scope reduction or coverage, not more hours.

**August 24 — decision review.** Record renewal rate overall and by predeclared segments, the crossover results, member reasons, operating errors, staff hours/days off, and data limitations. Decide separately whether to (1) make the progress practice standard, modify it, or stop it; and (2) investigate a fifth choir, livestream, rebrand, or employer plan. No frozen project restarts without a named hypothesis, owner, small test, capacity budget, success/failure threshold, and stop date.

Publish each Monday: attendance by choir; progress-clarity score by condition; renewals after August 10; oldest unanswered member message; song-sheet errors; test deviations; each person’s hours and last full day off; showcase contractual milestones; incidents/member concerns. Unknown or missing values are labeled **unknown**, never estimated.
