## Learning plan: visible progress

Ellis assigns two choirs to Cohort A and two to Cohort B on **July 17**, balancing recent attendance and using different conductors where possible. Record the assignment before viewing new outcomes.

For each choir, establish:

- attendance for each of the previous six rehearsals and each new rehearsal;
- a one-question anonymous pulse before the trial and weekly afterward: “I can tell whether my singing is improving” (1-5);
- conductor minutes required for the tactic;
- song-sheet errors, replies exceeding 24 hours, and missed coverage.

The tactic is exactly one written song goal at rehearsal start plus one short closing audio recording that members can use to compare against the goal. Use a common template; no editing or production. Record/share only under existing consent, offer a no-recording path, restrict access to that choir, and delete trial files after the renewal analysis.

- **July 20-August 2:** Cohort A uses the tactic; Cohort B runs normal rehearsals.
- **July 24:** Ellis checks fidelity and burden only. Fix inconsistent delivery, but do not change the success rule.
- **August 3 checkpoint:** Continue A and start B for one week unless there is a stop condition. This tests whether the result appears with other conductors while giving every choir the experience before renewal.
- **Stop immediately** if the tactic causes missed incident coverage, a reply breach attributable to it, an additional song-sheet error, a consent failure, or more than 15 minutes of conductor work per rehearsal. Ellis may retain the written goal alone if it stays under the limit; document that as a different tactic.
- **August 10 decision:** Make the full tactic the rehearsal standard through August 24 only if at least three of four choirs improve the clarity score by at least 0.5 points from their own baseline, no choir's attendance falls more than five percentage points versus its six-rehearsal baseline, and no stop condition occurs. If two or fewer meet the rule, stop claiming the tactic works and use normal rehearsals. Preserve the results either way.

These thresholds are operating rules, not statistical proof. Four choir-level observations cannot isolate the conductor, seasonality, or novelty. Renewal effect remains unknown until members decide.
