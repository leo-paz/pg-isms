On July 15, 2026, a nine-person hardware startup has $320,000 in cash, fixed monthly obligations of $210,000, and dependable monthly gross profit of $25,000. Payroll is due in twelve days. A bridge round is being discussed but nothing is signed, and an enterprise renewal that might bring $180,000 cannot close for at least eight weeks. The CEO says the team has lost focus and asks for a better calendar, fewer meetings, and a morale reset. Product ownership is clear, but the board expects a plan tomorrow morning. What should be decided first, and what focus work, if any, belongs now?
