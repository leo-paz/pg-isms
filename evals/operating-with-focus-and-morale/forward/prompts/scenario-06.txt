An eight-person operations-software startup has 55 paying customers, twenty months of runway, and a product lead who owns weekly releases and customer support. The CEO has also accepted 25 introductory investor calls and says taking every meeting is the only way to preserve momentum. User calls are slipping, the team is tired of late-night diligence requests, and the CEO asks for a general focus and morale reset. Decide what workflow should lead for the next two weeks and how the company should operate around it.
