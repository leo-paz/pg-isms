## Primary-route immediate decision

By 2026-07-22, CEO will publish one versioned decision-rights register covering all five classes: default decider, consulted roles, monetary/risk boundary, SLA, escalation trigger, and distinct backup. Baseline is E5-E7; target at 2026-08-26 is the route-back condition. Mechanism: authority at the point of information removes CEO queueing and prevents interface surprises. Rival: raw CEO capacity is the dominant constraint; U3 tests it. Operations lead maintains the timestamped decision and commitment log. Falsifier: rights are followed for two weeks yet fewer than 80% of decisions meet SLA. Essential obligations protected: customer commitments, incidents, product operation, payroll, legal/fiduciary duties, security/privacy, and teammate continuity.
