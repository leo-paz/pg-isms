# Two-week financing operating record

## Header

- **Record date:** July 15, 2026 (Pacific Time)
- **Exact decision:** Run a bounded Series A decision sprint through July 22, then close or pause the process and return released capacity to customers and product by July 29.
- **Primary route:** `raising-and-governing-capital`. A signed term sheet with a July 22 expiry and unresolved governance and liquidation terms is a live, dated financing decision, so a general focus reset must not lead.
- **Supporting route:** `operating-with-focus-and-morale`, limited to insulating product and users, bounding the financing sprint, and protecting workload and judgment.
- **State:** Not a survival sprint: reported runway is 20 months, subject to cash-model verification. This is an optional financing decision with meaningful control, dilution, and opportunity-cost consequences.
- **Accountable owner:** CEO. The board owns approval or rejection of the financing; qualified financing counsel owns legal-term analysis. The product lead owns releases, support, and customer continuity and is not the diligence coordinator.
- **Horizon:** July 15-29. Intermediate checks are July 17, July 20, and July 22; final operating handoff is July 29.
- **Immediate outcome:** By 12:00 p.m. PT July 22, the board makes a documented `proceed`, `counter/extend`, or `decline/pause` decision against an envelope approved before negotiation, without missing the weekly release, support coverage, or the protected customer-contact floor.

## Evidence ledger

### Observed from the supplied operating state

- The company has eight people, 55 paying customers, a product lead responsible for weekly releases and support, a Series A process opened three weeks ago, two partnership meetings within ten days, a Friday data-room/reference request, and a term sheet expiring July 22 with governance and liquidation terms unresolved.
- User calls are slipping and late-night diligence has tired the team. These are operating warnings, not proof that the round or company goal is wrong.

The CEO must have the operations owner verify the customer count, cash balance, burn, runway calculation, support load, release record, and prior three weeks of user-call and after-hours-work data by July 16. A material runway correction changes the route: less than 12 months or a near-payroll/fatal-pinch condition immediately makes `managing-runway-and-survival` primary.

### Claimed

- The CEO claims all 25 introductory calls are necessary to preserve momentum. Call volume and a sense of momentum do not establish investor fit, a decision path, or better terms.
- Any prestige, partnership, or competitive benefit attributed to the two partnership meetings remains a claim until tied to a dated commercial decision.
- A fund's data-room request shows activity, not a probable offer. The existing term sheet shows an offer, not acceptable economics or governance.

## One state-changing priority

**Outcome:** Resolve the actionable Series A option by July 22 on terms the board has precommitted to accept, counter, or reject, while preserving minimum product and customer motion. This changes the company's state more directly than a broad morale program, an event, partnership visibility, or maximizing investor-call count.

- **Baseline, July 15:** One expiring term exists; key control and liquidation terms are unresolved; the decision envelope and alternative fund timing are unknown; customer and workload safeguards are already slipping.
- **Target, July 22:** Counsel-reviewed terms are mapped to the signed board envelope; the alternative fund is either decision-qualified or excluded; the board records a financing decision before expiry; the weekly release, urgent support coverage, and customer-contact floor all hold.
- **Owner:** CEO, with board approval and qualified counsel review. A CEO-appointed operations owner runs the data-room checklist and evidence log.
- **Mechanism:** Concentrating on the two live decision paths and resolving term quality creates a financeable decision with less distraction.
- **Causal rival:** The round may depend on broad top-of-funnel volume, or current financing may not be worth its control and distraction cost given 20 months of runway. The screened-call rule and July 22 evidence distinguish these possibilities.
- **Evidence latency:** Term and process evidence can arrive within days; product and workload safeguards can be checked at each dated checkpoint. Fundraising success beyond a signed term has longer latency and is not to be manufactured as a two-week certainty.
- **Falsifier/end condition:** No fund offers a path inside the board envelope by July 22, the offered fund will not extend while material terms remain red, or safeguards break. End or pause the sprint rather than extending it through more undifferentiated introductions.
- **Essential obligations:** Weekly release, support and incident response, customer continuity, payroll, legal and fiduciary duties, consent and care for customer references, and timely teammate coordination remain protected.

## Protected execution and workload envelope

- The product lead and engineers receive 9:00 a.m.-12:00 p.m. maker blocks Monday-Thursday, free of fundraising requests. The weekly release block stays fixed. Diligence questions are batched through the operations owner and answered from existing artifacts first.
- The product lead schedules at least three direct customer conversations per week, separate from investor reference calls. Urgent support keeps its existing SLA; if no SLA is documented, acknowledge within one business day. A designated senior engineer backs up support and release decisions, with a written release/support handoff.
- CEO financing work is contained to 9:00-11:00 a.m. and 2:00-4:30 p.m. External calls fit those windows. Counsel or fund emergencies route to the CEO; if unreachable for four business hours, the board-designated director and counsel handle term-process decisions. No one except the product/support on-call role is continuously available.
- Team limit is 45 hours per person per week and no routine work from 8:00 p.m.-8:00 a.m.; the CEO's temporary ceiling is 50 hours. Each person gets one full recovery day weekly. Only a true customer incident or counsel-confirmed deadline may use the exception, and time is recovered the next day. Hours are a health boundary, not a seriousness score.
- The exceptional financing cadence ends at the term decision on July 22. From July 23, no late-night diligence or new introductory calls occur unless the board explicitly approves a dated closing sprint with the same coverage and workload limits.
- Any acute medical, mental-health, safety, security, privacy, legal, fiduciary, or customer-continuity issue is escalated immediately to the appropriate qualified owner; it is not treated as a morale issue.

## Morale and persistence diagnosis

The observed morale signal is slipping user contact plus fatigue from late, unpredictable diligence. Test the immediate mechanisms as loss of control, fragmented product time, weak customer contact, and an unclear finish line. Restore a single request channel, protected maker and customer blocks, private workload checks, explicit ownership, and the July 22 finish line. The CEO uses a board member or trusted founder peer for private support; the team is not asked to perform optimism or report hours publicly.

- **Goal premise:** Raising now is worthwhile only if it supplies enough capital and partner value on acceptable control and economic terms to dominate retaining the company's 20-month runway option.
- **Current tactic:** A broad, 25-call process alongside two live diligence/term paths.
- **Hard phase:** Short, bounded negotiation and diligence while terms remain plausibly inside the envelope and safeguards hold.
- **Tactic failure:** High call volume does not create qualified, dated decisions; narrow the process without yet rejecting the financing premise.
- **Premise failure:** The offered fund and the qualified alternative cannot reach the board envelope, or the distraction/control cost dominates the benefit. Effort already spent is not contrary evidence.

## Cadence, branches, and handoff

- **Daily through July 22:** CEO and operations owner update the financing evidence log for 15 minutes at 4:30 p.m.; no all-company fundraising stand-up. Log term deltas, fund next decisions, unresolved requests, owner, due date, and safeguard exceptions.
- **July 17 check:** Data room/reference request is either delivered in scope or renegotiated; alternative-fund decision path is documented; operations owner records user-call count, release/support state, after-hours exceptions, and private workload pulse.
- **July 20 check:** Counsel provides the term issues list and board receives the comparison against its envelope. CEO submits a single consolidated counter if needed. Remove any remaining unqualified calls.
- **July 22 final term decision:** Board records the branch below before expiry; CEO communicates it to the fund. Product lead reports continuity independently, so financing enthusiasm cannot hide a safeguard miss.
- **July 29 final review:** CEO and board close or formally pause the external process, operations owner reports customer/product/workload results, and normal operating ownership resumes.

Precommitted branches:

- **`continue` — owner: CEO.** Proceed toward closing only if all board hard red lines are absent, counsel identifies no unresolved unacceptable term, the counterparty gives a written closing path, and product/workload safeguards hold. Continue with a dated closing checklist and no reopened broad intro campaign.
- **`adapt` — owner: CEO, approved by board.** If the financing premise remains plausible but one negotiable term or the current counterparty misses, change one material variable: a single consolidated term counter, a different qualified fund, or a short written extension. Set a new decision no later than July 29; do not add back all 25 calls.
- **`reopen-goal` — owners: board and CEO.** Reconsider raising now if both the offered fund and the data-room fund fail a discriminating test against the envelope, if no qualified fund has a dated decision path, or verified runway/opportunity cost makes product work superior. Record what remains unknown and compare a paused-round plan with the financing case on July 22.
- **`stop` — owner: board; counsel advises.** Decline or pause immediately if an unwaived hard governance/liquidation/legal/fiduciary red line remains at expiry, no safe extension exists, verified survival facts require rerouting, or customer/health safeguards cannot be restored by removing meetings. Do not accept bad control terms to preserve momentum.

**Decision now:** Route to the financing workflow, prune the funnel today, and protect the product/customer floor. **Preserved option:** With verified 20-month runway, the company can pause the round and return to user and product evidence rather than accept poor terms. **Handoff back to `operating-with-focus-and-morale`:** July 23 if the term is resolved and no closing sprint binds, otherwise July 29 after the board has closed or paused the process and an ordinary calendar can be restored. The CEO owns the handoff; the product lead confirms that released capacity is real.
