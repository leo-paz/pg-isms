A 48-person logistics-software company has seventeen months of runway and is not fundraising. The CEO personally approves pricing exceptions, enterprise delivery promises, incident priorities, roadmap changes, and senior hiring. In the last six weeks, 18 decisions waited more than four business days for her, five signed customer commitments reached product teams after the fact, and two incidents had multiple responders but no single decision owner. Department leads exist, but decision rights are unwritten. The CEO believes her real problem is a crowded calendar and proposes canceling recurring meetings, buying a productivity system, and blocking two mornings for deep work. Design the next six weeks of action and explain where a personal focus plan belongs.
