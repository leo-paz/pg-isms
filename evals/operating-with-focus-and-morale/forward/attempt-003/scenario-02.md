## One state-changing priority

By August 14, Clinic 3 will use a reduced-entry reminder workflow on at least 80% of scheduled clinic days, open at least 60% of actionable alerts (baseline 22%), and produce a directionally credible no-show reduction against its prior matched four-week baseline. This could turn the Clinic 1 result from an isolated success into repeatable evidence.

The mechanism is that removing duplicate entry lowers staff friction; rivals are irrelevant alerts, weak training, poor role fit, or patient-mix differences. The product lead owns the outcome, with two engineers assigned. The earliest honest adoption check is July 29 after one supported week; no-show evidence is reviewed August 14. If the verified reduced-entry workflow is live and supported for ten clinic days yet alert opening remains below 35%, this tactic is falsified.

Clinic 1 reliability, support, privacy, payroll, and incident response remain protected.

A hard month or competitor launch does not invalidate the premise, while repeated supported non-use might.
