## One priority

By August 14, make clinic C a second sustained usable deployment: raise alert opens from 22% to at least 60% over the final ten weekdays, with use on at least eight of those days, while clinic A remains active and support/privacy safeguards hold. This can distinguish a fixable workflow problem from weak value faster than more demos. Proposed mechanism: removing double entry makes alerts easy enough to act on. Rival: staff do not value the reminder outcome or lack authority/time regardless of friction. Earliest honest signal is one full week after release. Falsifier: after two materially different, observed-user-informed iterations, opens remain below 40% and four of five staff say the workflow is not worth using.

## Morale, persistence, and decision rules

Treat morale as a signal, not a market verdict. Track reversals, defects, missed user sessions, decision waits, and a private twice-weekly 1–5 energy check. Restore agency through named ownership, twice-weekly clinic feedback, visible adoption results, and a clear August 14 finish line. The goal premise is “a usable reminder workflow can materially reduce missed appointments.” The current tactic is “remove verified workflow friction at clinic C,” not “work longer.”

At each check, the product lead records opens, active days, task time, staff feedback, defects, confounders, and safeguard status. **Continue** if the 60%/8-day target is met and safeguards hold. **Adapt** if opens are 40–59% or task time improves without sustained use: change one observed blocker or segment and set one new bounded test. **Reopen-goal** if the two discriminating iterations remain below 40%, clinic A's effect fails validation, or repeated interviews reject the outcome itself; CEO decides with the data and clinic owners. **Stop** immediately for a survival, legal/privacy, clinical-safety, or customer-harm boundary, or if both clinics withdraw. Preserve the code, learning, clinic A continuity, and interview data rather than expanding prematurely. Next decision: CEO, August 14.
