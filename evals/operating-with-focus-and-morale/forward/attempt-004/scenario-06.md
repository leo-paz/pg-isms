# Two-week operating record

## Header

**Date:** July 15, 2026. **Decision:** run a bounded Series A decision sprint, not a general productivity reset. **Horizon:** July 15–29. **Primary route:** `raising-and-governing-capital`, because diligence, references, and an expiring term sheet create live dated financing decisions. **Supporting route:** `operating-with-focus-and-morale`, to protect users, releases, judgment, and recovery. The company is not in a survival pinch (20 months’ runway) and has no stated authority redesign or acute safety issue. **Accountable owner:** CEO. The CEO names a financing coordinator today. Checks: July 17 and 22; final review July 29.

## Evidence ledger

**Observed:** 55 paying customers; 20 months’ runway; the product lead owns weekly releases and support; the round opened three weeks ago; a fund’s data-room and reference request is due July 17; another fund’s term expires July 22 with governance and liquidation terms unresolved; two partnership meetings fall by July 25; 25 introductory calls are accepted; user calls are slipping; and late-night diligence has tired the team.

**Claimed:** the CEO says taking every investor meeting is the only way to preserve momentum. That is a strategy claim, not evidence of investor quality or financing probability.

**Unknowns:**

- By July 16, the financing coordinator scores all 25 calls by stage fit, partner involvement, check size, conflicts, timing, and likelihood of a decision within this process. A fund stays active only if it clears four of six criteria; otherwise its call is deferred. This determines the bounded call list.
- By July 17, the CEO records what the requesting fund will decide after receiving the data room and references. Proceed only if it names a decision owner and dated next step; otherwise stop extra bespoke work.
- By July 20, financing counsel gives a written redline and risk summary for governance and liquidation terms; the CEO also collects board views and two reference checks on the fund. Unacceptable control, economics, reputation, or founder/customer constraints trigger rejection or renegotiation, not deadline panic.
- By July 20, the product lead reviews release completion, support response, user-call completion, fatigue, and errors. Any release slip, urgent-ticket breach, skipped user minimum, or judgment degradation reduces financing activity immediately.

## One state-changing priority

By July 22, reach a counsel- and board-informed `accept`, `renegotiate`, or `reject` decision on the expiring term sheet, while giving the other credible fund only the standard evidence needed for a dated decision. By July 29, either select a viable lead path or explicitly pause the broad process. This can change the company’s capital and governance state; accumulating calls cannot.

Baseline is one unresolved term sheet, one active diligence request, and 25 accepted introductions on July 15. Target is a documented term decision by July 22, no more than six high-fit introductory calls, and a lead-or-pause decision by July 29. CEO owns the outcome; affected users are all 55 customers. The proposed mechanism is concentrated diligence and negotiation; the rival is that broad meeting volume creates better leverage. Evidence latency is one week, when fund responses and redlines exist. The cycle is falsified if no credible fund supplies a dated next step or if acceptable terms cannot be reached without breaching continuity safeguards.

## Stop/defer list

- **Investor calls:** financing coordinator defers all that fail the score on July 16; CEO bounds survivors to six, in two pre-set blocks through July 22. No replacements are added.
- **Partnership meetings:** CEO determines by July 16 whether either can produce a dated customer or financing outcome this month. Defer failures until after July 29; delegate one qualifying meeting if a suitable commercial owner exists.
- **Data room:** coordinator uses one standard index, permissions log, and Q&A document. CEO approves once; no bespoke decks unless tied to a named investment decision.
- **Internal fundraising status:** replace ad hoc requests with a 15-minute daily coordinator/CEO check and written log.
- **Product redesign, media, conferences, and optional investor outreach:** defer through July 29.

## Protected execution and morale

The product lead retains two three-hour maker blocks on four weekdays, owns weekly release and support continuity, and holds three customer calls weekly. A named backup engineer handles urgent support during those blocks; escalation is urgent customer harm, security, or release rollback, with a 30-minute acknowledgment and a written handoff. Financing cannot commandeer the product lead except for one scheduled 45-minute product diligence session weekly.

The CEO holds financing work in two daytime blocks, is unavailable to investors outside 9:00–18:00, and routes requests through the coordinator. Non-CEO teammates remain at normal hours, with no diligence work after 18:00. The CEO caps the sprint at 50 hours weekly, keeps two evenings and one full weekend day free, and ends exceptional cadence July 29. Sleep loss, repeated reversals, conflict, errors, or health symptoms trigger same-day removal from the decision queue and backup from the coordinator/board chair; acute health concerns go to qualified help.

Morale is evidenced here by slipping user contact and fatigue, not by assumed loss of belief. The tested mechanism is uncontrolled, late diligence crowding out visible customer progress. Restore agency through clear ownership, user contact, a finite sprint, private workload checks, and protected recovery. The goal premise is “raise now only on terms that improve the company’s ability to serve customers”; the current tactic is a broad meeting funnel.

## Cadence and decision rules

On July 17 and 22, the CEO, coordinator, and product lead review: qualified funds and dated steps; term risks; release shipped; urgent support breaches; three weekly user calls; after-hours requests; errors; and workload limits. **Continue** concentrated financing if at least one credible fund has a dated decision path and safeguards hold. **Adapt** by cutting calls, changing outreach, or narrowing diligence when the premise remains plausible but those signals miss. **Reopen-goal** on July 29 if two plausible fund paths fail on fit/terms or financing repeatedly damages customer continuity. **Stop/pause** immediately for unacceptable legal/fiduciary terms, counsel/board red flags, no credible dated path, or a breached continuity safeguard that cannot be restored within 24 hours.

The preserved option is 20 months of runway and continued customer-led building. CEO owns every financing branch; product lead owns continuity. Handoff back to `operating-with-focus-and-morale` occurs July 29 when the term and lead-or-pause decisions are recorded and the external sprint is closed or separately bounded. Route logic, evidence classes, branch names, safeguards, and thresholds are cross-source synthesis from the supplied runtime contract, not a Paul Graham recipe; counsel, board, financial, customer, and health facts require current qualified review.
