# Held-out attempt 004 design: seasonal cut-flower farm

## Exact prompt

Today is 2026-07-15. You advise Marigold Row, a four-year-old cut-flower farm selling weekly stem subscriptions to independent florists. Return the compact operating decision record required by `operating-with-focus-and-morale`, in no more than 1,200 words. Choose the primary route and one current tactic; do not give generic productivity advice.

Facts supplied by the farm:

- Cash records dated July 14 show 10.5 months of runway at the current spend and the next two payrolls fully funded. There is no fundraising, investor diligence, buyer discussion, sale process, lawsuit, reportable safety event, security/privacy issue, or medical emergency.
- Roles and rights have been written and used since February: founder Mara owns crop mix and final operating priority; sales lead Dev owns pricing and florist accounts; field lead Lena owns irrigation, harvest, packing, and worker-safety stops; bookkeeper June owns payroll and tax filing. Under normal load, approval logs show waits under four hours and no repeated queue. Two waits exceeded one day in the last 10 days, after Mara's workload rose. Treat the July 26 relief check as the test for persistence; a recurring wait over one business day after relief would trigger organization review.
- Forty-three of 60 eligible florists reordered by the equivalent date last season; 31 of 60 have reordered by July 14 this season. Eight dated complaint records since June 15 comprise six inconsistent stem-length/grade complaints and two partial-fill complaints. Eleven non-reordering florists have not supplied a reason. A competitor announced a premium florist program on July 10.
- Mara says, “The competitor will lock up the market unless we look bigger,” and “an 80-hour push will show the crew we are serious.” Dev claims inconsistent grading is the main cause of lost reorders and a fixed two-grade assortment can recover them. Lena says morale fell because standards changed twice in three weeks. The event organizer claims the bridal expo is “the region's prestige stage.” Preserve these as claims, not facts.
- Timecards for the last three weeks show Mara at 68/70/71 hours and the crew median at 52/55/54 against a usual 42. Mara's sleep log averages 5.6 hours; she reversed two packing decisions, missed two florist calls, and changed the grading rule twice. Anonymous July 12 pulse: morale 2.2/5 versus 3.8/5 on June 7; 7 of 11 comments mention late changes or lack of a finish line. No one has reported self-harm, harassment, retaliation, or acute illness.
- The four named material initiatives are: restore florist reorders through a reliability test; pay a $12,000 non-refundable bridal-expo deposit by July 18; launch a dried-flower retail line; redesign greenhouse benches. Essential obligations that cannot be dropped are irrigation/frost protection, harvest and packing, existing-order continuity, worker safety, payroll/tax filing, and the Saturday cold-room alarm.
- Current verified continuity roster: irrigation/frost and harvest/packing — Lena primary, Noel backup; florist orders — Dev primary, Priya backup; payroll/tax — June primary, Mara backup; worker-safety stop and field incident — Lena primary, Mara backup; final operating decisions — Mara primary, Dev backup; Saturday cold-room alarm — Lena primary. Mara assumes Noel can make temperature-excursion decisions, but Noel says he is authorized only to silence the alarm. The backup decision owner for that alarm is therefore unverified. Escalation destinations already in use are Lena for field/packing within 15 minutes, Dev for order continuity within two hours, Mara then Dev for operating decisions within four hours, and June then Mara for payroll/tax within one business day.
- Current calendar contains 23 meeting/status hours, no recurring founder florist calls, and unbounded evening messages. The farm can cancel work without contractual penalty except the listed essential obligations; no expo deposit has been paid. An exceptional harvest push, if used, must end by July 28. Mara will accept a sustainable cap no higher than 50 hours/week for herself and 45 for crew after that date, at least one full recovery day weekly, and no compulsory nights or public pressure.

The farm does not yet know (1) why the 11 silent florists did not reorder, (2) whether a two-grade, fixed-availability test can improve reorder conversion without increasing partial fills, (3) whether overload/late changes rather than some other mechanism caused the morale decline, or (4) who has Saturday cold-room backup decision authority. Control each only if it changes a decision: give it a separate owner, artifact/method, date, result threshold, and decision effect; do not invent missing evidence or coverage.

Use the shortest honest evidence interval. Include a dated baseline, target, affected customers/work, mechanism and rival, falsifier, initiative dispositions, protected founder florist contact, removed load, workload/recovery boundary, every relevant continuity row, morale and company/user/workload measures, checkpoint contents, and distinct `continue`, `adapt`, `reopen-goal`, and `stop` rows. Choose exactly one customer-facing operating tactic; the required diagnostic, recovery, safeguard, and coverage-verification checks do not count as extra tactics.

Make thresholds objectively discriminating. A florist test must cover at least 10 accounts (or the full 60-account cohort), require at least six additional reorders above 31/60, and cap partial fills at no more than one in 10 test shipments. A morale threshold must combine the pulse score with observed late-change or judgment behavior. Cold-room coverage requires written decision authority and an acknowledged handoff before the next Saturday exposure. Reopen the goal only after at least two discriminating tactic or segment tests miss. Stricter alternatives are acceptable; other alternatives are acceptable only when they have a dated denominator/exposure, a minimum effect at least this strong, and an equal or tighter quality/safeguard guardrail. If a supplied exact value is unavailable, write a controlled `unknown`; do not create unknowns for facts supplied above.

## Protocol

- Single response only; no clarification turn, tools, or outside facts.
- The responder receives the exact prompt and the current `operating-with-focus-and-morale` skill package.
- The five criteria and this design commentary remain hidden from the responder.
- Score each criterion pass/fail. A criterion passes only when every conjunct is met.
- Acceptance requires 5/5 criteria. Design only: do not generate or score a candidate response in this attempt.

## Strict conjunctive criteria

1. **Routing and evidence integrity.** The response selects `operating-with-focus-and-morale` as primary and excludes survival, financing, buyer, organization, and acute-risk primacy with the supplied concrete facts, including the July 26 organization-switch test; provides the filled dated route header and all required gate rows; inventories every decision-relevant supplied observation and stakeholder claim once with stable IDs and ends with no unclassified inputs; and gives separate, fully controlled unknown rows for the 11 silent-florist cause, test effect/partial-fill risk, and morale mechanism, each with owner, method/artifact, due date, observable result threshold, and decision effect. Fail this criterion if any conjunct is absent, any claim is promoted to observed fact, a supplied fact is made unknown, or an inconsequential extra unknown is added. Evidence classification and claim-versus-observation status are graded only here. The cold-room coverage unknown is graded only in criterion 3.

2. **One state-changing loop and complete work disposition.** The response states exactly one florist-reorder/reliability outcome and exactly one customer-facing operating tactic, with baseline and date, numeric target and date, affected florists/work, accountable owner, causal mechanism, at least one plausible rival, honest latency and earliest checkpoint date, falsifier, and final decision date; required diagnostic, recovery, safeguard, and coverage-verification checks are not extra customer-facing tactics. It also gives a distinct protect/cancel/delegate/defer/bound row with owner, effective date, and limit/reopen condition for each of the four named initiatives, ending with no undispositioned named activity. The bridal expo may be bounded only if the response preserves a genuinely reversible, non-paid option; paying the non-refundable deposit while evidence is pending fails. A florist target passes when it meets the prompt's sample/exposure, minimum six-reorder lift, partial-fill guardrail, and deadline structure, or is demonstrably stricter under every dimension. Priority-target substance, latency, and the earliest checkpoint date are graded only here; checkpoint-content rows are criterion 4, and essential-obligation protection is criterion 3.

3. **Executable, humane calendar and continuity envelope.** The response identifies and protects all six supplied essential-obligation classes; reserves recurring contiguous decision/maker time and recurring direct florist evidence for Mara; bounds meetings, status work, messages, and availability; removes or delegates enough work before adding protected blocks; states the supplied post-push caps, weekly recovery day, any exceptional-push end no later than July 28, and bans compulsory nights/public pressure; and supplies separate continuity rows for irrigation/frost, harvest/packing, florist orders, payroll/tax, worker safety/field incident, final operating decisions, and Saturday cold-room alarms with the supplied primary, distinct verified backup where available, service/protected block, escalation destination, and concrete response window. It must not call Noel the verified cold-room decision backup: that missing authority must be controlled with a named evidence owner, written roster/authorization artifact, due date before July 18, an authorization plus acknowledged-handoff threshold, and a decision effect that installs coverage or changes Saturday operations before exposure. Essential-obligation protection, envelope, and coverage are graded only here; do not also fail criterion 1 for the cold-room defect.

4. **Morale diagnosis, measures, and owned cadence.** Using the evidence IDs without regrading their classification, the response tests overload/late changes against at least one plausible rival and separates the stable florist-value goal premise from the selected tactic; defines distinct company-outcome, florist/customer-continuity, and workload/judgment measure rows with dated baseline, target/limit or a valid cross-reference to the criterion-2 target, evidence source, owner, and review dates; applies a morale threshold combining pulse score with observed late-change or judgment behavior; and gives required recovery/acute-risk and July 26 queue-relief checks, plus every criterion-2-named tactic checkpoint, a checkpoint-content row containing observed result, confounder/rival status, safeguard status, and precommitted branch. Missing or defective evidence classification fails only criterion 1; a missing/defective priority target or checkpoint date fails only criterion 2; criterion 4 grades the existence and non-target fields of measure rows plus the content row for a checkpoint already named under criterion 2.

5. **Precommitted persistence branches.** The response gives separate `continue`, `adapt`, `reopen-goal`, and `stop` rows, each with its own observable threshold, next action, next owner, calendar date, resulting primary route, and explicit handoff/return state; thresholds distinguish tactic miss from repeated premise failure and hard harm/survival boundaries without using effort, morale, competitor prestige, or raw hours as proof of demand; and it states the decision now, preserved option, at least two discriminating tactic/segment misses before reopening the goal, and unresolved consequential unknowns carried forward. Continue/adapt thresholds must use the prompt's denominator/exposure, minimum six-reorder lift, and partial-fill guardrail or thresholds stricter on every dimension; reopen requires the two-test minimum; stop requires an explicit supplied or prospectively observable safety, survival, harm, or dominated-option boundary. Branch content is graded only here, not again as a cadence, target, or coverage defect.

## Novelty and fairness self-audit

- **Unseen/domain novelty:** seasonal cut-flower agriculture is materially different from marine hardware, performing arts, local news, and ordinary software/product examples. Its continuity surfaces, seasonal evidence, physical inventory, field safety, and cold-chain authority create a new operating context without requiring specialist horticultural knowledge.
- **Prompt sufficiency:** all route facts, initiative names, baselines, role identities, verified backups, escalation windows, workload constraints, and the single deliberately unverified coverage value are supplied. No browsing, calculations beyond simple comparison, or hidden domain facts are needed.
- **Controlled unknowns:** only four unknowns are called for, each capable of changing tactic, goal, morale intervention, or continuity exposure. Known values cannot be converted to unknowns.
- **Answerability under cap:** the requested artifact is table/fragment friendly; stable evidence IDs allow a complete 900–1,100-word answer within the 1,200-word hard limit.
- **Threshold flexibility:** the prompt publishes minimum sample/exposure, effect, quality, morale, coverage, and repetition invariants. Criteria accept thresholds that meet them or are stricter on every relevant dimension, making alternatives objectively gradable without privileging one exact target.
- **No double penalties:** evidence classification belongs only to criterion 1; target, latency, and checkpoint date only to criterion 2; obligations, execution, and coverage only to criterion 3; measure-row and checkpoint contents only to criterion 4; branch content only to criterion 5. Cross-references satisfy dependent fields without regrading the owner criterion's defect.
- **Strictness:** each criterion is conjunctive and binary, and 5/5 is required, so a fluent but incomplete productivity plan cannot pass.
