## Novelty and non-overlap rationale

This is an ex-ante transfer design: no existing eval, held-out, score, prompt, or microtest artifact was inspected. The setting is a nine-person marine-hardware company with a scarce tidal deployment window, licensed field-safety coverage, and evidence that cannot honestly arrive for 72 hours or be cleaned until after a 12-day trial. It tests long-latency focus, direct user contact, workload recovery, and decision cadence rather than a common immediate-metric SaaS, launch, fundraising, or generic calendar-cleanup case.

The deliberate adjacent boundary is `building-and-evolving-organizations`. A five-item approval queue could tempt an immediate executive hire or committee, but the prompt gives explicit rights, named owners/backups, eight weeks of fast normal-load decisions, and onset during a bounded overload spike. Focus/recovery remains primary only through a dated relief test. A persisting queue or revealed authority/interface defect must switch organization work to primary.

Other primary routes are concretely excluded: funded payroll and 14 months of runway exclude survival primacy; optional coffees without a live process exclude capital primacy; no buyer contact excludes exit primacy; no acute incident excludes safety, legal, security, or medical routing.
