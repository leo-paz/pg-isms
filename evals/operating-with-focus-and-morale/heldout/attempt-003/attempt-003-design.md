## Novelty rationale

This is a transfer test in a worker-owned multilingual local-news service. Its state-changing outcome is member renewal around civic-decision usefulness; its continuity surfaces are corrections, member alerts, payroll/legal coordination, and confidential-source security. That is materially different from marine hardware, software, fundraising, acquisition, greenhouse hardware, and performing arts. The case tests the portable mechanisms—route precedence, observed/claimed/unknown control, one evidence loop, attention disposition, workload recovery, continuity coverage, honest latency, morale diagnosis, and persistence branching—without depending on any excluded domain's vocabulary or operating model.
