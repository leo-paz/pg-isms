# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: dashboard-request-handoff-delay

Reviewer ID: learning-baseline-001

Do not implement the larger dashboard yet. The observed delay is not lack of visibility; the exceptions are already visible. Learn why approval is centralized and what information or authority is missing at the handoff.

Shadow several exception cycles from detection through resolution. For each one, record who notices it, what decision is required, why the worker cannot make that decision, what the supervisor checks, how long each wait lasts, and what happens when approval is wrong or late. Compare supervisors, shifts, and exception types. Then test the smallest operational change: a rule that lets workers resolve a safe class of exceptions, an approval threshold, or an asynchronous evidence packet that a supervisor can clear quickly.

The next decision should be based on cycle time and error rate, not stated preference. If delegated rules or a streamlined approval step remove most of the forty-minute delay without increasing mistakes, build support for that workflow. A larger dashboard is justified only if observation shows that supervisors genuinely lack decision-critical information and that presenting that information changes approval speed or quality.
