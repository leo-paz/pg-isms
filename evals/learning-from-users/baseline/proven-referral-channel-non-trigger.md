# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: proven-referral-channel-non-trigger

Reviewer ID: learning-baseline-001

The primary route is growth and channel optimization, not open-ended customer discovery. The team has a defined segment, paying users, repeat use, and six months of retention evidence. It should instrument the accountant-referral funnel, identify where referred restaurants fail to activate, test changes to onboarding, and measure whether the same motion works in adjacent restaurant subsegments without degrading retention.

User learning should support those experiments with targeted diagnosis, but it should regain ownership if the current model stops transferring. The handback condition is behavioral: referred accounts in the adjacent segment fail to activate or retain despite competent execution, expansion requires materially different workflows or buyers, or interviews and observation reveal that the original value proposition does not address the new segment's actual job. At that point the unknown is no longer how to scale a proven motion; it is whether the team has the right problem, user, and product for the proposed expansion.
