# Baseline scorecard

Case ID: long-latency-diagnostic-learning

Reviewer ID: learning-baseline-001

## Score

Score: 4/5

1. **1/1 — Respects the outcome latency:** It establishes the prospective follow-up now and explicitly refuses to compress nine-month patient evidence into interviews.
2. **1/1 — Stages evidence to risk:** It uses blinded retrospective samples, a supervised shadow workflow without patient exposure, and a conditional procurement step.
3. **0/1 — Safety is not a separate hypothesis:** Clinical or analytical performance, operations, clinician usefulness, procurement, and outcomes are separated, but safety remains embedded in false-positive controls rather than named and tracked independently.
4. **1/1 — Sets pre-exposure gates:** The plan locks thresholds and pilot rules up front, gives every claim an owner and expected date, and permits advancement only when the claim-specific evidence supports it.
5. **1/1 — Keeps claims proportional:** The evidence ledger prevents workflow findings or commercial interest from standing in for clinical performance or delayed outcomes.
