## Ownership and composition

**User learning owns the workflow now.** Independent dental offices have been selected as a segment, but the actor, workflow, consequence, repeated use, and willingness to pay remain unobserved. The team should not spend $80,000 optimizing paid-search CAC or landing-page conversion: clicks and signups would measure message response before establishing demand.

Learning's claim is that the staff member responsible for claims status repeatedly spends consequential time checking unresolved claims, will grant workflow access, will use a bounded claims-status service in real work, and the office manager or owner will pay for it. Rivals include occasional rather than recurring pain, payer delays the tool cannot change, missing authority or documentation, a billing-service user rather than office staff, and a workaround whose cost is below the proposed price.

Recruit eight independent offices that filed claims in the prior 30 days and whose responsible biller and budget owner will participate; exclude chains, friends, agencies speaking for offices, and paid-search leads without the qualifying workflow. Obtain consent and minimize protected health information. Observe the most recent unresolved-claim sequence, actors, systems, workaround, delay, consequence, and purchasing path. For offices with the repeated job, run a reversible concierge or minimal prototype for four weeks while logging real claims handled, prompts required, repeated use, exceptions, custom labor, errors, and price response.

**Growth's limited role** is to recruit the learning-defined sample and test messages under a small capped recruitment budget. It may record source and qualification rate, but learning owns the inclusion rule, claim, evidence threshold, and interpretation. Growth must not optimize CAC, scale the channel, or call landing-page conversion validation. If observation yields a clear falsifiable product specification, shipping can build the bounded test while learning continues to own the uncertainty.

**Handoff gate.** On the assumed review date September 4, 2026, hand the defined segment to growth only if at least six of eight offices expose the real workflow, at least five use the service for ten or more real claims per week in each of four weeks without founder prompting, and at least three pay or sign a dated paid commitment at the tested price. The decision artifact records each office's workflow evidence, use by week, workarounds, buyer, payment, confounders, harms, and pass/fail. If workflow evidence is strong but repeat use or payment misses, continue one specified learning test; if the job does not repeat or consequences are weak, return the preserved evidence to opportunity choice. Do not move the $80,000 into growth until the gate passes.
