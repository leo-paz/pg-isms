# Learning from Users Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: learning-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — The dashboard request is deferred in favor of discriminating rivals.** The record contrasts analytics visibility/inadequacy, source and integration reliability, recruiter authority, notifications, manager response time, and other process delay, and isolates them across the bounded comparisons.
2. **1/1 — The Learning Record includes the full contract.** It defines a comparable mid-market approval-gated segment, six-team inclusion rule, team-baseline comparison, numeric reproduction/workflow/demand thresholds, August 31 review, and safety, authority, sample-fragmentation, and concierge stop rules.
3. **1/1 — Sandbox work is consented and staged toward costly evidence.** Written access, retention/deletion, redaction, and recording consent govern observation; repeat behavior precedes security/integration review and dated paid-pilot, deployment, or payment evidence, and sandbox access alone cannot validate demand.
4. **1/1 — Growth is recruitment-only during learning.** The learning lead owns inclusion, claim, thresholds, and interpretation; growth recruits six matching teams and is explicitly barred from optimizing ads, landing pages, or inclusion criteria.
5. **1/1 — The full handoff sequence is named.** A passed workflow goes from the bounded test owner to shipping, qualifying repeated use plus paid/deployment commitment goes to growth, and failed or analytics-rival evidence returns to opportunity choice rather than authorizing a silent pivot.

Score 5/5
