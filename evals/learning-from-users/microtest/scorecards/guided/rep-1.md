4. **1/1 — Learning owns the test while growth only recruits.** The founder controls inclusion, claim, threshold, and interpretation; growth sends neutral invitations from that list and is prohibited from channel optimization until validation.
5. **1/1 — All next-owner gates are named.** A passed causal test hands the bounded reversible change to shipping, repeated use plus the commercial threshold hands the segment to growth, and failed access, claim, or analytics evidence returns to opportunity choice.
