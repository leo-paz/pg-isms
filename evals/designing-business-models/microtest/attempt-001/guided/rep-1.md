Primary owner: `designing-business-models`—the immediate uncertainty is capture, pricing, channel economics, and the dispatch boundary, not whether the product can produce an operational effect.

## Decision now

- Do not keep one flat price for all volume. Grandfather the 12 renewed sites at $500 through October 12, 2026, while testing two volume packages with new accounts: **Core**, $500 per month for up to 2,500 routed deliveries, and **Scale**, $1,250 per month for 2,501–5,000. Price deliveries above 5,000 at $0.25 each. If a pharmacy uses the outside dispatch API, charge that separately at $0.60 per dispatched delivery; its known direct cost is $0.40. Collect monthly in advance. Allow at most a 10% discount, only for annual prepayment.
- Reject the wholesaler proposal as written. Access to 600 pharmacies is not 600 buyers, and 25% of revenue plus two-year exclusivity plus a custom integration concentrates distribution risk before conversion and support economics are known. Counter with a 90-day, non-exclusive, 30-introduction co-sell test: 25% of collected first-year revenue on referred accounts, no share on pre-existing accounts, no territorial lock, and a reusable integration capped at $25,000 and three engineering weeks.
- Keep dispatch partnered for the next 90 days. The proposed $600,000 build is 100 times current renewed MRR of $6,000 and takes six months. At $0.40 per delivery, $600,000 equals 1.5 million partner transactions before maintenance, on-call, compliance, delay, or execution risk. Negotiate the existing SLA and price, build a replaceable adapter, and qualify a backup. Reconsider making only at the precommitted volume, control, cash, and capability gates below.

Interpretation is fixed before results:

- A request for a discount is not willingness to pay at list price. Discounted wins are reported separately and count toward list-price acceptance only if the discount is no more than 10% and tied to annual prepayment.
- The six pilot nonrenewals are not automatically price rejection. By July 28, code each one as effect/need, price/budget, deployment or support, courier execution, closure/timing, or unknown, with owner corroboration where available.
