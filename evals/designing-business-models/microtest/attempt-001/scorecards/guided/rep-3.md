# Microtest scorecard

Variant: guided
Rep: 3
Score: 4/5

1. **Pass — ownership and handoffs.** The explicit primary-owner opening names designing business models and is followed by ordered specialist handoffs; product/shipping receives metering and billing, engineering receives the adapter, and growth receives specified cohort execution.
2. **Miss — separate economic records.** The answer has separate small/large rows and an existing-renewed economics row, but the six non-renewers receive only an interpretation rule and interview plan, not their own complete economic record across all required fields.
3. **Pass — bounded commercial test.** The $500 plus $0.80 offer is assigned to owners/sales, uses named small and large cohorts, supplies dated day-45/day-75/day-90 reviews and numeric thresholds, and explicitly defines acceptance, rejection, discount treatment, and nonrenewal interpretations.
4. **Pass — boundary comparison.** The boundary and channel sections cover dependency, exclusivity, switching, internal capability, delay, capital, interfaces, quality/SLA, and logged supplier-versus-startup failure evidence before any integration or exclusive channel.
5. **Pass — branches, gates, and next owners.** Complete price, channel, and boundary branches are predeclared; retention and patient/service effects gate selection; export, direct sales, alternate suppliers, and a later build remain open; every material pass/fail class has a named next owner.
