# Microtest scorecard

Variant: guided
Rep: 1
Score: 4/5

1. **Pass — ownership and handoffs.** It starts with `Primary owner: designing-business-models` and an ordered handoff list; shipping owns billing/package rollout, engineering owns adapter/instrumentation work, and growth executes passing offers/channels.
2. **Miss — separate economic records.** Core and Scale receive separate actor and economics rows, but the 12 renewed and 6 non-renewed pharmacies do not receive separate complete economic records. Nonrenewals are only scheduled for cause coding, so their user/buyer/payer/beneficiary, value, WTP, sales/support, cost, retention, and confounder record is not explicit as a cohort.
3. **Pass — bounded commercial test.** The CEO offers explicit Core and Scale prices to 12 prospects per cohort, with dated August 28 and October 12 reviews, numeric payment/deployment/margin thresholds, and frozen payment, rejection, discount, nonrenewal, churn, and pricing-branch interpretations.
4. **Pass — boundary comparison.** The wholesaler and courier choices are compared across exclusivity, dependency/coordination, switching, capability, delay, capital, interface, quality, and trace-based supplier-versus-architecture failure attribution before expansion or integration.
5. **Pass — branches, gates, and next owners.** Pricing and boundary branches are precommitted; retention and attributable-effects gates are numeric; direct sales, a backup supplier, grandfathering, and later build options remain reversible; the final ledger names the next owner by result.
