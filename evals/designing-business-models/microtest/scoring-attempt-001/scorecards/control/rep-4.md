# Scorecard: control rep 4

- Variant: control

## Criterion 1 — Miss

Evidence: A founder owns the account dataset and the CTO owns dispatch leverage and reversibility.

Exact miss: `designing-business-models` is not named as primary, handoffs are not ordered, and the required shipping/engineering and post-gate growth routing is absent.

## Criterion 2 — Miss

Evidence: It specifies an 18-site account dataset, volume segmentation, renewed/non-renewed evidence, owner and technician feedback, costs, support, renewal, and outcomes.

Exact miss: It does not create four explicit complete economic record views and omits payer, beneficiary, and sales effort from the account record while leaving confounders only partially represented.

## Criterion 3 — Miss

Evidence: It gives an actual provisional price card, named volume cohorts, founder ownership, weekly and day-45/day-75/day-90 reviews, six deployments, two Scale prospects, and a 70% margin guardrail.

Exact miss: Churn is not given a predeclared interpretation in the pricing test, and discounting is constrained but not tied to a complete accept/reject/churn branch.

## Criterion 4 — Pass

Evidence: It compares the wholesaler and courier boundaries through revenue share and integration labor, exclusivity and termination, partner dependence and switching, capability, six-month delay, $600,000 capital, a provider-neutral interface, SLA/quality evidence, and attribution of losses or limitations actually caused by the partner before a build decision.

## Criterion 5 — Miss

Evidence: Pricing, channel, and partner/build branches are stated, and a non-exclusive pilot, backup supplier, and thin interface preserve reversibility.

Exact miss: The price/channel gates do not include a numeric retention threshold plus an accountable-effects threshold, and next owners are not named for every material post-gate result.
