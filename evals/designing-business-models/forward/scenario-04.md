## Remaining business-model check

Finance prepares one amount-and-absorption memo before negotiation. The CEO owns its operating assumptions; the business-model owner verifies only that the capital accelerates the already validated venture shape.

- Map 100% of net new cash to named quarterly operating milestones, including a 15% contingency, rather than to an undifferentiated growth budget.
- The amount should fund 18–24 months of the approved base plan and at least 12 months of the downside plan after expected close; a term sheet does not extend runway until cash is received.
- No more than 25% of the round may remain without a planned deployment inside 24 months. Hiring must have named managers and quarterly onboarding capacity, which the organization workflow verifies.
- Recalculate the plausible venture outcome after the round amount and planned future capital. The capital fits only if the reachable market, validated margins, growth motion, and founder goal can still support venture-return outcomes after expected dilution; investor interest alone is not that evidence.
- Record current customer, channel, security, and cost assumptions with dates. A change in them reopens only the affected field.

Precommit the handoff: **fit** if a proposed amount clears every runway, allocation, absorption, and return check; **resize** if it funds under 18 months, leaves more than 25% unallocated inside 24 months, or exceeds hiring/implementation capacity; **delay or seek a different capital path** if neither amount can reach a named milestone while preserving 12 downside months. If both term sheets fund an amount that fits, business models reports “both fit” and expresses no preference. If one fits, it reports that amount fact to the capital owner without weighing preferences, board rights, option pools, or vesting.

No new offer, pricing test, sales-motion test, or boundary test is warranted: those decisions are validated and closed. The rejected alternative is having `designing-business-models` rank or negotiate the term sheets. The preserved option is to reopen a specific economic field only upon contrary deployed customer or cost evidence. The next and final owner is `raising-and-governing-capital`.
