Primary owner: `raising-and-governing-capital`

Handoffs: 1. `raising-and-governing-capital` leads term-sheet comparison, investor incentives, instrument economics, dilution, control, governance, counsel coordination, and negotiation. 2. `designing-business-models` performs only a bounded capital-amount/absorption and venture-return-shape check. 3. `managing-runway-and-survival` takes priority if current time-to-zero binds, because a signed term sheet is not cash.

The business-model questions are already closed by the facts: venture scale, buyer, annual price, direct sales motion, gross margins, and capital need are validated. Liquidation preferences, pro rata rights, board seats, option-pool treatment, founder vesting resets, lead-investor choice, and negotiation strategy are not pricing or business-model decisions. This workflow should not choose either term sheet or reopen payer, package, or sales design.

The limited business-model check is a short, dated review of:

- whether the amount raised matches the already validated staged product, sales, and hiring milestones rather than merely maximizing available capital;
- whether the company can absorb the planned hiring and spend without outrunning management capacity or evidence latency;
- whether the resulting ownership and required outcome remain compatible with the company's evidenced venture-return shape and the founders' goals; and
- whether current runway, closing risk, cash receipt timing, and a downside plan require a separate survival branch.

The founders should provide the capital owner with the validated milestone budget, monthly cash plan, hiring/absorption schedule, expected evidence dates, and downside case. The capital workflow, with qualified legal and tax advice where appropriate, then compares the two offers and negotiates their economic and control terms. The next owner for every term-sheet result remains `raising-and-governing-capital`; business models returns only if the proposed amount or return requirement contradicts the validated venture shape.
