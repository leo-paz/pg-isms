## Company boundary

Keep regulatory judgment, accountable sign-off, exception handling, customer relationship, permissions, and quality standard inside the company for now. Productize intake, calculations, workflow, evidence trace, and draft generation behind versioned interfaces. Customers retain their data; contracts define permitted use, export, deletion, and audit access. Human sign-off remains mandatory, with monitoring, rollback, and incident ownership.

The service/software interface records whether a defect arose from source data, product logic, regulatory interpretation, implementation, or reviewer error. Standard integrations and export reduce customer switching cost. Commodity hosting or specialist components may be partnered only with data terms, service levels, conformance tests, replaceability, and an alternate supplier; partners do not receive regulatory accountability. Eliminating services now is rejected because the facts show that judgment, implementation, and accountability are part of value, not proven scaffolding.

## Bounded product/repeatability test

From 2026-08-15 through 2027-02-15, the COO offers eight qualified organizations two explicit choices, four per cell where scheduling permits:

- **Hybrid managed reporting:** existing scope with the tool-backed standardized workflow, fixed implementation boundary, and a 10%–15% premium over that client’s comparable prior contract.
- **Software-led reporting:** annual contract at $24,000–$36,000, standardized onboarding, capped expert review of 10 hours per quarter, named usage limits, and separately priced extra judgment/implementation. At least four invitees must not already buy open-ended consulting.

This is a test of whether service can be bounded, not a disguised free pilot. Payment means collected cash on an annual contract; a letter of intent, accelerator interest, usage, or praise does not count. A deployment requires the customer to complete one real report under the assurance/human-review process. Discounts are evidence at the net price. Renewal intent is recorded but not labeled renewal; only a subsequent paid term qualifies. Churn/rejection is classified as price, missing expert accountability, implementation burden, product failure, budget, or procurement.

The COO owns the decision; Product and the accountable regulatory lead may veto release. Total incremental spend is capped at $250,000 from retained earnings, including product hardening, assurance, sales, and support. No equity, 30-person hiring plan, automated regulatory sign-off, misleading claim, or removal of service from existing clients is allowed. Metrics are collected payment, deployment, implementation and expert hours, time saved, error/rework, on-time filing, support, fully loaded margin, concentration, expansion order, and customer/data incidents. Confounders include report complexity, regulatory change, existing relationship, client size, filing calendar, and expert mix.

Thresholds at 2027-02-15:

- **pay / venture option:** At least 3 of 4 standalone-software prospects pay and deploy, at least 2 are new non-consulting clients, median onboarding is at most 20 staff hours, ongoing expert work is at most 10 hours per client per quarter, fully loaded software gross margin is at least 70%, on-time/error outcomes are no worse than managed service, and bottom-up adjacent-market evidence supports the $100 million ARR condition. This preserves a venture-product option; it does not itself authorize equity before paid renewal and a milestone capital plan.
- **revise / durable hybrid:** If the hybrid cell gets at least 3 of 4 paid deployments and raises measured operating margin to at least 28% while standalone software misses any pay threshold, select the durable hybrid. Revise only the software/service boundary implicated by rejection; keep judgment and accountability in the offer.
- **preserve-free:** If clients will pay for the managed outcome but not a separate tool, retain the tool as an internal capability included in service economics. It is not a standalone free product and does not justify a product sales channel.
- **stop:** Stop standalone commercialization on any missed-filing, material reporting, permission, security, or deceptive-claim incident, or if fewer than 2 of 4 standalone prospects pay. Roll back the affected workflow and retain human-led delivery. Stop the whole experiment if the spend cap threatens the profitable base.

Selected branch now: durable, profitable service-plus-software funded by retained earnings and customer payments. It fits the verified economics and founders’ stated preference; it is not a claim that consultancies are inherently superior. Reject immediate venture financing, removal of services, and 30 hires because current reach, independent product WTP, repeatability, and venture-return evidence do not support them. Preserve the standalone-product option through the dated test. Switch only after the pay/venture threshold plus a later paid-renewal cohort; then hand amount/absorption/return fit to business models once more and financing/control to `raising-and-governing-capital`. Delay costs are capped test spend and six months; premature scaling risks the profitable base, customer outcomes, and founder control.
