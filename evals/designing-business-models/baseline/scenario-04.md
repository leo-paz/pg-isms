# Raw evaluation output
Case ID: scenario-04
Reviewer ID: business-model-baseline-001

The primary workflow is a financing and term-sheet decision, supported by experienced venture counsel and financial modeling. It is not principally a business-model design problem: the buyer, pricing, sales motion, gross margins, and capital requirement have already been validated. Choosing between liquidation preferences, pro rata rights, board control, option-pool treatment, and vesting resets requires cap-table scenario analysis, legal interpretation, reference checks on the investors, and a negotiation strategy led by the founders with their lawyer. The designing-business-models workflow should not purport to choose the term sheet or lead legal negotiation.

The limited business-model check is whether the amount, timing, and constraints of each offer fit the already validated plan. Confirm the runway and milestones the round must fund, expected follow-on financing, hiring and option-pool needs, dilution under realistic exit and down-round scenarios, and whether governance or investor rights could prevent the company from executing its chosen model. This can provide inputs and surface tradeoffs, but the final term-sheet recommendation belongs in the financing, governance, and legal workflow.
