# Evaluation scorecard

Case ID: paying-users-begin-now

Reviewer ID: preparing-baseline-001

## Score

Score: 4/5

1. **1/1 — Begins from trust and urgent demand:** It explicitly says those two facts make abstract preparation unnecessary and recommends starting now.
2. **1/1 — Bounded reversible trial:** It proposes narrow paid pilots lasting 4–8 weeks with defined scope, price, success measures, and an end date for the three prospects.
3. **0/1 — Personal runway is not fully operationalized:** Roles, commitment, equity, vesting, expenses, and quitting timing are explicit, but the answer only says runway must “justify” transition; it never has each founder quantify a personal runway requirement.
4. **1/1 — Evidence and dated decision:** It defines concrete continuation and warning signals and requires a decision date and thresholds before the trial.
5. **1/1 — Avoids delaying detours:** It rejects abstract preparation, premature fundraising-style work, branding, and other work that would displace selling and delivery.
