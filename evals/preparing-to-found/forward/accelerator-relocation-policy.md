# Raw evaluation output
Case ID: accelerator-relocation-policy
Reviewer ID: preparing-forward-v3-001

Do not impose a blanket relocation mandate. The accelerator has two different decisions: how to strengthen the regional ecosystem, and whether relocation improves a particular active team’s odds. Design the ecosystem intervention first with `building-startup-ecosystems`; then apply a team-level location test separately to every company.

Adopt a **mechanism-based, opt-in residency policy**:

1. For the region, identify the supply you are trying to build—customers, specialized talent, investors, peers, suppliers, regulators, or founder culture—and fund programming that creates that access. Publish regional metrics such as local customer introductions, hires, follow-on capital relationships, and durable founder participation. Do not count forced physical presence as impact by itself.
2. For each team, record its industry, stage, target customers, hiring and capital needs, and the precise mechanism the region can improve. Require concrete evidence such as named customers, a relevant cluster, scarce laboratory or supplier access, or investors suited to that business.
3. Record the full burden for that team: family and caregiving, immigration, housing and payroll cost, existing customers or committed capital elsewhere, and disruption to shipping or selling. A biotech company needing local lab infrastructure and a remote SaaS team seeking generic “energy” should not receive the same answer.
4. Offer a reversible 4–8 week residency with travel, housing, workspace, immigration assistance, and customer or talent introductions. Permit partial-team participation and remote completion. Teams may decline without losing funding, status, or program access.
5. Agree in advance on a team-specific rule: relocate longer only if the trial produces a material access advantage that exceeds the full burden and cannot be obtained through deliberate substitutes such as periodic visits, a local hire, advisers, or a satellite presence.

Review each company at the end of its trial and review the policy after the cohort. The accelerator owns the regional supply metrics; each founding team owns its company decision. Continue the residency benefit only where outcomes—not attendance—show that the region created durable access. The immediate action is to replace the mandate with a written opt-in trial and team decision record before offers go out.
