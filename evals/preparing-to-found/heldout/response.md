# Held-out transfer response

Case ID: procurement-cofounder-leave-transfer
Generator ID: preparing-heldout-v4-001

Recommendation: do not resign now. Use the six-week unpaid leave for a bounded cofounder trial with these two colleagues; do not divert into joining another startup unless this trial shows that founding is the wrong path and operating experience is the binding gap.

The evidence supports testing, not committing. Fourteen months of savings makes a trial feasible, and the hospital letter shows a possible problem, but there is no product, payment, or proof that the friends can execute together under pressure. The binding unknown is whether this team can turn procurement interest into urgent, paid demand while doing difficult work together.

Before the leave starts, the three should choose one narrow procurement workflow, assign product, customer, and technical owners, and agree that the six weeks create no automatic equity or permanent-role commitment. During the trial, they should interview the hospital's actual buyer and users, ship the smallest usable workflow, ask for a paid pilot, and keep a weekly record of decisions, conflict, follow-through, and customer evidence.

Decision gate: on the final day of the leave, resign and join as a cofounder only if a named hospital buyer has committed to a paid, time-bound pilot; the team has delivered and supported a usable product together; each founder has met explicit responsibilities under deadline; and the three have aligned on roles, commitment, ownership expectations, and personal runway. If any condition fails, return to the job and preserve the option; diagnose whether the missing element is demand, team fit, or startup-operating experience before choosing a new experiment.
