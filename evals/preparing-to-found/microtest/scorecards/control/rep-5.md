# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It first defines measurable regional outcomes and a program policy, then permits team-appropriate ways to satisfy it.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every company has a team-appropriate plan, with alternatives approved according to that team's operating model and constraints.
3. **0/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** It offers generic outcomes but does not require per-team industry, access mechanism, destination evidence, or a mechanism-specific test.
4. **0/1 — Full relocation burden.** Labs, caregiving, visas, and distributed work are considered, but cost, committed local ties or capital, and relocation distraction are not all operationalized.
5. **0/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** Quarterly targets and end-of-cohort reporting do not establish a reversible location trial or a dated or evidence-triggered location revisit.

Score 2/5
