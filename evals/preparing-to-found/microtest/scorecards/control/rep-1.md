# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It rejects the blanket mandate, makes measurable regional impact the program objective, and defines a cohort-level compact before describing team paths.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every team must choose a concrete path, and relocation is retained only for a team for which it is the strongest route rather than imposed cohort-wide.
3. **0/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** The generic menu does not record every team's industry, identify its access mechanism, or test evidence that this destination improves that mechanism.
4. **0/1 — Full relocation burden.** It recognizes unequal burdens and mentions parents and visa holders, but it does not operationalize family, immigration, cost, committed ties or capital, and relocation distraction as a complete team-level check.
5. **0/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** Monthly impact reporting is not a bounded location test, and the response gives no observable condition or date for revisiting each team's location decision.

Score 2/5
