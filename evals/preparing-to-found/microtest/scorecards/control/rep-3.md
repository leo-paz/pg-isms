# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 3

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It defines a regional local-impact compact and scorecard before offering relocation or other mechanisms to individual teams.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every team chooses and documents its own commitments, while relocation is an opt-in path rather than a cohort-wide rule.
3. **0/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** The menu does not require each team's industry or a test showing that this destination improves that team's needed access mechanism.
4. **0/1 — Full relocation burden.** Support for family and immigration constraints is useful but does not cover cost, committed local ties or capital, and relocation distraction as a full decision check.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** It makes the policy a one-cohort pilot, checks results at months three, six, and nine, and uses the evidence to decide whether a narrower future rule is warranted.

Score 3/5
