Variant: control

Rep: 2

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** The accelerator approves how much presence is necessary for each company under team-specific residency plans, with different biotech and SaaS outcomes.
3. **0/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** Industry examples are illustrative only; the response does not require each team to record its industry and test evidence that this destination improves a concrete access mechanism.
4. **0/1 — Full relocation burden.** It names parents, visa holders, and uneven costs but omits a complete per-team check for committed local ties or capital and operating distraction.
5. **0/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** Exceptions and pre-admission review do not create a reversible location experiment with an observable revisit date or condition.
