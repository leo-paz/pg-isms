# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 4

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It starts with a program-level local-impact compact and city scorecard, then lets teams use different mechanisms.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every company supplies its own plan, and presence is mandatory only for activities that genuinely require it, producing conditional team-level outcomes.
3. **0/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** Biotech and SaaS examples do not amount to requiring every team to record industry and test this destination's advantage for a named access mechanism.
4. **0/1 — Full relocation burden.** Caregiving, visa constraints, and relocation grants do not cover committed local ties or capital and operational distraction in a complete team decision record.
5. **0/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** Monthly impact reporting measures the compact but does not define a reversible location test or an observable gate for revisiting each location decision.

Score 2/5
