# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 4

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It first designs a measurable local-impact compact at the accelerator level, then evaluates the lowest-cost mechanism for each company.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every team gets an individual record and moves only if its own test proves sustained presence uniquely valuable.
3. **1/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** It requires industry, a named access mechanism, destination evidence, and concrete reversible access tests.
4. **1/1 — Full relocation burden.** It explicitly checks caregiving, immigration, cost, existing-customer ties, and operational distraction for every team.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** It uses a four-to-six-week test and a dated move/hybrid/stay gate based on observed benefit versus total cost.

Score 5/5
