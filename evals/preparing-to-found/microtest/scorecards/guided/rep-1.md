# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It explicitly assigns regional impact to program design and rejects forced presence as the universal mechanism before assessing teams.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Each accepted company receives a mechanism-matched trial and a longer stay only when that team's measured benefit exceeds its full cost.
3. **1/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** Each team documents industry, a concrete access mechanism, evidence that the destination improves it, and a reversible trial matched to it.
4. **1/1 — Full relocation burden.** It requires family, immigration, cost, existing-customer ties, and disruption to be recorded for every team rather than assuming free mobility.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** It uses a six-to-eight-week reversible trial, a dated continue/change/stop review, and an eight-week policy review.

Score 5/5
