# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 3

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It preserves the funders' local-impact objective through program commitments before applying a conditional location rule to companies.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** It requires a separate company record and allows different outcomes for biotech, SaaS, parents, and visa holders.
3. **1/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** Every company records industry, mechanism, evidence the city improves it, substitutes, and a reversible residency or access test.
4. **1/1 — Full relocation burden.** It covers family, immigration, full cost, existing customers or capital, and operating disruption instead of assuming mobility.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** A four-to-six-week reversible residency precedes longer moves, with a dated review and an evidence-based condition for approval.
