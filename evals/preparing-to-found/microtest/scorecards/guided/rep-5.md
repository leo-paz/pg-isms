# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It turns the city's objective into a measurable program compact before applying team-specific location gates.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Each team receives its own decision gate, and relocation depends on that team's evidence rather than cohort membership.
3. **1/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** The gate records industry, a specific access mechanism, evidence that the destination improves it, and a concrete substitute test.
4. **1/1 — Full relocation burden.** It records family and caregiving, immigration, cost, existing customers or capital, and operating disruption.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** It tests substitutes in four to six weeks and requires an evidence target and review date before continue/change/stop decisions.

Score 5/5
