# Preparing to Found Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 2

Scorer ID: preparing-micro-scorer-002

1. **1/1 — Program objective before team location.** It defines a city-facing outcome scorecard and a conditional program policy before deciding whether any individual team relocates.
2. **1/1 — Separate team decisions.** Every team is evaluated separately and moves only when its own test shows an important, otherwise unavailable advantage.
3. **1/1 — Mechanism and destination advantage.** It requires industry, mechanism, destination evidence, and a concrete team-specific test such as lab residency, scheduled visits, or a local pilot.
4. **1/1 — Full relocation burden.** The record includes family care, immigration, cost, existing facility or customer ties, and disruption for every team.
5. **1/1 — Reversible test and revisit rule.** It requires the least costly reversible test plus an evidence threshold and review date before a longer commitment.

Score 5/5
