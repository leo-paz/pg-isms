## Response

Do not impose a blanket nine-month relocation rule. By tomorrow, recommend a **local-impact contract with team-specific residency plans**: every company must propose measurable benefits to the region—such as local hiring, supplier spend, partnerships, public events, or use of regional facilities—but the accelerator approves how much physical presence is actually necessary for that company.

This is defensible because a uniform mandate confuses presence with impact and imposes very different costs on different teams. A biotech company may gain substantially from sustained access to local labs, while a remote SaaS company may create equal local value through hiring and partnerships without moving its entire team. Parents and visa holders also face constraints unrelated to commitment or quality, so a blanket rule would likely exclude strong companies for reasons that do not advance the funders' objective.

Set a minimum common obligation—for example, key program weeks in person plus a quantified regional-impact plan—and allow documented exceptions or alternative commitments. Review each plan before admission and report aggregate outcomes to the city. That gives funders evidence of local benefit while preserving the accelerator's ability to recruit the strongest cohort.
