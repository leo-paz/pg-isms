Recommend against a blanket nine-month relocation rule. Instead, adopt a one-cohort local-impact compact: every company must submit a measurable plan for benefiting the region, but the mechanism can match the team. Biotech firms might use local labs and suppliers; remote SaaS teams might hire locally, run customer pilots, or hold regular in-person work sessions; teams facing caregiving or visa constraints can participate without moving.

By tomorrow, present funders with a scorecard that tracks local spending, jobs, partnerships, customer pilots, and founder-days in the city. Require each team to choose two or three relevant metrics, establish a baseline, and report monthly. Offer relocation grants and workspace as incentives for teams that can benefit from moving, while reserving mandatory presence only for activities that genuinely require it. This gives the city auditable local impact without excluding strong companies or imposing a rule whose burden and value vary dramatically by business and founder circumstance.
