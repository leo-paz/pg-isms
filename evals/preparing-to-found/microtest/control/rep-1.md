# Recommendation: Require local impact, not blanket relocation

Variant: no-guidance

Rep: 1

Agent task: /root/prep_micro_control_1

Do not require every company to relocate for nine months. Instead, make admission conditional on a nine-month **local-impact compact**: each team must choose and commit to a concrete, measurable way of benefiting the city, while the accelerator reports results against cohort-wide targets.

Give teams several qualifying paths, such as maintaining a local operating presence, hiring local workers or interns, purchasing from local vendors, running pilots with local institutions, serving local customers, or delivering recurring founder hours and events in the city. Require every plan to name a baseline, nine-month target, evidence source, and accountable owner; review progress monthly and publish an aggregate funder report. Set minimums high enough that a team cannot satisfy the compact with a token visit or one event.

This is more defensible than a universal relocation rule because it measures the outcome the funders actually want while avoiding arbitrary burdens that differ sharply across biotech labs, distributed software teams, parents, and visa holders. It also protects the accelerator from selecting for ease of movement instead of company quality. Some teams may still choose relocation when it is the strongest route to impact, but relocation becomes a means rather than a proxy.

By tomorrow, approve this policy direction and authorize staff to return within one week with the qualifying paths, minimum thresholds, exception rules, and reporting template. Tell funders plainly: the accelerator will guarantee auditable local contribution from every company and measurable cohort-level impact, not physical presence that may produce little durable benefit.
