Recommend rejecting a blanket nine-month relocation requirement and replacing it with a one-cohort **local-impact compact**: every company must choose and document a credible way to benefit the region, while relocation is offered as an opt-in path with support rather than imposed as the only path.

By tomorrow, present funders with a simple menu and scorecard. Teams could satisfy the compact by relocating key decision-makers, hiring local staff or interns, using local suppliers and labs, holding customer pilots locally, mentoring regional founders, or maintaining a recurring in-person presence. Require each team to set two measurable commitments before admission and report them at months three, six, and nine. Offer housing, immigration counsel, lab partnerships, and family support to teams that do relocate.

This is defensible because it aligns the policy with the funders’ actual objective—local economic and ecosystem impact—while avoiding arbitrary burdens that may exclude strong biotech, distributed, caregiving, or immigration-constrained teams. Make it a nine-month pilot, publish aggregate results, and let the evidence determine whether any narrower relocation rule is warranted for a future cohort.
