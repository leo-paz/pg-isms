# Building Startup Ecosystems Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 3

Scorer ID: ecosystems-micro-scorer-006

1. **0/1 — Diagnosis is not the first job.** It rejects generic programming, but directly prescribes commercialization staff, grants, and a pre-seed fund after assuming the IP process is the bottleneck.
2. **0/1 — The three observed conditions are not rival hypotheses.** Approval time, capital, and retention are tracked, but the response does not specify measurements that distinguish the three observed conditions as competing causes.
3. **0/1 — Targeted allocation lacks the feasibility gate.** Selective proof-of-concept grants, co-investment, and founder recruitment proceed without the controllability, selection or reach, density, and local-precondition gate.
4. **0/1 — Experiment contract is incomplete.** It includes a target population, budget, mechanism measures, and two-year outcomes, but lacks a named owner, comparison, and separate dated stop, change, and expand rules.
5. **0/1 — Mobility and outcome ownership are not separated.** Outside investors are included, but founders retained locally remains an undifferentiated outcome and there is no explicit mobility protection or institutional-versus-regional split.
