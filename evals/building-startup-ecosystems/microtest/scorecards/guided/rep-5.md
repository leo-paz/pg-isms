# Building Startup Ecosystems Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: ecosystems-micro-scorer-006

1. **0/1 — Diagnosis is not the first job.** It rejects all three defaults, but immediately funds and launches the IP and investor mechanisms. Quarterly remeasurement follows commitment rather than first identifying the limiting reagent.
2. **0/1 — The three observed conditions are not rival hypotheses.** It marks investor access uncertain and measures departures, but never makes IP delay, ambitious-founder outflow, and investor scarcity three rival causes with discriminating measurements.
3. **1/1 — Scoped feasibility gate.** It explicitly classifies the university-wide process and link work as non-cohort work, explains the inapplicable gates, and does not create an eligibility-limited allocation.
4. **1/1 — Complete experiment contract.** The vice president for research owns a two-year experiment over university applications, with departmental and historical comparisons, mechanism measures, multi-year outcomes, and separate stop, change, expand, and remeasure rules. Selection is inapplicable.
5. **1/1 — Institutional versus regional outcomes and mobility.** It separates the controlled IP mechanism from uncertain capital and company outcomes, rejects local-stay rules, and treats departures, returners, outside investors, and cross-region ties as assets.

Score 3/5
