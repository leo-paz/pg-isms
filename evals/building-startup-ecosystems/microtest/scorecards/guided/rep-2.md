# Building Startup Ecosystems Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 2

Scorer ID: ecosystems-micro-scorer-006

2. **0/1 — Founder outflow is replaced by a different hypothesis.** It calls IP delay, investor access, and insufficient founder intent rivals. Interviews and departure tracking do not turn the observed outflow of ambitious founders into a distinct, discriminable competing cause.
3. **1/1 — Scoped feasibility gate.** It classifies the work as institution-wide friction removal and outside links, explains why cohort gates are inapplicable, and limits claims to what is controllable.
4. **1/1 — Complete experiment contract.** A named owner, university spinout population, staggered departmental comparison, mechanism evidence, multi-year outcomes, and separate stop, change, expand, and remeasure rules are all present. Selection is inapplicable.
5. **1/1 — Institutional versus regional outcomes and mobility.** It distinguishes controlled IP changes from partially controlled investor access and keeps tracking founders who leave, their outside relationships, and possible returns.
