# Building Startup Ecosystems Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 4

Scorer ID: ecosystems-micro-scorer-006

1. **1/1 — Defaults rejected; diagnosis comes first.** It declines all three defaults and puts a dated baseline, departure-reason review, and comparisons in the first 90 days.
2. **0/1 — The causal story omits founder outflow as a rival.** The working hypotheses are IP delay and weak investor links. Founder drop-off and departure are measured as outcomes, not tested as a third competing causal constraint.
3. **1/1 — Scoped feasibility gate.** It explicitly treats the work as general institutional friction and outside links, explains why cohort and density gates are inapplicable, and avoids a targeted subsidy or cohort.
4. **1/1 — Complete experiment contract.** The vice president for research owns a two-year university-wide pilot with staged departments and external comparisons, mechanism and multi-year measures, and separate stop, change, expand, and remeasure rules. Selection is inapplicable.
5. **1/1 — Institutional versus regional outcomes and mobility.** It states that the university controls its process rather than regional results, forbids local-residency rules, and tracks departures, returns, outside capital, and relationships as distinct flows.

Score 4/5
