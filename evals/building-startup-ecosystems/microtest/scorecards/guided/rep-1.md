# Building Startup Ecosystems Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: ecosystems-micro-scorer-006

1. **1/1 — Defaults rejected; diagnosis comes first.** It rejects all three artifacts and makes a three-month review of recent disclosures and attempted spinouts the first operating phase.
2. **0/1 — Founder outflow is not a rival hypothesis.** The stated rivals are IP delay, weak investability, and weak investor access. Founder flows are tracked later, but ambitious-founder outflow is not given a discriminating test as a competing cause.
3. **1/1 — Scoped feasibility gate.** It explicitly chooses university-wide friction removal and outside-link work, explains why cohort and density gates are inapplicable, and gives founders no money or privileged eligibility.
4. **1/1 — Complete experiment contract.** The vice president for research owns a two-year pilot covering recent disclosures and staged departments, with a departmental comparison, mechanism measures, multi-year outcomes, and separate stop, change, expand, and remeasure rules. Selection is inapplicable.
5. **1/1 — Institutional versus regional outcomes and mobility.** It distinguishes the controlled IP process from formation and network outcomes, rejects residency requirements, and tracks founder, company, job, capital, and relationship flows separately.

Score 4/5
