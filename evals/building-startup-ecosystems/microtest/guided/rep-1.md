Make one mechanism-first bet: a two-year **University Spinout Clearance and Capital Bridge** pilot, not the compulsory course, contest, or incubator.

**Gate check.** Treat this as university-wide friction removal plus outside-link work, not a selected cohort, hub, or founder subsidy. Cohort selection and community-density gates are therefore inapplicable; no founder receives money or privileged eligibility. The university controls IP processing, and it can credibly operate introductions to outside investors. In months 1–3, the named owner should still test the rival hypotheses—IP delay, weak investability, and weak investor access—using every recent invention disclosure and attempted spinout.

Put the VP for Research in charge, with a tech-transfer lead accountable for delivery. Use the $4 million roughly as follows: $2.4 million for additional licensing capacity, standardized founder-friendly terms, delegated approvals, and a published 60-day decision target; $1.1 million for recurring, curated diligence sessions and introductions with relevant outside seed investors; and $0.5 million for measurement and a reserve released only after the month-9 review. Roll out the clearance process first in matched departments, retaining the others briefly as a comparison, then expand only on evidence.

Track disclosure-to-decision time, abandoned cases and reasons, spinout formation, outside-investor diligence and term sheets, follow-on capital, and 12- and 24-month survival. Do not count course enrollment, pitches, events, press, or occupied desks as success. Record founder, company, job, capital, and relationship flows separately; impose no local residency requirement.

Use separate dated rules:

- **Month 9—stop:** stop the investor bridge if qualified investors do not enter diligence after repeated curated matches.
- **Month 9—change:** if approvals remain slow, change authority or terms; if approvals improve but formation does not, redirect the reserve to the newly evidenced bottleneck.
- **Month 15—expand:** extend the faster process university-wide only if the phased comparison shows materially faster decisions without worse licensing quality and more credible formation progress.
- **Month 24—remeasure:** compare formation, financing, survival, and flows with the pre-pilot baseline and comparison departments before renewing anything.
