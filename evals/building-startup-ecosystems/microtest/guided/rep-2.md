Replace the three proposed artifacts with one two-year **Spinout Flow Experiment**.

**Gate check:** This is institution-wide friction removal and outside-link work, not a targeted hub, cohort, or eligibility-limited fund. Cohort selection and community-density gates are therefore inapplicable. The university controls its IP process; access to investors is only partly controllable and should be tested rather than promised.

Name the vice president for research as owner and use the first 90 days to establish a dated baseline across the last three years of disclosures: approval time by stage, abandonment reasons, founder departures, investor introductions and diligence, company formation, and follow-on funding. Interview both founders who stayed and those who left. Treat at least three rival hypotheses separately—IP delay, weak early-investor access, and insufficient founder intent—and use funnel loss and matched interviews to identify which constraint binds.

Allocate the $4 million as follows: $250,000 for diagnosis and independent measurement; up to $1.5 million to redesign the general IP workflow with standard terms, parallel legal and commercial review, a named case owner, and a 90-day service target; up to $1 million for an outside-investor diligence channel in which relevant specialists review real spinout opportunities rather than judge a pitch contest; and hold $1.25 million behind dated evidence gates. Stagger the new workflow across comparable departments so untreated departments provide a temporary comparison.

Measure median approval time, disclosure-to-spinout conversion, abandonment, credible investor diligence and term sheets, formation, survival, and follow-on capital. Continue tracking founders who leave, including outside relationships and later returns, instead of counting all departure as failure.

At months 6, 12, 18, and 24, apply separate rules: stop any workstream that cannot affect its claimed constraint or creates serious gaming or inequity; change the mechanism if approval time falls without formation or investor diligence; expand the workflow only if the staggered comparison improves conversion without worse terms or harms; and remeasure company survival and network effects annually for at least three years after launch. Do not fund the incubator, compulsory course, or pitch contest unless later evidence identifies a distinct mechanism they can test.
