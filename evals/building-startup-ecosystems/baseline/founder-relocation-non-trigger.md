Case ID: founder-relocation-non-trigger
Reviewer ID: ecosystems-baseline-001

This is a company-level location decision, not a request to design a regional startup ecosystem. Route it to the startup's operating and talent-location workflow.

The next artifact should be a short, time-bounded location decision memo. It should name the two or three outcomes that matter most over the next 18 months, such as hiring three controls engineers, reaching target robotics customers, runway, lab access, and retaining the existing team. For Cleveland, Boston, and a split-team option, record evidence on candidate density and acceptance rates, compensation and relocation costs, relevant customers and partners, founder travel, facilities, taxes only where material, and the personal constraints of all six employees.

Before a move, run reversible tests: recruit for the hardest role in both markets, schedule a concentrated customer trip, price facilities, and test whether a Boston satellite or repeated working sessions create the needed access. The memo should end with a weighted comparison, the evidence still missing, a recommendation, a budget and owner, and a review date. That keeps a single firm's decision from being inflated into a claim about what an entire city should build.
