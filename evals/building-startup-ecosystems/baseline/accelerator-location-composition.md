# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: accelerator-location-composition
Reviewer ID: ecosystems-baseline-001

Do not impose a blanket nine-month relocation rule. Make local presence an outcome-oriented, company-specific condition: require each team to propose the smallest local engagement that creates a credible public benefit, and fund only commitments that match the company's work and the founders' constraints.

The policy can define a common floor, such as cohort sessions, local customer or university work, transparent reporting, and a minimum number of in-person working weeks. Beyond that, use tailored agreements. A biotech company might need sustained lab access and a local scientific hire; a remote SaaS company might concentrate customer pilots and cohort weeks locally; parents could use shorter scheduled residencies with family support; and visa holders should receive immigration review before any location condition. Publish exceptions and use the same decision factors for every team.

The ordered work should be:

1. Diagnose the city's public objective and the actual regional bottleneck, then define measurable local outcomes such as hires, lab partnerships, customer contracts, or durable operations.
2. Evaluate each company's stage, industry, required facilities, customers, talent, runway, founder obligations, and immigration constraints.
3. Negotiate a proportionate presence plan for each company, with resources, milestones, safeguards, and an exit clause.
4. Measure both company progress and local benefit against a comparable prior cohort, then revise the accelerator policy.

This preserves a defensible link between public money and local benefit without selecting only founders who can absorb an arbitrary move or forcing companies into locations that damage their prospects.
