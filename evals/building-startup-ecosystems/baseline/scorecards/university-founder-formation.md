# Baseline scorecard

Case ID: university-founder-formation
Reviewer ID: ecosystems-baseline-001
Score: 2/5

1. FAIL — Discovery covers labs, builders, operators, and institutional barriers, but does not explicitly test ambitious-peer conditions and early-investor access as candidate bottlenecks.
2. PASS — It centers voluntary project ownership, demanding milestones, user contact, prototypes, and persistent teams rather than appearances.
3. PASS — Entry and advancement are opt-in and based on demonstrated work, while pitch polish is expressly rejected.
4. FAIL — It names cohorts, indicators, outcome measures, and closing rules, but does not assign an accountable program owner.
5. FAIL — It lists many university-controlled mechanisms and longer-run outcomes without explicitly separating those two accountability horizons.
