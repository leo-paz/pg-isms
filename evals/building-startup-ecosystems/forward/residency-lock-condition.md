# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: residency-lock-condition

Reviewer ID: ecosystems-forward-9c90-001

Do not adopt the ten-year residency requirement or local-only hiring rule. They confuse founder residence, company location, jobs, capital, and relationships; isolate teams from needed markets and talent; and can select for applicants with fewer alternatives rather than stronger companies.

First establish a dated leakage baseline. Identify which recipients moved, when, why, what operations and jobs remained, where capital and customers came from, whether alumni or investors kept local ties, and how comparable nonrecipients behaved. Test rival explanations such as missing specialist capital, customers, talent, follow-on funding, or avoidable state friction. Departure alone is not proof that the grant failed.

If the evidence supports a controllable attraction constraint, run a voluntary two-year offer rather than a lock. Gate check before allocation: the state must identify the controllable mechanism, use credible specialist selection or reach, provide repeated community interaction, and show local preconditions such as relevant investors, operators, customers, or facilities. A weak gate means no targeted allocation; the state may instead test general-friction removal or outside links.

For a passing test, name an owner, cap the budget and cohort, state the uncertainty, and compare eligible recipients offered the package with a prior or matched cohort. Offer useful reasons to engage locally—specialist introductions, customer access, facilities, and optional relocation support—while allowing outside hiring and movement. Track formation, survival, growth, local and nonlocal jobs, follow-on capital, founder and company flows, and durable relationships, plus gaming and selection effects.

Stop if specialist demand or the proposed interaction mechanism does not appear. Change the offer if participants value a different constraint. Expand only after stronger company outcomes and durable local ties improve without trapping founders or excluding necessary talent. Remeasure flows after each cohort and again after two years. Alumni, returners, outside investors, and cross-region ties count as ecosystem assets, not leakage to suppress.
