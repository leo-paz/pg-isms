# Evaluation scorecard

Case ID: university-founder-formation
Reviewer ID: ecosystems-forward-9c90-001

- Criterion 1 — 1/1: Before selecting a program, it measures attempted and completed spinouts, demonstrated founder work, IP approval delay, cofounder formation, investor expertise and speed, outside customer links, departures, survival, and growth, then tests rival binding-constraint hypotheses.
- Criterion 2 — 1/1: It rejects incubator gloss, compulsory classes, and pitch competitions as core mechanisms and instead uses demonstrated project work, real teams, founder autonomy, peer interaction, outside users, and company-formation outcomes.
- Criterion 3 — 1/1: Founder participation is explicitly opt-in and based on demonstrated work, while any later cohort uses specialist selection rather than compulsory enrollment or pitch polish.
- Criterion 4 — 1/1: It names a vice-provost-level owner, fixed budgets and durations, a selected cohort, a pre-period or comparison, monthly mechanism indicators, formation and survival outcomes, and month-specific stop/change/expand rules.
- Criterion 5 — 1/1: It separates university-controlled IP-cycle or outside-link mechanisms and monthly measures from formation, survival, growth, and regional flow outcomes remeasured at months 12 and 24.

Score: 5/5
