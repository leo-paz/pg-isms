# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: university-founder-formation

Reviewer ID: ecosystems-forward-9c90-001

Do not fund the glossy incubator, compulsory classes, or pitch competitions as the program’s core. They measure visible activity without showing that they remove the constraint on durable founder formation.

Gate check: targeted cohort funding is deferred. The university has not yet shown a controllable constraint, credible selection or reach, a community-interaction mechanism, or the necessary local investor and institutional preconditions. General-friction diagnosis can proceed because it does not depend on cohort gates.

During months 1–6, appoint a vice-provost-level owner and publish a dated baseline: attempted and completed spinouts, time and attrition through IP approval, students and faculty doing demonstrated project work, founder and company departures, early-investor expertise and response time, alumni and customer links, formation, two-year survival, and growth. Interview founders and abandoned spinouts, then rank rival hypotheses such as licensing delay, weak cofounder formation, lack of specialist capital, or poor customer access. Require a discriminating measure before choosing one.

During months 7–12, spend a fixed diagnostic-and-friction budget on the best-supported, broadly available mechanism: for example, a time-boxed IP decision service if approval delay is causal, or structured access to outside domain investors and customers if links are causal. Use a pre-period or comparable department, a named owner, and monthly mechanism measures. Participation in any founder work is opt-in and based on demonstrated work rather than compulsory enrollment or polished pitches.

At month 12, consider a year-two formation cohort only if all four gates pass. Use specialist selection, small teams working on real projects, frequent peer interaction, founder autonomy, alumni and outside links, and a fixed budget and duration. The uncertainty is whether those mechanisms create companies that would not otherwise form. Compare with eligible nonparticipants where practical; track selection and gaming, formation, survival, growth, capital, talent and company flows, and who bears costs.

Stop the year-one test if its mechanism does not move by month 9. Change it if implementation, rather than the hypothesis, fails. Expand a cohort only after the interaction mechanism and leading formation measures improve without harmful selection. Remeasure the full baseline at months 12 and 24; do not infer success from attendance, demo days, press, or one cohort.
