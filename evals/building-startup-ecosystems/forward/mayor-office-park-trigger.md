# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: mayor-office-park-trigger

Reviewer ID: ecosystems-forward-9c90-001

The first job is to establish the regional flow baseline and identify the limiting reagent, not to fill the office park. An empty building is an asset, not evidence that offices are the missing mechanism.

Gate check: the first-year diagnostic is not a targeted hub allocation, so cohort gates are inapplicable. No hub spending should begin until the city can show a controllable constraint, credible selection or reach, enough founder–investor–operator interaction to create a community, and the relevant local preconditions.

For months 1–3, name one economic-development owner and define the geography, five-year objective, budget envelope, and dated baseline. Measure founder and company formation, survival, moves in and out, operator and specialist density, university spinouts and approval delay, early-capital expertise and decision speed, specialized-talent flows, customer access, and durable outside relationships. Separate company location, founder residence, jobs, capital, and network ties.

In months 4–6, interview founders, would-be founders, investors, universities, employers, and leavers. Convert observations into rival causal hypotheses—for example, slow university licensing, weak specialist seed judgment, a thin founder pipeline, or poor customer access—and define a discriminating measure for each. Do not name the weakest constraint from anecdotes alone.

In months 7–12, run one bounded test against the best-supported constraint. If the issue is general friction, test a permitting or spinout-service change; if it is outside access, test structured specialist-investor and customer links. Give the test a named owner, fixed budget, comparison group or pre-period, and explicit uncertainty. Track mechanism evidence plus formation, survival, growth, inflows, outflows, and relationships—not leases, attendance, or press.

At month 6, stop any hypothesis that lacks discriminating evidence and change the measurement plan. At month 12, stop a test that did not move its proposed mechanism; change it if implementation failed; expand only when mechanism evidence and leading flow measures improve without unacceptable gaming or displacement; and remeasure the complete baseline on the same definitions. The office park becomes a candidate only if a later gate check shows that shared proximity is the limiting mechanism rather than a ceremonial output.
