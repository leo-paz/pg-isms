# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: accelerator-location-composition

Reviewer ID: ecosystems-forward-9c90-001

Recommendation: reject a blanket nine-month relocation requirement. Replace it with an opt-in, mechanism-specific local-engagement offer and team-by-team location decisions. Public value should be measured through stronger companies, local jobs and capital where actually created, and durable regional relationships—not months of physical presence.

Gate check for the regional allocation comes first. The accelerator must show that a controllable constraint requires local presence, that selection or reach is credible, that repeated interaction will produce useful community density, and that the region has the relevant labs, investors, operators, customers, and services. If any gate fails, do not fund relocation as the mechanism; test diagnosis, general friction, or outside links instead.

The ordered workflow composition is:

1. `building-startup-ecosystems`: define the public objective, establish dated founder/company/job/capital/relationship flows, identify the limiting reagent, and test whether local presence is actually the regional mechanism.
2. `preparing-to-found`: separately evaluate each company’s industry, stage, talent, customer and investor access, lab needs, family situation, immigration constraints, cost, and reversible location options. Biotech lab dependence is not equivalent to remote SaaS, and neither overrides founder-family or visa realities.
3. `advising-and-supporting-founders`: after selection and each team’s location choice, design the support delivered to the cohort.

Run a bounded comparison between teams choosing relevant local participation and comparable teams using remote or short-residency access. Name an owner, budget, duration, and selection rule; record the uncertainty; and track the claimed interaction mechanism, formation, survival, growth, regional transactions and relationships, mobility, selection, gaming, harms, and cost bearers.

Stop if required local interaction does not occur or harms participation. Change the format if shorter visits or distributed access produce the mechanism. Expand only when company outcomes and durable regional links improve without adverse selection. Remeasure after each cohort and at a dated multi-year review; leases, attendance, and relocation counts alone do not establish local impact.
