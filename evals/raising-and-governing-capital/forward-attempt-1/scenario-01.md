## Offer, economics, and control gate

No offer or document exists today. For each future document record issuer, company acceptance, expiry, no-shop, approvals, conditions, and funding; a term sheet is not cash. New-money ownership is `investment / (fully diluted pre-money value + investment)`; quantify any pre-money pool top-up. Qualified finance must show current/post-close common, options, convertibles, warrants, promised grants, and all other securities.

Score only complete offers: amount/use 25; partner/references 20; control 20; fully diluted economics 20; closing certainty 10; truth response 5. Require ≥75/100 and every hard gate: ≤20% new-money ownership; ≤1x nonparticipating preference; no full-ratchet anti-dilution; common retains board control; no unilateral investor operating veto; objective tranches only; acceptable information/pro-rata, confidentiality, future-financing and exit constraints; counsel-cleared conditions and authority. Curable misses may be countered inside this envelope; otherwise walk away. Counsel controls interpretation, negotiation, acceptance, signature, and closing; finance/accounting/tax owners clear their domains.
