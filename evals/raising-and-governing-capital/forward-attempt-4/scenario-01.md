## 4. Execution

Process header: M5. The cofounder owns scheduling, qualification, and one source-of-truth pipeline; the CEO retains accountability. Product integration sprints, renewals, and customer incidents outrank investor work. No meeting outside a batch without CEO approval; interest, data requests, introductions, and unsigned documents remain zero cash.

|hold|C5 reconnect group|do not manufacture urgency|honor next-quarter request; factual update only with permission|no active-process evidence|record incentives/conflicts; preserve relationship|status 09-25; reconnect 10-01/cofounder|

Materials: one dated factual summary, synchronized C1–C3 definitions, M2/M3 use model, and versioned C8 room. Log access, recipients, downloads, confidentiality terms, and metric versions; disclose customer-identifying or integration-sensitive material only after purpose and permissions are cleared. Counsel reviews confidentiality, conflicts, IP restrictions, exclusivity, and every negotiated instrument before acceptance. Qualified finance/accounting and tax owners revalidate cash, ARR, collections, expenses, FD capitalization, pool, instruments, and modeled effects using current evidence.

If any investor-facing claim is materially wrong: stop the claim; finance verifies value, scope, and cause; preserve source, calculation, old/new files, timestamps, recipients, and audit trail; synchronize every artifact; correct every affected recipient before company acceptance or countersignature; obtain acknowledgment. A constructive response may continue under M4; new pressure, concealment, or unresolved inconsistency fails M4. Never imply scarcity, consensus, or commitment unsupported by C5.
