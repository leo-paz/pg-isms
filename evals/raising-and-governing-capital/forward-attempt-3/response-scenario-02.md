## Offers, process, and precommitment

Simple ownership before pool/other securities: A `5/(40+5)=11.11%`; B `7/(55+7)=11.29%`. Thus B is not less dilutive on headline arithmetic. A’s post-money pool increase dilutes all post-close holders as defined; B’s pre-money increase dilutes existing holders before investment. Fully diluted current and post-close ownership is U2.

Illustrative uncapped, pari-passu waterfalls assume only new preferred/common and stated ownership. At $20m exit: A takes $5m, common $15m; B takes `$7m+11.29%×$13m=$8.47m`, common $11.53m. At $60m: A converts for $6.67m, common $53.33m; B takes $12.98m, common $47.02m. At $150m: A takes $16.67m, common $133.33m; B takes $23.14m, common $126.86m. U2/U4—pool, cap, seniority, securities, and definitive language—falsify these illustrations.
