Start 2026-07-20; cofounder owns CRM, materials, and deadlines. On 2026-09-04, launch only if the handoff passes; otherwise stop meetings. No document exists, so amount, expiry, exclusivity, approvals, conditions, acceptance, funding, fully diluted dilution, preference waterfall, and control are unknown—not negotiable facts. Any material error stops circulation; finance verifies scope/cause, preserves old/new artifacts, timestamps and recipients, corrects all recipients consistently, obtains acknowledgment, and counsel clears the next step.

**Handoff predicate:** by 2026-09-04, reconciled cash stays above $1.62m; no protected obligation is late; a board-approved downside plan preserves the floor; recurring collected receipts show retained-customer improvement for two completed monthly cycles; integration evidence and ranged costs pass; qualified advisers clear the cap table and process. This permits a bounded round, but default-alive requires enough subsequent cycle history that downside recurring collections cover recurring outflow before the floor without financing.
