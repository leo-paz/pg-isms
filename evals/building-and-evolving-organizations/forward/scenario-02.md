## 6. Test and final decision rules

### Hypothesis, rival, scope, and artifacts

- **Hypothesis:** Written bounded authority, one outcome owner, explicit pre-signature review, and incident command will reduce CEO wait and coordination failures without reducing protected quality.
- **Rival:** Complete requests, enforcement of an existing control, capability, or capacity—not authority—may explain results.
- **Affected unit:** The five existing groups and Ana; there are no reporting-line or title changes.
- **Change owner:** Ana for the executive boundary; each domain owner for outcomes.
- **Evidence owner:** Product lead for the combined log; Engineering and Support leads for incident evidence; Implementation and Sales leads for commitment evidence; relevant group lead and HR owner for hiring evidence.
- **Artifacts:** Versioned rights register, pricing matrix, protection map, complete-request form, pre-signature commitment note, incident-command record, hiring charter/scorecard where used, meeting transfer note, decision log, and dated review packets.
- **Confounders to tag:** Request incompleteness, owner absence, qualified-review hold, customer deadline change, incident volume/severity, existing contractual obligation, and Ana intervention outside the declared boundary.
- **Preserved option:** Restore Ana preapproval in one affected domain, narrow one boundary, keep the proven domains delegated, or later open a tightly chartered capacity role if the separate gate is met.

### Measures

The Product lead reports these by domain and in aggregate at each review:

1. Count and percentage of complete eligible decisions acknowledged within one business day and decided within two business days.
2. Count of eligible decisions waiting five or more business days, split by CEO wait, owner capacity, incomplete request, qualified review, or other evidenced cause.
3. Percentage of eligible decisions resolved without Ana's preapproval; number and reason for Ana interventions.
4. Number of nonstandard commitments signed before all required written dispositions; target zero.
5. For every declared production incident: time to one named IC, number of simultaneous people claiming command, time to named customer communicator, transfer acknowledgment, and protected-risk escalation.
6. Reversal rate, using owner decisions materially overturned because the decision was outside the boundary or unsupported—not ordinary new evidence or changed conditions.
7. Protected harms: unauthorized contract, money-movement error, security/privacy breach, legal/compliance breach, employment-process breach, or customer-continuity harm attributable to the changed authority.
8. Four-meeting transfer: number chaired by a receiving lead or canceled for no purpose, Ana's attendance count, decisions without owners, and backup coverage.
9. Owner load: open eligible decisions, oldest age, decision time, missed deadlines due specifically to capacity, and backup activations.

### Safeguards

- **Customer:** No nonstandard promise without the commitment note; Support owns incident communication; accepted scope and handoff owner travel with the deal.
- **Employee:** Hiring uses a role charter, same job-relevant scorecard and evidence definitions, independent written ratings before discussion, accessible bounded work samples, and current HR/employment review. No title, compensation, equity, classification, or employment term is changed here.
- **Safety, legal, and financial:** Named qualified reviewers keep stop authority; money-movement, security/privacy, evidence preservation, incident response, and current legal/compliance controls remain active.
- **Continuity:** One owner and one backup per domain; acknowledged handoffs; twice-weekly early digests; domain-level rollback; no all-at-once org-chart change.
