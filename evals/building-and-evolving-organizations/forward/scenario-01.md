## 4. Current bottleneck record

1. **Outcome at risk or opportunity delayed:** No current customer, release, or decision outcome is shown to be at risk. The proposed opportunity is financing “maturity,” but its effect is only an investor claim. Founder overload and future coordination strain are plausible risks, not yet established bottlenecks.
2. **Baseline:** As of 2026-07-16, the company has 42 paying teams; the four engineer-owned areas produced six consecutive on-time weekly releases; customer response is reported under four hours; no decision is reported to have waited more than one business day; and the team claims no recurring handoff failure. The release interval is six weeks. The evidence intervals and calculation methods for support and decision latency are not supplied and will be frozen on 2026-07-20.
3. **Owner, path, interfaces, and shape:** One engineer owns each existing product area. The founders sell, support, review product decisions, and code. Exact founder-review boundaries, cross-area interfaces, absence coverage, and support routing are unknown and will be recorded in the test artifact rather than replaced with hierarchy.
4. **Proposed mechanism and rival:** The proposed mechanism is that executive and manager titles plus reporting lines signal maturity and improve coordination. The main rival is that, with no current queue or failure, added layers create review handoffs, weaken product-area ownership, reduce coding capacity if engineers become managers, and consume cash and ramp time without relieving a measured constraint.
5. **Prior smaller attempts:** No prior delegation, boundary, tooling, priority, or interface test is available in the prompt. That absence supports running the bounded test before changing roles.
6. **Current and essential versus speculative:** Shipping, customer response, selling, support, product decisions, and coding are current work. A COO job, two engineering-manager jobs, and a financing benefit from titles are speculative.
7. **Relief and falsifier:** Relief would matter if a repeated founder queue, handoff failure, capability gap, or dropped essential outcome emerged. The bottleneck claim is falsified for this horizon if all six weekly releases remain on time, customer response remains under four hours, no decision waits over one business day, no interface failure recurs, and no same essential queue is missed for three consecutive weeks because capacity is exhausted.
8. **Full cost:** The COO option entails unknown recruiting cost, compensation, runway reduction, founder search time, ramp time, executive-management time, new interfaces, and succession risk. Two internal manager appointments could divert up to two of the four current product owners from hands-on work; external manager hires would add still-unmodeled cash and ramp cost. Either version adds reporting and handoff paths, can dilute end-to-end ownership, can shift culture toward title and approval signals, can create selection bias and incentive gaming, and needs continuity and reversal plans. Compensation, employment, title, equity, tax, and legal terms are not available and will not be invented.
9. **Conclusion:** The observed shape is healthy on the supplied outcome measures. The only affirmative reason for hierarchy is optics, and the operating concern is unverified. Full costs therefore exceed the evidenced relief. Select `keep-flat`.

## 5. Selected change, ownership, and continuity

### Selected branch

Keep the current flat reporting shape. From 2026-07-20 through 2026-08-27, each engineer continues to own the end-to-end outcome for the same product area and may make routine, reversible product-area decisions without founder preapproval. Founder review changes from preapproval to a weekly outcome review for this bounded class.

The delegated class includes backlog ordering, implementation choices, and reversible feature details within the engineer's existing area and the already committed weekly release. It excludes cross-area commitments, pricing or contract changes, hiring or cash commitments, company strategy, security/privacy/legal questions, and decisions the owner cannot reverse without material customer harm. Excluded decisions retain founder review and any appropriate qualified review.

## 6. Bounded test and decision

### Test design

- **Hypothesis:** The company can preserve current delivery, customer response, decision speed, and ownership with a flat shape while routine reversible product decisions move from founder preapproval to engineer ownership.
- **Rival:** Founder review is preventing material errors, or a hidden sustained workload/capability gap already requires different role capacity.
- **Affected unit:** All four existing engineer-owned product areas; only the decision class defined above changes.
- **Start:** 2026-07-20.
- **Intermediate:** 2026-08-06.
- **Final:** 2026-08-27.
- **Change owner:** Founder A.
- **Evidence and continuity owner:** Founder B.
- **Artifacts:** dated cash-forecast snapshot; baseline note; product-area owner/backup map; decision log; weekly release ledger; customer-response summary; handoff register; founder and engineer task/interruption logs; intermediate review; final signed decision record.
- **Measures:** on-time weekly releases; customer-response time using the calculation frozen on 2026-07-20; maximum product-decision wait; repeated interface failures; material reversals or rework attributable to omitted founder review; essential work deferred or dropped because capacity is exhausted; and accountable-owner coverage.
- **Confounders:** a release-scope change, customer incident, founder or engineer absence, demand spike, security/privacy/legal issue, or financing work. Founder B dates and records any confounder; it does not get silently attributed to organization shape.
- **Preserved option:** Founder preapproval can be restored for one unsafe decision class immediately. The company may later freeze and open a job-specific role charter if a sustained residual capacity or capability gap is observed and the survival case permits it.

### Safeguards

- The test does not relax security, privacy, legal, contractual, cash, hiring, strategic, or irreversible-decision review.
- Every area retains one outcome owner and a named backup; no customer issue is handed off without an accepting owner.
- No person receives a manager title, compensation change, or hidden people-management duty during the test.
- The evidence log records reversals and rework as well as speed, so fast but poor decisions cannot pass.
- Any employment, title, compensation, equity, accessibility, tax, securities, or background issue arising from a later role is owned by current qualified HR/counsel before action; none is decided in this record.

- **Decision now:** `keep-flat`; run the test. No COO hire, engineering-manager appointment, reporting-line change, or management search through 2026-08-27.
