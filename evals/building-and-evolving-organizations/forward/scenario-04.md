## 6. Bounded organization test and decision

- **Hypothesis:** Preserving the existing structure while making survival workstream ownership, backups, escalation, and evidence explicit will let the company execute the cash decision without a new layer, business-unit split, or culture committee.
- **Rival:** The survival workload creates a repeated cross-functional decision queue or a sustained residual capacity gap that the existing owners cannot handle after priorities and interfaces are narrowed.
- **Baseline:** Clear ownership and reporting, settled founder roles, and no recurring coordination failures as of July 16; no quantified historical latency or workload data was supplied.
- **Change owner / evidence owner:** CEO / CEO-designated current employee named in the July 17 board artifact. CEO remains accountable if delegation is not completed.
- **Dates and scope:** Start July 17; intermediate review July 31; final review August 14; all 18 employees remain in the current structure.
- **Artifacts:** Board-approved survival envelope; reconciled 13-week cash schedule; critical-workstream owner/backup map; dated decision-and-handoff log; current certification critical path; exception record.
- **Measures:** July 17 decision completion; July 25 payroll completion; count of unowned critical workstreams; organization-caused decision delays longer than one business day; missed critical and noncritical commitments caused by ambiguous ownership or handoffs; protected-control breaches.
- **Safeguards:** No bypass of payroll, employment, money-movement, certification, reliability, safety, legal, financing, or essential-continuity controls. Preserve evidence, respectful dissent, current owner authority, and a direct same-day escalation path. Confounders—including certification changes, customer/supplier changes, financing events, illness, and scope cuts—are logged rather than relabeled as organization effects.
- **`keep`:** Keep the current shape through the next survival review if the board selects the envelope July 17, payroll is completed July 25, every critical workstream has one owner and backup by July 18, there are zero critical commitments missed because of ownership or handoffs, and there is at most one organization-caused decision delay longer than one business day in each 14-day interval.
- **`revise` / change-process:** If there are two organization-caused decision delays longer than one business day in a 14-day interval, or one noncritical missed handoff, while ownership remains clear and no protected outcome is crossed, change only the responsible interface or decision limit. CEO names the change and owner within one business day and reviews that single change seven calendar days later.
- **`add-capacity` / open-role:** Only after priorities, decision limits, and the relevant interface are corrected, if the same essential workstream remains under capacity for three consecutive weekly reviews and causes at least two documented critical deadlines to be missed or forecast to miss in a 14-day interval, write a role charter for the demonstrated gap and compare delegation, bounded specialist help, and hiring at full cash/runway cost. Do not default to a chief-of-staff title.
- **`rollback-or-stop`:** If payroll is missed, an employment or money-control violation occurs, a certification/safety control is bypassed, an essential continuity commitment is missed, evidence is not preserved, or critical accountability becomes ambiguous, stop the organization experiment, restore the prior safe assignment, preserve the record, and transfer the consequence immediately to the CEO and relevant qualified owner.
- **Decision now:** Survival first; no chief of staff, business units, or culture committee.
- **Unresolved unknowns:** Exact cash timing, payroll amount, reducible obligations, employment-action costs, certification and collection dates, available signed financing, reserve chosen by the board, and whether survival execution produces a repeated organization mechanism.
- **Preserved option:** Revisit a narrow role, decision-rights change, leader-role evolution, incentive/culture intervention, or process/control only after its separate trigger is observed and its full cost fits the approved survival envelope.
- **Switch condition:** Return to `building-and-evolving-organizations` when the survival envelope is approved and either a `revise` or `add-capacity` threshold is met; select only the branch matching the logged mechanism. Financing remains with `raising-and-governing-capital`.
- **Next owner per result:** CEO keeps the structure under `keep`; the affected current department owner runs the seven-day interface test under `revise`; CEO owns any evidence-based role charter under `add-capacity`; CEO plus the relevant qualified finance, HR/legal, certification/safety, or continuity owner acts under `rollback-or-stop`.
