## 4. Current bottleneck record

1. **Outcome at risk:** Qualified mid-market opportunities are not receiving timely ownership and follow-up; founder attention is the scarce interface. Revenue impact is unknown and must not be inferred from Elise's pipeline forecast.
2. **Baseline:** As of July 15, the sales founder carries at most six active opportunities and qualified deals wait about 12 days. The exact measurement interval, count, distribution, and affected prospects are unknown; the July 24 funnel audit supplies them.
3. **Current owner and path:** The sales founder owns the end-to-end qualified-opportunity outcome and currently performs the selling. Until a hire passes the bounded test, that founder retains it. Every qualified opportunity must have one named owner, last contact, next action/date, and backup in a shared access-controlled opportunity register by July 17.
4. **Primary mechanism:** A recurring hands-on capacity and transfer gap at the sales founder prevents timely follow-up and requires a first player-coach leader who can personally operate and codify the motion.
5. **Causal rivals:** Poor prioritization or queue hygiene; inconsistent qualification; missing delegation to an existing team member; prospect-controlled waiting; or a transient arrival spike. Each is tested in the July 17-24 register cleanup and baseline audit.
6. **Prior attempts:** Unknown. By July 17 the sales founder records any previous changes to priority, ownership, tooling, qualification, scheduling, or delegation and their dated results. The company does not claim these cheaper interventions failed without that record.
7. **Current and essential versus speculative:** Timely follow-up of already-qualified deals is current and essential. Eight representatives, $4 million of network pipeline, a broad executive layer, and founder-status transfer are speculative.
8. **Relief value and falsifier:** Relief means at least 90% of qualified deals receive follow-up within two business days, no opportunity is orphaned, and the founder's routine active load becomes sustainable while conversion and customer trust are protected. The capacity bottleneck is falsified for this decision if the two-week cleanup achieves that response threshold at sustainable founder load with no recurring backlog; select `no-hire` on August 7 and monitor instead.
9. **Full cost:** Exact cash compensation, variable compensation, benefits, recruiting, tools, travel, legal work, equity/dilution, and runway effect are unknown and due July 24/August 5. Noncash costs are founder ramp time, candidate ramp, extra handoffs, CRM/register upkeep, decision interfaces, risk of scaling a weak motion, bias from prestige or confidence, incentive gaming around “pipeline,” customer continuity, and succession. Eight immediate representatives multiply cash, ramp, management, coordination, continuity, and reversal costs before the mechanism is proved; they are excluded.
10. **Conclusion:** The recurring stated delay is large enough to justify a bounded first-leader selection and player-coach test, but not eight immediate hires or governance/equity concessions. A one-week queue audit is the smallest intervention that can still falsify the role need before selection.

## 5. Selected change

### Frozen role charter — July 17

- **Outcome:** Own the qualified-opportunity flow end to end, reduce the approximately 12-day response delay, transfer routine selling work from the sales founder without losing customer context, and turn the existing founder-led motion into a measured operating system.
- **Initial span:** Hands-on player-coach with no authorized representative hiring and no assumed direct reports. Any current sales-adjacent contributors discovered in the July 17 team-shape check remain in their existing reporting lines unless separately approved.
- **Hands-on work:** Triage and follow up qualified opportunities; conduct calls; document qualification, stages, next actions, losses, and customer commitments; build weekly funnel review; test the founder-to-leader handoff; and coach only within the confirmed initial scope.
- **Decisions delegated after start:** Routine opportunity priority, cadence, next action, and documented follow-up within the approved pricing, security, privacy, contractual, and product boundaries.
- **Decisions retained:** The sales founder retains strategic-account exceptions, unapproved pricing or contractual departures, hiring, compensation, organization design, and founder/company governance. Current qualified owners retain security, privacy, legal, incident, and exceptional-risk approval. No candidate receives cofounder status or unspecified executive rights through this charter.
- **Interfaces:** The leader provides the sales founder a weekly funnel record and escalates stalled or exceptional opportunities within one business day. Product and the current customer/security owner receive customer-learning and commitment logs at the existing review cadence, or weekly if no cadence exists. No sales promise overrides their current review.
- **Information artifact:** One access-controlled opportunity register with qualification date, owner, backup, last contact, next action/date, stage, value definition, source, customer-controlled hold, loss reason, and security/legal/contract exception. Access follows least privilege.
- **Continuity and succession:** Until the 90-day test passes, the sales founder is accountable and a second founder is designated as absence coordinator by July 17. The leader documents every active opportunity so the designated backup can cover an absence of more than two business days. Bus-factor escalation fires whenever an opportunity's material context exists with only one person or its owner/backup fields are blank for one business day.
- **30/60/90 evidence:** By day 30 after the verified start date, every qualified opportunity has an owner, backup, and next action, and at least 90% receive follow-up within two business days for two consecutive weeks. By day 60, the response threshold holds for four consecutive weeks, no non-customer-controlled wait exceeds five business days, and the founder has no more than three routine active opportunities. By day 90, the same ownership and response controls hold, conversion is not materially worse than the verified pre-hire baseline over the available trustworthy interval, customer/security exceptions remain within current approved boundaries, and a measured sustainable-capacity model supports any proposed next role.
- **Evolution:** The job may evolve from player-coach to people leadership only after the `add-capacity` predicate in section 6 passes. The title and compensation must match the approved current job, not an aspirational eight-person organization.

### Common candidate evidence process

**Work sample, due July 24:** Each candidate gets the same anonymized/synthetic packet describing the 31-customer motion, the six-opportunity founder capacity, the approximately 12-day delay, and the approved security/contract boundaries. In a maximum 90-minute exercise, the candidate produces no more than two pages covering: first-30-day triage; opportunity ownership and founder handoff; the measures and definitions they would establish; one rival cause and falsification test; a customer/security escalation; and the evidence threshold for zero, one, or more future representatives. A 30-minute structured discussion tests decisions, not presentation polish. Offer accessible equivalent formats and reasonable accommodations. Do not use the artifact as unpaid company work; if the company chooses to operationalize any candidate work, agree compensation first.

**Structured interviews, complete July 29:** Ask every candidate for evidence of (1) personally building or transferring a sales motion under incomplete information, (2) reducing a real queue or capacity constraint, (3) deciding not to add headcount or staging it, (4) preserving a high-consequence customer commitment boundary, and (5) revising a confident forecast after contrary evidence. Probe the candidate's personal actions, baseline, result, failure, and what a direct observer could confirm.

**Targeted references, complete July 29:** For every candidate, distinguish direct observation from reputation and ask about hands-on early-motion work, hiring calibration, forecast calibration, coaching, customer judgment, and the environment in which results occurred. “Not observed” remains an evidence gap, not a negative inference.

**Scorecard:** Recruiting lead, sales founder, and current customer/security owner each submit a written 1-5 rating with cited evidence before discussion. Weighted dimensions are: hands-on early-motion diagnosis and work (25%); queue relief, qualification, and operating design (25%); customer/security judgment (15%); staged hiring and coaching judgment (15%); evidence and forecast calibration (10%); and ownership/interfaces/decision-rights judgment (10%). Critical dimensions are the first three. A candidate passes only with at least 4.0/5 on each critical dimension, at least 3.5/5 weighted overall, no unresolved material security/legal/integrity concern, and completed common evidence. Pedigree, employer prestige, charisma, confidence, communication speed, shared style, and willingness to accept a particular cash/equity mix receive no score.

After independent ratings, the recruiting lead audits differences of two or more points on any dimension for evidence, inconsistent access, accommodation effects, and prestige/style bias. Discussion may correct a factual error with a written reason; it may not retroactively change the rubric. If multiple candidates pass and their weighted totals are within 0.25 points, all tied candidates receive the same 30-minute follow-up by August 5 on only the disputed critical dimension, and only that dimension is rescored.

### Conditional post-hire bounded test

- **Change owner:** Hired leader for the operating change; sales founder remains accountable through day 90.
- **Evidence owner:** Recruiting lead maintains the 30/60/90 record; sales founder verifies funnel measures; current customer/security owner verifies exceptions; finance owner verifies cost.

- **Measures:** Percent of qualified deals followed within two business days; count waiting more than five business days excluding logged customer-controlled holds; orphaned-owner/backup count; founder routine active-opportunity count; qualification-to-next-step and qualification-to-close rates over the trustworthy interval; approved-boundary exception count; leader and founder load; and fully loaded cost.

**Post-hire thresholds:**

- `keep`: At current zero-representative scope, at least 90% of qualified deals receive follow-up within two business days for four consecutive weeks, zero opportunities are orphaned or wait more than five business days without a logged customer-controlled hold, founder routine load is at most three active opportunities, conversion is not materially worse than the verified baseline over the available trustworthy interval, and there is no material customer, security, privacy, legal, employment, or continuity harm.
- `revise`: The mechanism is supported and no rollback condition occurs, but follow-up is 80-89%, one interface repeatedly misses, or the founder retains more than three routine opportunities. Change exactly one of qualification, ownership boundary, review cadence, or workload allocation; run it for 14 days; set one final review date when the revision is approved. Do not add headcount during the revision.
- `add-capacity`: `keep` has passed, and for four further consecutive weeks correctly qualified arrivals exceed the measured sustainable capacity of the founder/leader system by at least 20%, more than 10% of deals breach the two-business-day threshold despite the proved process, and finance confirms the full cost is below the approved survival envelope. Open only one prewritten representative charter first. Eight representatives require eight separately evidenced capacity increments; the initial proposal creates no entitlement to them.
- `rollback-or-stop`: Any material unapproved security, privacy, legal, pricing, or contractual commitment; evidence tampering or metric redefinition to hide misses; loss of a named accountable opportunity owner for more than one business day; two consecutive weeks below 70% follow-up within two business days; or crossing the approved survival envelope. Stop headcount expansion, restore the sales founder as operating owner, preserve records, maintain customer coverage, and hand employment consequences to qualified HR/employment counsel rather than improvising them.
