Case ID: scenario-02
Reviewer ID: organization-baseline-generator-001

The next 45 days should distribute decision authority and repair cross-functional commitments without changing the entire organization.

Days 1–10:

- List the recurring decisions Ana currently approves and assign one accountable owner for each category.
- Establish written limits: sales owns standard pricing; a designated commercial leader owns exceptions within a defined range; product owns roadmap sequencing; an incident commander owns each production incident; functional leaders own hiring below an agreed seniority level.
- Require technical and implementation review before any nonstandard customer commitment is included in a contract.
- Create one intake record for custom-work requests, showing customer value, delivery effort, tradeoffs, and the final decision.

Days 11–25:

- Introduce a lightweight decision log recording the decision, owner, deadline, consulted parties, and rationale.
- Establish an incident protocol: one named incident commander, explicit roles, a shared status channel, and a short retrospective afterward.
- Give team leads explicit authority to reject custom work outside published criteria or escalate it to a named commercial-product pair.
- Transfer Ana’s four weekly meetings to appropriate leads. Ana may attend temporarily as an observer but should not remain the default chair.
- Set escalation rules: Ana becomes involved only in decisions exceeding defined financial, strategic, legal, or customer-risk thresholds.

Days 26–45:

- Review decision age weekly. The immediate target should be eliminating routine decisions waiting five business days.
- Audit new contracts for evidence of pre-signature delivery review.
- Run at least one incident simulation to test ownership.
- Coach Ana and the leads on delegated decision quality. Review outcomes after decisions rather than requiring advance CEO approval.
- Correct unclear authority or inadequate thresholds, but do not reclaim whole decision categories after one imperfect outcome.

By day 45, publish a concise decision-rights document and evaluate progress using decision latency, unreviewed commitments, incident ownership, and the number of matters still requiring Ana.
