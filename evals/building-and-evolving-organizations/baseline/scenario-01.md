Reviewer ID: organization-baseline-generator-001

Make no major reporting-line changes during the next six weeks. The current structure is producing timely releases, fast customer support, rapid decisions, and clear product-area ownership. Hiring a COO and two engineering managers would add substantial coordination cost without evidence of an operational problem they would solve.

Over the six weeks:

- Document current ownership, including which founder or engineer decides each recurring product, customer, and technical issue.
- Reduce founder overload selectively. Identify founder tasks that are frequent but routine, then assign them to existing owners with clear escalation thresholds.
- Keep engineers reporting as they do now. Consider an engineering manager only when coaching, prioritization, hiring, or coordination demands begin causing measurable failures.
- Define explicit triggers for future organizational changes, such as decisions regularly waiting several days, repeated cross-area conflicts, declining release reliability, or founders becoming persistent bottlenecks.
- If investor readiness is a concern, present the ownership map, operating metrics, and hiring triggers as evidence of deliberate management maturity.

Do not create executive or management roles as signals. Reassess after six weeks using operational evidence.
