# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: opportunity-learning-growth-composition

Reviewer ID: choosing-forward-001

The ordered composition is:

1. `choosing-startup-opportunities` frames and compares the three directions as problem, user, wedge, founder-fit, timing, and adoption hypotheses.
2. `learning-from-users` owns the hypothesis, sample, evidence threshold, test, and interpretation for each viable candidate.
3. `acquiring-and-growing-users` becomes primary only after one segment demonstrates real use, payment, or an equally discriminating commitment defined before the test.

Do not spend $100,000 or let click-through rate choose the company. Ads can measure message response, but cheap clicks do not establish that a user has the problem, will adopt a solution, or will remain. CAC is not meaningful before there is a credible retained or paying unit. At this stage the growth lead may recruit a pre-specified learning sample or seed a narrow beachhead, under the learning plan and with a small capped budget; that does not transfer decision ownership to growth.

First create one opportunity record per direction: named user, observed problem and workaround, cost, founder experience and access, smallest wedge, adoption effort, dated why-now evidence, disqualifiers, and the next cheap discriminating test. Mark unsupported directions `stop` or `discard`; mark a direction `deepen` only when its test justifies it. Then hand the learning owner an explicit hypothesis and a sample that includes non-friendly and disconfirming users.

Release growth work only at a dated gate defined before testing: one named segment must use the wedge in its real workflow repeatedly and make payment or another hard commitment proportionate to its adoption cost, across enough independent users that the result is not one friendly outlier. The record must also identify who can be reached and the initial beachhead. Once that gate passes, growth can test acquisition channels, activation, and expansion. If it fails, preserve the observed users and constraints and return to opportunity choice or revise the problem/segment/wedge hypothesis; do not optimize CAC around an unevidenced direction.
