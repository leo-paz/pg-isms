# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: ambition-versus-urgency

Reviewer ID: choosing-baseline-001

Choose the machine-shop inventory problem. It has observed operational pain, six near-term pilot commitments, founder-domain fit, and a reachable path to more users. The consumer wellness option may have a larger theoretical ceiling, but its current market-size claim is not evidence that anyone urgently wants this team’s product.

Start with the six pilots and define the narrowest workflow that removes a frequent spreadsheet error. Before building deeply, document the last several incidents, their cost, who owns the workaround, and what the shops will commit in data, implementation time, and payment. Use the pilots to learn whether the problem repeats across shops and whether a simple tool becomes part of the weekly workflow.

The choice would be wrong if the apparent urgency does not survive costly commitment or repeat use—for example, the six shops will not share data or pay, errors are infrequent and cheap, each shop requires bespoke integration, or the reachable segment is too small to support the founders’ ambition even after identifying adjacent workflows. A sufficiently strong falsification should reopen opportunity search, including the wellness direction only after it earns comparable behavioral evidence.
