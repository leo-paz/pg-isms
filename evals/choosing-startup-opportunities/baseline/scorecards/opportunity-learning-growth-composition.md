## Score

Score: 3/5

2. **0/1 — No explicit per-hypothesis learning handoff:** It recommends investigation in general, but does not hand each plausible problem to learning-from-users with its own sample, evidence standard, and interpretation responsibility.
3. **0/1 — Premature ad testing remains allowed:** It permits bounded ad message tests before validation without limiting them to recruiting a defined learning sample or seeding a narrow learning beachhead.
4. **1/1 — Rejects channel metrics as validation:** It explicitly says click-through rate, cheap traffic, and channel economics cannot choose the company or substitute for problem evidence.
5. **1/1 — Gates primary growth work:** Meaningful growth spend waits for a specific customer and job, repeated behavioral demand, commitment or payment, retention, and usable acquisition economics.
