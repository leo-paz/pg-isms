# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: selected-direction-interviews

Reviewer ID: choosing-baseline-001

The primary route is customer discovery for a selected problem, not broad opportunity selection. The team already has unusually strong evidence for the direction: direct observation and paid commitments. Its immediate job is to map the late-payment reconciliation workflow precisely enough to choose the first product boundary.

Sample 12–15 property managers across portfolio size, accounting system, payment volume, and whether reconciliation is handled by an owner, employee, or outside bookkeeper. Include the three design partners but do not let them dominate the sample. Ask each participant to reconstruct the last reconciliation from trigger to completion: inputs, systems opened, handoffs, exceptions, frequency, time spent, error consequences, and what happens when the work is delayed. Request artifacts or a screen-share, identify the highest-variance cases, and finish by testing willingness to provide data and use a narrowly scoped prototype.

Return to opportunity choice only if the detailed work invalidates the premise—for example, the pain is confined to the three committed firms, the manual work is rare or harmless, the buyer cannot authorize adoption, or a required integration makes the reachable initial market uneconomic. Ordinary variation in workflow should refine the initial segment and product, not reopen an unrelated idea search.
