# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: opportunity-learning-growth-composition

Reviewer ID: choosing-baseline-001

The ordered workflow is: opportunity discovery, problem validation, solution validation, initial delivery and retention, then scalable growth. Ads can be a bounded message test during discovery, but click-through rate and cheap traffic cannot choose the company because they do not establish that a defined user has a painful problem or will keep using and paying for a solution.

First select reachable user groups and investigate recent behavior, existing workarounds, frequency, severity, and founder advantage. Narrow to one problem only after the evidence repeats across users and produces a costly commitment. Then prototype the smallest solution, secure a handful of design partners or paying customers, and observe whether it completes the important workflow. Next establish repeated use, retention, and a credible economic relationship before optimizing acquisition.

Release meaningful growth spend only when the team can name a specific customer and job, show repeated behavioral demand, convert users to a defined commitment or payment, retain an initial cohort through the job’s natural cycle, and measure enough gross margin or customer value to set an acquisition-cost bound. Until that gate is met, the growth lead should support learning with small capped experiments rather than spend the $100,000 or let channel economics substitute for opportunity evidence.
