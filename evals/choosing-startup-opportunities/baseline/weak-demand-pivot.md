# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: weak-demand-pivot

Reviewer ID: choosing-baseline-001

Narrow to compliance teams. The broad meeting-recorder proposition has weak usage despite substantial signup volume, while a small, coherent segment uses the product daily for a specific painful job and is volunteering a concrete paid requirement. That is stronger demand evidence than the aggregate top-of-funnel number.

The next decision artifact should be a segment evidence memo paired with a paid-pilot plan. It should show the six teams’ shared workflow, current manual alternative, usage frequency, audit/export requirements, buyer and security approver, retention-control requirements, and willingness to pay. It should also state what would falsify the niche: for example, fewer than three teams agreeing to a paid pilot, retention controls requiring infeasible infrastructure, or daily use being driven by a temporary mandate.

For the next four weeks, stop broad acquisition work, interview and observe the six active teams plus several comparable compliance teams, and offer a narrowly scoped paid design-partner agreement with retention controls. Measure successful audit exports, weekly active teams, time saved, renewal intent, and implementation burden. If the pattern repeats and paid pilots convert, reposition around compliant meeting records. If it does not, reconsider the opportunity rather than resuming the generic product solely because it accumulated signups.
