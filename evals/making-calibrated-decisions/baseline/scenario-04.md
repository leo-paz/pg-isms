# Decision record — AI code-review agent proposal

**Record date:** July 20, 2026  
**Decision owner:** founders  
**Review date:** August 7, 2026, after a two-week test

## Decision

Do not pivot now. Spend up to $8,000 on the reversible concierge test while maintaining the current product and roadmap. In parallel, ask the three named design partners directly for problem interviews and, if interest is real, a concrete test commitment. Revisit the pivot only with observed behavior and a comparison to the current product’s trajectory.

## Why

The celebrity may have an early view of a genuine market shift. Prestige can correlate with access to information, but it is neither customer evidence nor a substitute for a causal argument. Their two falsifiable claims are that an agent category will dominate and that three specific partners will adopt within 30 days. The second claim is presently unsupported because they have not spoken with those partners.

Company-specific evidence points elsewhere today:

- Forty-two teams already pay, so abandoning the product has a real opportunity cost.
- Monthly logo churn of 4.5% is concerning and should be understood, but six months of improving expansion revenue is evidence of increasing value among retained customers.
- Five customers independently requested faster dependency analysis. None requested autonomous review. Requests are not orders, but this is stronger direct problem evidence than a third party’s prediction.
- A pivot consumes four months and pauses the roadmap; the concierge test costs $8,000, takes two weeks, and preserves the current option. The value of information is high because the cheap test can disconfirm the riskiest assumptions before the irreversible commitment.

## Test design

Recruit ten teams from a mix of current customers, the named design partners, and qualified prospects. Do not describe a generic “AI agent” and count enthusiasm. Offer a concrete concierge workflow that simulates the proposed autonomous review outcome, including expected inputs, turnaround, review surface, and human oversight.

Record before starting:

- the problem each team currently experiences and its frequency/cost;
- whether the buyer will provide repository access and spend time integrating;
- a specific task the service must complete;
- willingness to pay or sign a time-bounded paid pilot if it works;
- accuracy, usefulness, trust, security, and workflow thresholds;
- usage and repeat usage during the test.

Separately test whether faster dependency analysis would produce greater adoption or revenue with less disruption.

## Decision thresholds

On August 7:

- **Advance to a bounded product bet, not yet a full pivot**, if at least 6 of 10 teams complete the real workflow twice, at least 3 make a concrete paid-pilot or equivalent procurement commitment, outputs meet the predeclared quality/trust bar, and the named partners’ claimed demand is substantially validated.
- **Extend the test without pivoting** if usage is strong but payment, security, or autonomy acceptance remains unclear. Set a new narrow question and deadline.
- **Stop the agent branch** if teams praise the concept but will not connect data, use it repeatedly, or commit resources/payment, or if the agent does not outperform a dependency-analysis improvement on customer value and business impact.
- **Consider a pivot only after** evidence also shows the new opportunity’s expected value exceeds continuing the current product, accounting for four months of lost roadmap, churn risk, existing expansion, technical feasibility, and customer migration.

## Evidence discipline

Treat the celebrity’s view as one weighted input. Ask what nonpublic observations, causal mechanism, and disconfirming evidence underlie it. Record their prediction now so it can be scored later. Do not discount it because it is contrarian, and do not amplify it because its source is famous.

The founders should also segment churn and expansion: if churn clusters among teams needing dependency analysis while expanding accounts value the existing product, that changes which product bet is most coherent. If current retention deteriorates sharply during the test, revisit sooner; if expansion continues, the bar for abandoning the product rises.
