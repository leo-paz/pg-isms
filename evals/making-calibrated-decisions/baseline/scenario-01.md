# Decision record — onboarding, partner exclusivity, and seed extension

**Decision date:** July 20, 2026  
**Decision deadline:** July 31, 2026  
**Owners:** CEO integrates the decision; product, sales, finance, and counsel execute within their specialties.

## Current state

- Cash is $1.05 million. Monthly collected revenue less cash expense implies an approximately $90,000 monthly net cash burn and about 11.7 months of simple runway, before fundraising costs, revenue changes, or one-time obligations.
- Customer concentration is material: three of twelve customers represent 61% of ARR. A contract that deepens dependence on one partner may improve revenue while reducing strategic flexibility.
- The prototype result is promising: 11/14 completions versus 6/14 on the current flow. The sample is small and moderated, and it does not cover administrators or accessibility tooling. It supports a staged test, not a universal launch.
- The $240,000 annual contract and exclusivity are not facts until documented and signed. The scope, term, remedies, exit rights, and opportunity cost of exclusivity are unknown, and counsel has not reviewed the draft.
- One investor’s comment is a useful hypothesis about fundraising optics, not evidence that financing is available or that announcement timing should control the product or contract decision.

## Decision

1. **Do not ship the onboarding change to everyone next week.** By July 24, product will complete administrator and accessibility-tool checks and define instrumentation and rollback. If those checks pass, begin a limited, reversible rollout no earlier than July 27. Expand only after observing completion, activation quality, support burden, and regressions by user type.
2. **Do not accept exclusivity today.** Sales may continue negotiating, but no commitment should be made before counsel reviews an actual draft and the CEO sees an economic comparison between the contract and constrained opportunities. Prefer the narrowest possible restriction—specific use case, named account segment, geography, and short duration—with explicit performance obligations and termination/expiration mechanics. This is a governance gate, not legal advice.
3. **Begin fundraising readiness now, but do not launch a full seed-extension process solely because of the proposed announcement.** Finance should produce a 13-week cash forecast, scenario runway, concentration analysis, and target raise/use-of-funds by July 27. The CEO may begin quiet investor discovery. The timing of a public process should follow financing need and readiness, not depend on an unsigned partner or a universal product launch. This is not investment advice.

## Branches and gates

**July 24 — product evidence gate**

- If administrator workflows and accessibility checks pass, instrumentation is reliable, and rollback is immediate: proceed to a small rollout.
- If a safety, accessibility, permission, or administrator regression appears: hold the current flow and repair/test before rollout.

**July 27–30 — rollout gate**

- If the limited cohort shows a meaningful onboarding improvement without worse activation, support, or accessibility outcomes: expand in stages.
- If results are ambiguous: keep the cohort limited and collect more evidence.
- If guardrails regress: roll back.

**July 25 — contract-control gate**

- If no reviewable draft and written commercial commitment exist: treat the $240,000 as pipeline, not contracted revenue; do not grant exclusivity or announce it.
- If a reviewed draft exists: evaluate expected cash contribution, collection timing, implementation cost, concentration increase, restricted pipeline, duration, and exit conditions. Sign only if the company’s authorized decision-makers accept the downside under a conservative case.

**July 27 — financing gate**

- If the 13-week forecast or downside case shows less than the company’s chosen operating buffer: accelerate financing exploration and parallel cash-preservation options.
- If runway remains adequate: prepare deliberately; do not manufacture urgency around an announcement.
- If the partner signs on acceptable terms: update the forecast only for the payment schedule actually committed, and communicate status accurately to investors.

**July 31 — integrated review**

The CEO records each gate’s evidence, decision, owner, and next review date. These are three coordinated decisions, not one package: a failed product gate does not automatically kill a sound contract, an unsigned contract does not justify an announcement, and fundraising need should be assessed independently.

## Key unknowns to resolve

Payment timing and implementation cost; exact concentration after the contract; exclusivity scope and opportunity cost; pipeline conflicts; administrator and assistive-technology outcomes; retention/activation effects; downside runway; and whether multiple investors—not one—show real interest under accurate assumptions.
