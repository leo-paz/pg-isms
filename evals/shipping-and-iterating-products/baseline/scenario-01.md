# Raw baseline output

Case ID: scenario-01
Reviewer ID: shipping-baseline-001

Release the core workflow to the ten crews next week rather than holding it for six weeks of nonessential polish. Treat the spreadsheet as a supported fallback during the trial, and make the tool's scope explicit: it is for creating shoots, assigning and acknowledging handoffs, and seeing blockers. Avatars, themes, advanced permissions, and analytics stay out unless a pilot crew identifies one of them as a blocker to completing that path.

Before release, verify the core path end to end, add basic error logging and a way for the team to restore or correct a handoff, and name one team member as the pilot contact. Capture a simple baseline from the spreadsheet: missed handoffs, time to acknowledgement, unresolved blockers, and coordinator effort. Onboard the eight new crews in small cohorts over several days, while keeping the two existing crews as reference users. Do not migrate or delete spreadsheet data in a way that makes rollback difficult.

Review feedback and usage daily during the first week, then weekly. Prioritize defects that prevent or corrupt a handoff, followed by repeated sources of confusion. Compare completion, acknowledgement time, blocked-item visibility, fallback use, and support requests across crews. Ship small fixes as evidence arrives. At the end of two weeks, decide whether to expand, revise the workflow, or pause; only schedule the proposed polish when observed use shows that it materially improves adoption, control, or decision-making.
