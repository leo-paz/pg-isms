# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — Shipping owns the smallest complete visible outcome.** It ships delivery through acknowledgement and action with the existing keyboard workflow, while deferring rules-engine, campaign, and compulsory-launch work.
2. **1/1 — The release record is complete.** It names the supervisor/clerk job, separates manual-use and payment evidence from unvalidated web use, states the remaining outcome-and-migration hypothesis and delivery, integrity, placement, job, and role rivals, fixes scope and deferrals, dates reviews, and precommits thresholds.
3. **1/1 — The paid-pilot cohort is bounded and reversible.** A deliberately mixed cohort includes experienced shortcut users, keeps email and comparison users, receives migration guidance, office hours, and staffed escalation, and has explicit pause and rollback triggers and owners.
4. **1/1 — The evidence contract covers real work.** It observes delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task time, interruption recovery, errors, fallback, and support across experience levels and uses day-14, day-21, and day-30 expand/change/rollback/stop branches.
5. **0/1 — Growth ownership is transferred too late.** Delivery and integrity machinery return to engineering and a named job or need uncertainty returns to learning, but the response gives controlled recruitment and activation to growth only after released-workflow evidence. The paid pilots already cross the ownership gate; that evidence should limit broad scaling, not delay ownership.

Score 4/5
