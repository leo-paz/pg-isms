# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — The notification-to-resolution loop is primary.** It ships the complete alert, acknowledgement, assignment, and resolution path with legacy behavior available while deferring the rewrite, campaign, and all-user launch.
2. **1/1 — The release record contains the full decision contract.** It names the supervisor and clerk job, validated manual-use and payment evidence, unvalidated workflow and redesign risk with channel, timing, role, and compatibility rivals, scope and deferrals, dated reviews, and numeric gates.
3. **1/1 — The release is representative, supported, and reversible.** Matched paid-pilot cohorts include heavy shortcut users before staged broader exposure, keep email and legacy cards, receive migration help and staffed support, and use precommitted pause and rollback rules.
4. **1/1 — Measurement follows real work and recovery.** Delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task time, interruption recovery, errors, support, fallback, and recovered work are compared across new and experienced users before dated continuation, change, or stop branches.
5. **0/1 — The final ownership statement contradicts the earlier bounded handoff.** Engineering owns machinery and named contradictory channel, timing, or job behavior returns to learning. Although the response lets growth run bounded paid-segment activation, it ultimately says growth owns activation and retention only after a released cohort passes; paid pilots already cross the ownership gate while released evidence constrains scale.

Score 4/5
