Variant: guided

Rep: 4

1. **1/1 — The first complete visible loop is the release.** It scopes urgent delivery, acknowledgement, and entry into the existing resolution path, preserving clerk keyboard work while deferring the rules engine, other alerts, cosmetics, and campaign.
2. **1/1 — The release record is fully specified.** It records actors, job, validated manual and paid evidence, the falsifiable outcome-versus-migration uncertainty and later delivery, salience, and rules rivals, explicit scope, calendar, evidence minimum, and thresholds.
3. **1/1 — The cohort and migration plan are safe and representative.** A paid-pilot canary includes new and experienced clerks, retains email and the legacy queue, names champions and support, and uses precise pause and rollback conditions.
4. **1/1 — Evidence covers outcomes, recovery, and support.** Delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task time, interruption/re-entry, errors, fallback, abandonment, and support are segmented by experience and feed the dated day-30 expand/change/rollback/stop branches.
5. **1/1 — Ownership and downstream gates are correct.** Rules-engine architecture stays separate with engineering, only the unresolved notification need returns to learning, and growth may own targeted activation and retention for the paid segment now while the broad campaign waits for released-workflow evidence.
