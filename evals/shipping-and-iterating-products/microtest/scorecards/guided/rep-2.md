# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 2

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — The bounded end-to-end flow ships first.** It completes delivery, deep-linking, acknowledgement/assignment, and evidence capture while deferring rule authoring, unrelated redesign, acquisition, and forced migration.
2. **1/1 — The filled record contains every required decision input.** The target job, validated manual and paid evidence, remaining workflow, migration, rule, authorization, and integrity uncertainties, explicit scope and deferrals, calendar, and numeric gates are all recorded.
3. **1/1 — Representative release and migration controls are complete.** Sequential canaries include experienced and newer clerks, preserve email and compatibility paths, provide shift training and support, and precommit immediate safety and workflow rollback rules.
4. **1/1 — Real workflow evidence controls the dated branches.** Delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task time, interruption recovery, errors, support, and fallback are compared across experience segments before day-15, day-23, and day-30 expand/iterate/redesign/rollback decisions.
5. **0/1 — Growth ownership is transferred too late.** Architecture stays in engineering and only newly exposed user-need uncertainty returns to learning, but the response withholds same-segment recruitment, activation, and retention ownership until released-workflow gates pass. The paid pilots already cross the ownership gate even though those gates correctly constrain broad scaling.

Score 4/5
