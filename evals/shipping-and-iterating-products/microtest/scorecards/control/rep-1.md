# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — Shipping is primary and narrow.** It releases the existing urgent-exception notification flow behind flags, keeps the core acknowledgement path central, and defers the rules-engine rewrite and broad acquisition.
2. **0/1 — The release record lacks falsifiable rivals.** It calls the card layout the main adoption risk and supplies dates and gates, but it does not record competing explanations that would distinguish a workflow, delivery, need, or rules limitation.
3. **1/1 — Cohort, fallback, migration, and stop controls are operational.** It uses small shift-aware pilot cohorts, avoids an enthusiast-only sample, keeps email and the old queue, supplies champions and a change guide, and names concrete pause and rollback triggers.
4. **0/1 — Recovery is not measured across experience groups.** Delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task performance, errors, support, and tenure cuts are measured, but reopen/revert events do not test or measure interruption recovery for newer and experienced clerks.
5. **0/1 — The ownership handoff is incomplete.** Growth is constrained by released evidence, but no newly exposed need uncertainty is returned to learning and rules-engine architecture is not explicitly retained by engineering.

Score 2/5
