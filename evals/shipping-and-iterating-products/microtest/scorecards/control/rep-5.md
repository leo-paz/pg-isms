# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — The core exception loop ships without adjacent programs.** It scopes urgent notification, acknowledgement, escalation, and queue return while deferring the general rules engine, broad acquisition, and mandatory migration.
2. **0/1 — Rival uncertainties are not in the release record.** The response records evidence strength, scope, dates, and numeric gates, but it does not pre-register rival explanations that the release evidence will discriminate.
3. **1/1 — The cohort and fallback contract are complete.** Small paid-pilot cohorts retain email and legacy cards, receive runbook and shift support, and are governed by specific pause, local disable, and rollback rules.
4. **1/1 — Real behavior and recovery are measured by experience.** The plan measures delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, task time, errors, support, fallback and assisted recovery, segments by tenure and interaction style, and makes a dated expand/iterate/rollback decision.
5. **0/1 — The learning handoff is missing.** Engineering owns any later rules-engine proposal and growth remains constrained, but no contradictory need behavior or newly exposed need uncertainty is returned to learning.

Score 3/5
