# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 2

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — The smallest complete flow ships first.** The plan completes detection, notification, deep-linking, and acknowledgement for validated urgent exceptions while deferring the general rules engine, forced migration, and broad campaign.
2. **0/1 — The release record omits rival release explanations.** It records the target user, evidence, scope, calendar, and numeric gates, but does not state falsifiable rivals for whether delivery, queue migration, the user job, or rule machinery explains a miss.
3. **1/1 — Exposure and migration are bounded and reversible.** A representative paid-pilot cohort retains email and the old queue, receives training and an immediate feedback route, and is governed by precommitted incident and workflow rollback rules.
4. **0/1 — Fallback use is not interruption-recovery evidence.** The plan measures delivery, outcomes, task time, errors, support, fallback use, and experience segments with dated branches, but it never observes or measures recovery from interrupted queue work for both newer and experienced users.
5. **0/1 — The learning boundary is absent.** Engineering owns delivery and reliability and growth waits for safe use, but the response never routes only a newly exposed need uncertainty or contradictory behavior to learning.

Score 2/5
