# Shipping and Iterating Products Microtest Scorecard

Variant: control

Rep: 4

Scorer ID: shipping-micro-scorer-001

1. **1/1 — A narrow production release precedes expansion.** It ships the current urgent-notification candidate, limits fixes to the core outcome and guardrails, and defers rules-engine, campaign, and broad redesign work.
2. **0/1 — The record does not contain falsifiable rivals.** It distinguishes validated need evidence from unvalidated all-user safety and gives dates and thresholds, but never records competing explanations for an outcome miss as part of the release decision.
3. **1/1 — The rollout is representative, supported, and reversible.** Paid-pilot cohorts include experienced clerks, retain email and legacy behavior, receive a guide, onboarding, champions, and escalation, and have numeric pause and rollback triggers.
4. **0/1 — Recovery and newer-user behavior are not measured.** Delivery, acknowledgement, resolution, errors, task time, and support enter a dated day-28 branch, but interruption recovery is only an operational rollback drill and the evidence plan does not separately measure newer clerks.
5. **0/1 — Work is misrouted to learning.** Flexible rule authoring and broad layout redesign are placed in a learning backlog instead of keeping machinery and workflow fixes with engineering/shipping and returning only a new need uncertainty to learning.

Score 2/5
