The current facts support a staged operating reset: protect HarborGrid immediately, remove approval bottlenecks, build coverage before Omar’s October constraint, and test the redesigned founder roles through June 2027.

The financial baseline supports that approach: recurring agreements exceed fixed obligations by $20,000 per month through June 2027, and the $1.8 million cash balance alone equals 18 months of current fixed spending. Preserve that cushion, but do not use urgency around cash to drive an unnecessary founder decision.
