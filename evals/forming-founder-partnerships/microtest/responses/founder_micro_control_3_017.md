## How to make the longer-term decision

Score the proposed operating model against five questions:

1. Can Meridian meet customer and incident obligations without exceeding either founder’s stated limits?
2. Does every recurring decision have one accountable owner and a bounded escalation route?
3. Does the updated cash model remain acceptable after new hires or contracted coverage?
4. Can both founders spend meaningful time on what still motivates them?
5. Can the arrangement be reversed or handed off cleanly if it fails?

If the answer is yes, run the redesigned model. If qualified coverage is not secured by mid-September, reduce release scope and new commitments before asking either founder to violate their limits. If the founders cannot agree on authority, responsibilities, or sustainable compensation by August 31, the board should begin a counsel-guided orderly handoff or separation process—but that should be a contingency, not the opening move.
