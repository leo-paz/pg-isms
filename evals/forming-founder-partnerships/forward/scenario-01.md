## Trust gate

Accordingly, there is no `observed-breach` and no basis for a character verdict. Trust is simply unproven because consequential shared work is missing.

The narrow evidence check is the trial plus the employment/IP review. Mara is the independent process and evidence owner for candidate behavior; each person's qualified current counsel independently owns the legal safety of that person's participation. Until both counsel gates clear, safeguards are: no prototype work, no shared employer information or equipment, no transfer of background or company IP, no public founder title, no permanent stock promise, a new isolated repository with least-privilege access, a written decision log, and written design-partner data boundaries.

### Predeclared thresholds

- **`pass`:** Both legal gates cleared before work; both people began the disclosed full-time schedule on August 1; the partner confirms in writing that the prototype completes the agreed representative workflow using allowed test data; both attended the workflow session and demo; the artifacts show timely disclosure of blockers, completion of each person's unpleasant work, at least one substantive challenge by each person, and resolution or safe deferral of the joint decisions; and no trust-gate trigger occurred. A pass permits, but does not compel, a `formalize` decision followed by counsel-drafted terms.
- **`change`:** Trust remains intact and the agreed workflow is delivered, but observed evidence shows a materially different scope, role, availability, or dependency plan would work better. Mara then offers only that narrower reversible role or trial scope, with no cofounder title, and sets a new decision record before further work.
- **`extend`:** Both counsel gates cleared and both candidates met every threshold within their control, but the design partner supplied the workflow, allowed data, or access too late for a fair outcome. Mara may extend once, only for the named missing evidence, through August 28. Any other cause does not qualify for extension.
- **`stop`:** Counsel does not clear safe participation; Ishan refuses candidate status or reversible terms; either person does not make the disclosed August 1 transition; a material outside obligation is concealed; a trust breach occurs; a critical commitment is missed without prompt disclosure and recovery; or the prototype cannot meet the agreed core workflow for reasons within the pair's control. Mara then ends the candidate process, preserves the records and partner continuity, and uses `solo-and-search` or a scoped contractor/adviser rather than forcing a founder match.
