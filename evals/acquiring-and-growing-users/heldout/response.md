# Held-out response
Case ID: growth-heldout-003
Generator ID: growth-heldout-generator-003

## Operating memo — July 14 to August 14, 2026

**Lead: Growth.** Growth should own one activation-repair cohort for the next four weeks, with Engineering serving a single bounded workflow change after observed evidence. Do not add volume or a channel. The segment has crossed the handoff into growth work: companies paid, uploaded current work, and generated proposals. Runway, pricing, packaging, and participant policy are not the binding constraints.

**Diagnosis.** Acquisition is not the bottleneck. Cold email → demo, demo → paid start, paid start → real upload, and upload → generated proposal all beat their predeclared floors. The first broken transition is generated proposal → independently submitted live proposal. The dashboard's 17/39 submitted rate is already below its 60% floor, but it also overstates product activation: 11 submissions required 28 hours of untracked founder rewriting and formatting. Only 6/39, or 15.4%, submitted without staff editing. That rescue averaged about 2.5 founder hours per rescued account and cannot be treated as scalable momentum.

The 5/6 repeat rate and 2/5 referral rate among independently activated accounts are encouraging downstream signals, but the sample is tiny and conditioned on surviving the broken step. They justify repairing activation, not expanding acquisition.

**Bounded move: repair independent submission, then test it on exactly 20 accounts from the same segment and source.**

- July 14–16: spend no more than ten founder-hours observing current customers work from generated draft through live submission. Prioritize rescued or stalled accounts and include a few independent submitters as contrasts. Record the exact point of hesitation, edits attempted, time, abandonment, and whether staff touched or sent the artifact. Observation and product explanation are allowed; editing and sending are not.
- July 16: Growth names the single repeated workflow block only after it is directly observed. A practical selection rule is the same block in at least three rescued or stalled accounts. Preserve all other possible changes for later; if no block meets that rule, ship no speculative change.
- July 17–24: Engineering gets one workweek to remove only that block. The acceptance test is that a target user can take a real generated proposal through live submission without staff editing or sending. Do not use founder labor to make the test pass.
- July 20–31: enroll the committed 20 accounts, unchanged segment, inclusion rule, source, and fee. Do not discount, add another channel, or add automation. Log every funnel step by account plus all staff explanation, editing, sending, and support time.
- August 14: freeze the cohort and decide from the independent-activation count. An account activates only if it uses a real walkthrough to generate and submit at least one proposal to a live prospect without any staff editing or sending. Every paid account remains in the denominator; a rescued submission is a failure for this measure. Report upload and generation failures diagnostically, but do not substitute them for the decision metric. Repeat and referral remain secondary observations.

**Assumptions:** The paid fee plus real current-work upload is the predeclared evidence transferring ownership to Growth. A live submission can be verified at account level. All 20 have at least seven full days with the shipped workflow before the August 14 freeze; if that is false for an account, keep it in the denominator and flag the exposure timing rather than changing the threshold.
