# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: scenario-01
Reviewer ID: growth-forward-001

`learning-from-users` remains the primary owner. The company has not crossed the evidence gate: it does not yet know whether independent clinic managers have an urgent or repeated inventory problem. Ad click-through, opens, and signups are exposure or recruitment signals, not evidence of need. The growth lead may execute recruitment, but may not reinterpret those signals as validation or optimize a scalable acquisition channel.

Use the learning owner's sample definition as the contract. Recruit 20 current independent-clinic managers who personally participate in ordering or stockout handling, preserve the required cold-prospect share, and exclude people without current responsibility for that work. Use direct, bounded outreach from more than one source so the sample is not just friends or conference followers. For every prospect, log source, eligibility, contact attempt, response or nonresponse, scheduling, completion, and any incentive. Obtain consent for observation and avoid collecting unnecessary clinic, employee, or customer data.

Keep two records separate:

- The recruitment record measures eligible people contacted, replies, scheduled observations, completed observations, nonresponse, time, and cost by source. These facts help repair recruiting when the sample is incomplete.
- The learning record, owned by `learning-from-users`, captures observed ordering and stockout behavior, frequency and consequences, current workarounds, who is affected, and contradictions. Outreach engagement does not enter the demand-evidence column.

The smallest intervention is founder-assisted recruiting and scheduling, not a landing-page experiment. If cold managers do not respond, change the contact wording, timing, or source only enough to fill the predeclared sample; do not select an opportunity because one source produces more clicks. If observation shows no repeated or consequential problem, return that result to learning and stop growth work. If a defined segment shows the behavior and then demonstrates real use, payment, or an equally discriminating predeclared commitment, ownership can transfer: growth can map source-to-activation cohorts and test a bounded channel. Until then, the next owner after every recruitment result is `learning-from-users`.
