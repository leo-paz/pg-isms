# Acquiring and Growing Users Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 5

Scorer ID: growth-micro-scorer-001

1. **FAIL (0/1) — The growth constraint and enterprise latency are explicit, but survival state is absent.** The response starts from six paying founder-acquired pilots, makes integration and week-eight scheduling—not leads—the binding problem, and preserves the six-to-eight-week evidence window. It never records runway, time-to-zero, default-alive/default-dead status, or another survival assumption/branch.
2. **PASS (1/1) — The source-to-retention beachhead loop is complete.** One narrow supported-system segment moves from source and qualification through readiness, setup, verified integration, real scheduling, repeated weeks, retention, and referral/expansion. Activation binds integration to user-performed value; source/state cohorting is explicit; and setup and retention rivals include procurement, access, product defects, workflow fit, reversion, and founder rescue.
3. **FAIL (0/1) — Founder/manual ownership and skill routing remain incomplete.** Founder acquisition continues, but implementation/customer success runs onboarding and support. A combined product/engineering DRI owns setup artifacts, integration, instrumentation, reliability, and rollback rather than separating shipping-owned setup/exposure from engineering machinery; business-model ownership alone does not rescue the conjunction.
4. **PASS (1/1) — Rates, unit measures, calendar gates, and branches are explicit.** The funnel includes referral/expansion and requires numerator/denominator for every transition rate by source and cohort, while time, support per site, acquisition/onboarding cost, concessions, and user incidents are recorded. Day-14/21/30/60 reviews precommit keep-manual, repair, expand, pause, and stop-reseller decisions over honest maturity windows.
5. **PASS (1/1) — Full-loop evidence gates every scale path.** Activation, consecutive-week retention, support budget, no hidden founder rescue, service quality/user-impact errors, and viable reseller economics must pass before reseller, trade-show, or sales expansion. Leads, demos, setup starts, praise, signed pilots, and referrals are explicitly insufficient alone.

Score: 3/5
