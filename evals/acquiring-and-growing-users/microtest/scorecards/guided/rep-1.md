# Acquiring and Growing Users Microtest Scorecard

Variant: guided

Rep: 1

Scorer ID: growth-micro-scorer-001

1. **PASS (1/1) — Growth ownership, the constraint, and the state record are explicit.** Six paying founder-led pilots establish the post-validation growth context; integration-limited activation and independent retained value—not lead volume—bind; the six-to-eight-week evidence latency is protected; and facts, product uncertainties, price/subsidy, founder dependence, and runway/survival conditions are recorded.
2. **PASS (1/1) — The beachhead loop and causal comparison are complete.** The plan defines a narrow supported-system dental-group segment and adjacency, maps source through paid pilot, setup, access, live integration, two real workflows, independent week-eight use, and referral/expansion, segments source and implementation states, and tests access, integration, workflow-value, and founder-rescue rivals.
3. **FAIL (0/1) — The routing is strong, but founder-led onboarding/support is explicitly removed.** Shipping owns interface/workflow changes, engineering owns connector reliability/instrumentation, and the business-model owner settles reseller economics. The founder may sell and attend kickoff but may not operate the integration, then exits onboarding; implementation and customer-success owners provide the binding-point support, so the founder-led recruitment/onboarding/support conjunction fails.
4. **FAIL (0/1) — Referral and cost are not fully expressed as numerator/denominator cohort measures.** The response gives exact funnel cohort counts, explicit setup/integration/retention denominators, support hours per activated site, time gates, economic guardrails, dated reviews, and keep-manual/repair/expand/stop branches. It never defines the customer-referral numerator and eligible retained-customer denominator; the ten reseller introductions measure partner lead quality, not that referral transition, and cost lacks an equally explicit cohort denominator.
5. **PASS (1/1) — All expansion paths remain behind the full loop.** Reseller, trade-show, and hiring scale wait on setup, activation, early independent use, mature retention, supportability, safety/user effects, and acceptable economics. The response explicitly prevents the 400-account claim, leading indicators, referrals, praise, starts, or demos from substituting for the joint gate.

Score: 3/5
