# Raw evaluation output

Case ID: scenario-04

Reviewer ID: positioning-baseline-002

The primary workflow is trust and safety/community governance, supported as appropriate by legal, security, and crisis-communications review. It includes evidence preservation, immediate risk reduction, investigation, consistent policy application, sanctions, appeals, moderator operations, and communication with affected members. People with that authority and expertise—not a competing-and-positioning process—should adjudicate the cases.

Positioning has only a limited downstream role. Because the audience, promise, and alternatives are already settled, it can help express how the community's public promise translates into clear member expectations and ensure the eventual communication does not contradict that promise. It may also identify reputational confusion that the policy and communications owners should address.

Positioning should not write enforcement standards, choose penalties, determine facts, decide appeals, or publish case outcomes on its own. Those decisions require a legitimate governance process, consistent precedent, due process, privacy safeguards, and an assessment of safety and legal risk. The team should route the open work to the trust-and-safety owner, establish conflict-free reviewers and an appeal path, and publish only the level of detail that process determines is safe and appropriate.
