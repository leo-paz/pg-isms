Replication: 3

Position the company as the independent control plane for governed, auditable incident resolution across clouds—not as an AI-summary product or a generic open platform. The durable value is joining changes across three clouds, enforcing rollback playbooks, and preserving the audit trail. Treat summaries as a replaceable workflow component.

For the next 90 days:

- Reject the CEO’s proposed all-in plan. Do not copy the provider’s summary interface, declare an open platform, or commit the full acquisition budget to marketplace ads.
- Preserve the marketplace channel because its cohorts retain better, but stop increasing dependency before the fee rises. Model customer-level economics at 22%, renegotiate with the provider, and limit marketplace spending to campaigns that remain profitable under the new share.
- Test channel portability immediately: offer a representative set of prospects and renewals direct contracting, migration assistance, and equivalent procurement terms. Track conversion, sales-cycle length, retention signals, and willingness to leave marketplace procurement.
- Build resilience against restricted event history: create customer-controlled ingestion and archival paths, prioritize cross-cloud identity and billing decoupling, and document what workflows would fail under the announced restrictions.
- Keep the existing summary experience differentiated by context from timelines, playbooks, and audit records. Evaluate the open model inside end-to-end incident-resolution tests—not on summary benchmarks alone—and use it only if workflow quality, latency, security, and cost hold up.
- Publish a narrow, supported exporter and integration API based on the two consultancy implementations, but do not launch a broad free SDK yet. Interview those consultancies and relevant customers, then require evidence of adoption or purchasing influence before funding a platform organization.

At day 90, expand the SDK only if integrations demonstrably affect acquisition, retention, or expansion. Increase direct acquisition only if portability tests work. Otherwise, remain marketplace-present while negotiating economics and accelerating architectural independence. The strategic goal is optionality without abandoning the company’s strongest current channel.
