Replication: 1

Position the company as the cross-cloud system for governed incident resolution—not an AI-summary product and not yet an open platform. Its defensible value is joining changes across clouds, enforcing rollback playbooks, and preserving an auditable record. Summaries should remain an embedded workflow feature, with the open model evaluated on complete incident outcomes rather than benchmark parity.

For the next 90 days:

- Protect the core product from the provider’s historical-access restriction: determine exactly what data will disappear, secure permitted customer-owned ingestion and retention paths, and ensure timelines remain complete across all three clouds.
- Keep the marketplace channel, but do not commit the full acquisition budget before the fee increase. Model unit economics at 22%, preserve only campaigns that remain attractive, and run a controlled marketplace-versus-direct acquisition test.
- Test portability with existing customers and qualified prospects: offer direct contracting, migration assistance, and equivalent identity and billing workflows. Measure conversion, sales-cycle impact, retention risk, and willingness to leave the marketplace.
- Do not copy the provider’s native summary interface. That would frame the startup as a paid imitation of a free feature. Demonstrate instead how summaries trigger governed rollback actions and feed the audit trail.
- Do not declare an open platform yet. The two consultancy exporters show integration interest, but not proven buying demand. Document and stabilize a narrow exporter API, recruit those consultancies plus several customers as design partners, and test whether integrations influence purchases, expansion, or retention before funding a broad free SDK.
- Evaluate the open model in shadow mode on end-to-end incident resolution: action accuracy, rollback compliance, time to resolution, audit completeness, and operator correction rates.

At day 90, expand the SDK only if integrations demonstrate commercial pull; scale direct acquisition only if buyers will follow outside the marketplace; and retain marketplace spending only where 22% economics still work. This preserves the valuable channel while reducing dependency and concentrates positioning on the workflow the provider’s free summary does not replace.
