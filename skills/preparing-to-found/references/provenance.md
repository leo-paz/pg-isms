## Runtime scope

Decide whether and when to pursue a startup, then build readiness through owned work, skills, peers, and environment.

### Triggers

- Assess whether starting a startup is the right next move.
- Create a preparation plan or help a founder or active team choose a startup location.

### Non-triggers

- Evaluate an already chosen startup idea.
- Plan an active fundraising process.

### Stage conditions

- Use readiness work before commitment when options are still cheap to preserve; location decisions can recur after founding.
- Treat location advice as conditional on family, current immigration law, industry, distributed access, committed funding, and relocation distraction.

### Preserved tensions

- Deliberate preparation versus learning by beginning.
- Hub advantages versus relocation cost and distraction.
