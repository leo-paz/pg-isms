## Runtime scope

Generate, frame, compare, and pivot startup directions around real problems, founder fit, urgency, mission, and timing.

### Triggers

- Find or compare startup ideas.
- Reframe a solution-first concept or decide whether to pivot.

### Non-triggers

- Design interviews for a direction already selected.
- Choose a database or deployment architecture.

### Stage conditions

- Before strong user evidence, prefer problems the founders understand directly and can test cheaply.
- During a pivot, preserve learned constraints rather than restarting from slogans.

### Preserved tensions

- Organic problem discovery versus deliberate idea search.
- Ambitious upside versus immediate user intensity.
