## Runtime scope

Turn a problem into a small working product, launch at an appropriate risk level, and improve it through short evidence-producing loops.

### Triggers

- Scope a prototype, first launch, redesign, or iteration loop.
- Decide how much quality and reversibility a release requires.

### Non-triggers

- Run open-ended user discovery before choosing what to build.
- Select a company-wide fundraising strategy.

### Stage conditions

- Move quickly when errors are cheap and reversible; raise the gate for irreversible harm, trust, security, or legal exposure.
- A first release should maximize learning, while a redesign must account for existing user habits.

### Preserved tensions

- Fast launch versus quality and safety.
- Founder taste versus observed user behavior.
