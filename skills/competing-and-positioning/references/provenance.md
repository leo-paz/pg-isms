## Runtime scope

Position a startup against alternatives, anticipate incumbent and technology shifts, and build advantages without becoming rival-obsessed.

### Triggers

- Analyze competitors, differentiation, moats, positioning, or a platform dependency.
- Plan around an incumbent or technology transition.

### Non-triggers

- Choose a first user interview sample.
- Moderate behavior inside the startup's own user community.

### Stage conditions

- At the earliest stage, user learning usually outranks broad competitor surveillance.
- Platform leverage is attractive only with an explicit plan for dependency and policy risk.

### Preserved tensions

- Learning from competitors versus imitating them.
- Immediate distribution leverage versus long-term platform dependence.
