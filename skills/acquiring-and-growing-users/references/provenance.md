## Runtime scope

Choose a beachhead, acquire and activate users, measure the right growth phase, and retain users before scaling distribution.

### Triggers

- Recruit a validation sample or seed a narrow beachhead under a user-learning evidence plan.
- Design the first acquisition, activation, or marketplace-liquidity plan after a target segment shows real use or willingness to pay.
- Diagnose growth, retention, or expansion after demand is established.

### Non-triggers

- Validate whether the underlying problem exists; use user learning first.
- Fix marketplace or community trust, ranking, moderation, reputation, or abuse; use community governance.

### Stage conditions

- If the main unknown is whether or why the need exists, user learning owns the hypothesis, sample, evidence standard, and interpretation; growth may only execute recruitment or beachhead seeding under that plan, and owns optimization only after real use or payment.
- Recruit manually before optimizing a channel, and do not treat top-line acquisition or subsidized marketplace activity as healthy growth when activation, liquidity, or retention is weak.

### Preserved tensions

- Unscalable early recruitment versus scalable channels.
- Fast growth versus preserving user delight.
