# Inherited runtime contract

Apply the applicable product row before selecting a channel, metric, or cadence.

- **Survival:** If default-dead or time-to-zero is binding, run survival scenarios and a switch date before normal growth optimization.
- **Capital-light software:** Direct onboarding, product-led distribution, and day/week cohorts can fit reversible products; they do not waive high-harm gates.
- **Enterprise/integration:** Require workflow access, deployment, procurement, security, supportability, switching, and integration evidence. Compliments, self-serve signups, and undeployed pilots are not activation.
- **Hardware:** Use demonstrations, early units, preorder evidence, and channel-specific distribution over fabrication, supply, reliability, inventory, and physical-use intervals.
- **Biotech, energy, capital-heavy:** Use scientific, regulatory, partner, trial, deployment, and specialist-buyer milestones; broad acquisition and weekly software growth may not apply.
- **Marketplace/community:** Growth owns concentrated seeding and liquidity. Governance owns trust, ranking, reputation, moderation, abuse, norms, and interaction quality once participants interact.
- **High-harm/regulated:** Let trust, permission, assurance, monitoring, rollback, human review, and current law constrain distribution before channel optimization.
