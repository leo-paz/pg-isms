## 1. Route and today's decision

Use one pipe-tight row: `as-of|today's decision|horizon|venture/product and stage|survival state/binding constraint|primary then support|accountable owner|checks/final date|floor/switch IDs|venture-specific defaults refused`.

Apply these predicates first:

- For an operating company, derive no-financing downside/base/upside equilibrium against the cash floor. Select `managing-runway-and-survival` when cleared cash or protected obligations bind acutely, or every evidence-bounded path reaches the floor before recurring collected receipts can cover recurring outflow. Capital may then support only truthful reusable materials, qualification, and a distraction cap until the dated handoff fires.
- Do not route a company to survival merely because current receipts are below current outflow when obligations are current, cash is ample through the decision horizon, and observed growth supplies an evidence-bounded path. If it has chosen outside capital for a milestone, select `raising-and-governing-capital` with a bounded floor and a dated trend/floor falsifier.
- Sparse, pre-revenue, long-build, or capital-heavy status first routes to `managing-runway-and-survival` unless its completed handoff is supplied. In the route row, its final field must literally list `software-seed comparison; ramen profitability; weekly evidence` as refused defaults. Select `raising-and-governing-capital` only when that handoff proves cleared cash reaches dated technical, supplier, regulatory/safety, customer, and financing-readiness gates while preserving the approved floor, protected obligations, and no-raise/delay option. Zero-financing-to-product-completion is not the test.
- If whether to use outside capital or what the venture can absorb is unsettled, route to `designing-business-models` and inherit its amount/capital-fit handoff. Otherwise use this skill for round readiness/process, offers, terms, valuation, dilution, governance, or control.
- If financing is effective, funded, and settled and the request concerns ordinary board cadence, management systems, or executive accountability, route to `building-and-evolving-organizations`. Re-enter here only for a new financing, amendment, waiver, consent, investor-right, disclosure, acceptance, or closing decision.

Static runway alone proves neither default-alive status nor round readiness. Financing interest, diligence, soft circles, evaluation letters, strategic discussions, nonbinding documents, unsigned terms, and fund-signed but company-unaccepted offers are zero cash and zero committed demand.

## 3. Decision model

Emit only the selected model:

Any selected model whose branch family can accept or close includes one `G0` readiness/completion row. `G0` names the precommitted milestone/runway, partner/reference, truth, certainty, economics, and control thresholds; when multiple offers compete it also cites deterministic `SEL`. `ZaR` means those thresholds pass, current facts are verified or corrected, qualified counsel has reviewed the acceptance form, required approvals exist, and an authorized signer is ready while the company remains unsigned. With multiple offers it additionally requires `SEL`'s unique anchored winner. Acceptance produces `ZaE`, timestamped signed-and-delivered evidence. `ZcR` means `ZaE` exists, definitive documents are ready for release, conditions and approvals are complete, and funding mechanics plus counterparty wire confirmation are ready while no cash is assumed. Closing produces `ZcE`, effective documents and cleared funds. Readiness never requires its own completion evidence; acceptance is never called closing.

- **Ordinary bounded round:** `milestone/evidence|ranged uses/time|contingency/working capital|amount/existing-cash contribution/floor|economic-control envelope|no-raise or staged alternative|adviser checks|falsifier`, plus `downside/base/upside|milestone/date|cost/close date|cash-floor and gap/raise range|assumptions/control IDs|effect`. Add the compact `G0` row when `close-or-counter` is possible.
- **Capital-heavy readiness:** use the ordinary thesis/scenarios, but make independent technical review and current supplier evidence gates before fixing amount or completion date. Show working capital separately. Derive bounded provisional downside/base/upside funding ranges after existing cash, floor, process timing, contingency, and staged milestone cost. Every staged option names its observable milestone outcome, date, tranche/use, next commitment gate, and stop consequence. If an input is unknown, show a bounded formula and the range it changes; never leave downside unbounded or repeat management's number as fact.
- **Competing offers:** first render `ID|requirements/control IDs` with rows `D0` and `G0`; then render exactly three scenario rows: `downside/base/upside|each offer's normalized ownership, cash-service, priority, control and exit consequence|common assumption/falsifier`. `D0` records milestone/ranged amount-use/time, existing-cash contribution, floor, no-deal plan, falsifier, applicable instrument model, and `SEL`. Normalize only relevant fields: priced equity ownership/pool/preferences; SAFE or convertible cap/discount/MFN/pro-rata/conversion/seniority/maturity/interest; debt principal/interest/amortization/maturity/warrants/covenants/security/default/refinancing; revenue-share percentage/repayment cap/term/cash burden. `SEL` freezes before scoring: hard gates per factor; weights summing to 100; factor-specific observable anchors for scores 0, 3, and 5; scores 1, 2, and 4 only by stated numeric or ordered interpolation; weighted total `sum(weight*score)/5`; minimum total; tie margin; and tie/unresolved behavior. A selectable offer passes every hard gate and is the sole highest total beyond the tie margin. Otherwise counter, continue, or no-deal—never prefer an offer by label or first-passing status. `G0` names six subgates: `A` amount/use; `E` normalized economics; `C` control and restrictions; `P` partner/reference behavior; `T` truthful synchronized materials and response; `Z` document/authority/certainty state. `T` includes stop/verify/correct/audit/acknowledgment when an error exists. `ZaR` additionally requires `SEL`, adviser-reviewed acceptance form, approvals, and signer readiness while company-unsigned. Acceptance produces `ZaE`; definitive documents, conditions, approvals, and funding readiness produce `ZcR`; effective documents and cleared funds produce `ZcE`. Put facts and unknown-control tuples in section 2 and cite them here.
- **Survival route-away:** show cash, recurring collected receipts, recurring outflow, derived net burn, obligations, floor, and downside/base/upside consequences. Define a dated default-alive test requiring sufficient cycle history in which downside recurring collected receipts cover recurring outflow before the floor without assumed financing; its decision date must occur only after the required cycles can finish following the last material intervention. A capital handoff gate alone is not equilibrium.

Revalidate present instruments, terms, laws, costs, market conditions, and investor behavior from dated current evidence. Do not invent legal, tax, engineering, investment, fiduciary, or market conclusions.
