## Runtime scope

Prepare and run a time-bounded financing process, select aligned investors, and reason clearly about terms, dilution, valuation, control, and truth.

### Triggers

- Plan or diagnose a startup fundraising round after the capital model is chosen.
- Compare investors, financing instruments, terms, valuation, dilution, debt burden, control, or financing timing before company acceptance or closing.

### Non-triggers

- Decide whether the venture should use outside capital at all or choose its capital model and return shape.
- Diagnose a default-dead or sparse capital-heavy company without a completed survival/readiness handoff; run survival first even when a round seems credible.
- Design ordinary post-close management, board cadence, or executive accountability when no financing, amendment, waiver, consent, or investor-right decision is pending.

### Stage conditions

- Raise when the company has an appropriate milestone and enough process leverage; long-build and capital-heavy ventures may finance before product launch only after a survival/readiness handoff proves cash reaches the decision gates while preserving a floor.
- Advice changes across venture type, seed, inside, and later rounds, hot and weak markets, and instrument families; verify current instruments, existing-investor rights, terms, and investor conditions.

### Preserved tensions

- Speed and certainty versus investor quality and terms.
- Persuasive framing versus truthful calibration.
