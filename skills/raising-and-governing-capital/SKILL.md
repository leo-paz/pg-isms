# Raising and Governing Capital

Turn financing into a bounded company decision: what milestone merits capital, which counterparties and instruments fit, what economic and control package is acceptable, and what evidence permits each irreversible step.

## Route before optimizing

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md) and return its state-specific record in no more than 1,200 words.

Use this skill only after the company has chosen outside capital and its capital model or return shape. Route that prior choice to `designing-business-models`. Route default-dead or acute-cash states to `managing-runway-and-survival` before enabling bounded capital work. Sparse, pre-revenue, capital-heavy ventures also require its completed cash-floor/readiness handoff; capital becomes primary only when that handoff proves cash reaches dated technical and financing gates while protecting obligations and a no-raise option.

Route ordinary post-close board cadence, management systems, and executive accountability to `building-and-evolving-organizations`. Re-enter here for a new financing, amendment, waiver, consent, investor-right, disclosure, acceptance, or closing decision.

## Establish the decision record

Classify every input once as observed-at-input, claimed, or unknown. Give every consequential unknown an owner, artifact, date, observable threshold, and decision effect. Count unsigned documents, soft circles, diligence, nonbinding letters, and company-unaccepted offers as zero cash. Label arithmetic and market assumptions; verify current instruments, terms, law, tax, accounting, and investor conditions.

Define the milestone, ranged uses and timing, contingency and working capital, amount, existing-cash contribution and floor, economic/control envelope, no-deal or staged alternative, protected work, and falsifier before running a process. Batch qualified counterparties with real dates, consistent materials, access controls, reference checks, and a founder-time cap. Never invent scarcity, demand, certainty, or professional conclusions.

## Compare and commit safely

Normalize each applicable instrument: equity ownership and waterfalls; SAFE or convertible conversion effects; debt service, warrants, covenants, security, maturity, and default exposure; or revenue-share cash obligations. Compare downside, base, and upside consequences plus partner behavior, truth, control, and certainty.

Before seeing results, define hard gates, factor-specific score anchors, weights, aggregation, and tie behavior. An offer is selectable only when it passes every gate and is the unique anchored winner; a tie or unresolved evidence means counter, continue, or no-deal. Never hard-code a preferred offer.

Keep states separate: readiness permits company acceptance; signed-and-delivered acceptance proves acceptance; definitive documents, conditions, approvals, and funding readiness permit closing; effective documents plus cleared funds prove closing. Correct material errors everywhere before company acceptance.
