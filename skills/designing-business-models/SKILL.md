# Designing Business Models

## Route ownership first

- If default-dead/time-to-zero binds, use `managing-runway-and-survival` first; a term sheet is not cash.
- Before product value, `learning-from-users` owns need/sample/evidence/interpretation; this skill may frame, not select, actors/capture/price/package/sales/channels. After value, learning executes customer-evidence collection under this skill's economic plan.
- `acquiring-and-growing-users` owns channel execution/cohorts and marketplace cold-start/liquidity/beachhead acquisition; `shipping-and-iterating-products` owns billing/offer/rollout artifacts; `engineering-for-leverage` owns interfaces/factories/instrumentation.
- `raising-and-governing-capital` owns instruments, dilution, investor incentives, financing, control, governance, and negotiation. After venture shape/capital need settle, recheck only amount/absorption/return fit; keep payer/price/package/sales closed absent contrary evidence.
- `competing-and-positioning` owns differentiation/platform dependence; `governing-platforms-and-communities` owns participant policy; `communicating-clearly` adapts settled economics.
- `building-and-evolving-organizations` owns roles, hiring, incentives, hierarchy, management. `navigating-acquisitions-and-exits` owns approaches, offers, timing, transactions; business models owns return assumptions/capability boundaries.
- Use `making-calibrated-decisions` only for cross-domain framework, conflicting advice, source calibration, or no specialist owner; otherwise inherit its invariants here.

## Fill the Business-Model Record

Start with `Primary owner:` and ordered `Handoffs:` naming every applicable route above. Use rows per field/segment/path/boundary. Fill cells with facts or bounded assumptions; never group, omit, or use placeholders:

1. product type; company/product/survival state; binding constraint; latency; reversibility/error cost; facts to verify;
2. per segment: beneficiary, user, buyer, payer, channel; value event, alternative, budget, effects; actual use/payment/deployment/renewal/retention/expansion and confounders;
3. per segment: willingness-to-pay evidence, value metric, price/package, revenue timing, sales effort, implementation, support, compute/physical cost, gross margin, concentration, switching, and expansion; predeclare acceptance/rejection/discounting/churn interpretations;
4. if venture type is contested, durable and venture-product paths, each with reachable market, revenue, margin, growth, hiring, capital, dilution, evidence latency, downside, founder goals, and plausible exits;
5. for each boundary: customer/data/standard ownership, internal-versus-partner coordination/dependency cost, capability/fixed cost, partner maturity/replaceability, interface/switching path, and supplier-versus-architecture failure attribution;
6. one bounded offer, sales-motion, or boundary test: segment, price/range or action, owner, cost, exposure, guards, metric, confounders, review date, and numeric thresholds—adjectives do not count. Precommit payer branches `pay/revise/preserve-free/stop`; pricing branches `keep/change/segment/grandfather/stop`; or boundary branches `integrate/dual-source/partner/repair/stop`; and
7. selected branch, retention/effects gate, rejected alternatives, preserved option, switch condition, delay cost, capital/capability gate, and next owner per result.

## Apply the predicates

When product value exists but payer or willingness to pay is unknown, business models is primary: test capture before fine pricing; pause ads or scalable channels that assume a revenue model. Free use/praise/clicks/bids/investor interest are not payment. Predeclare payment, paid deployment, renewal, or equivalent commitment. With stable paid behavior, tune economics; growth executes channels.

Do not force venture scale. Test whether service is value or scaffolding. Set a bounded product/repeatability milestone and dated switch. Equity accelerates only after venture-return evidence; otherwise preserve the durable path.

Integrate only when reduced coordination/dependency/control/quality/customer-capture risk outweighs capital, delay, management, and capability cost. Otherwise qualify replaceable specialists, interfaces, alternate suppliers, and service terms. Calling something core is not evidence.

Copy every applicable [product and stage gate](references/runtime-contract.md) factor into the record/thresholds. Open [provenance](references/provenance.md) or [evidence](references/evidence.json) only for source questions.
