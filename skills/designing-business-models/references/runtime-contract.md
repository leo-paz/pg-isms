# Inherited runtime contract

Apply the applicable product row before choosing a payer, test, boundary, or capital model.

- **Survival:** If default-dead or time-to-zero binds, `managing-runway-and-survival` owns scenarios and a switch date first. A signed financing offer is not cash. Economic tests serve that plan.
- **Capital-light software:** Reversible payer tests, subscriptions, customer funding, or ramen profitability may fit after current hosting, labor, acquisition, assurance, and compliance costs are calculated.
- **Enterprise/integration:** Include procurement, security, switching, deployment, implementation, support, sales effort, and evidence latency. Compliments and undeployed pilots are not stable buyer economics.
- **Hardware:** Include inventory, tooling, fabrication, certification, reliability, working capital, physical risk, supplier qualification, and current component/channel economics.
- **Biotech, energy, capital-heavy:** Match financing to staged scientific, regulatory, safety, infrastructure, trial, partner, and buyer milestones; reject tiny-round, launch-first, and weekly-software defaults.
- **Marketplace/community:** Growth owns initial participant liquidity and beachhead acquisition. Model take rate, subsidy, concentration, and transaction economics. Governance owns ranking, reputation, moderation, abuse, trust, and interaction quality.
- **High-harm/regulated:** Require current law, permission, assurance, monitoring, rollback, human review, incident response, and accountable user effects before price or channel exposure.
- **Truth, power, and user effects:** Require truthful claims, non-deceptive conduct, accountable user effects, safe disagreement under power asymmetry, and character screening before granting durable trust or control.
