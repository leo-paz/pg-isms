## Runtime scope

Diagnose the company's survival state, extend runway, restore revenue or profitability, and sequence spending and hiring accordingly.

### Triggers

- Assess default-alive status, burn, runway, cash crisis, or survival options.
- Choose emergency spending, revenue, profitability, or hiring actions.

### Non-triggers

- Design a normal financing process for a healthy company.
- Optimize long-term pricing without a survival constraint.

### Stage conditions

- Trigger at default-dead, not only at acute time-to-zero: survival state overrides normal growth, hiring, and financing optimization.
- Require enough operating history for a default-alive extrapolation; otherwise use scenario ranges, a calendar runway plan, and a written plan B with a switch date.

### Preserved tensions

- Frugality versus underinvesting in a proven engine.
- Fundraising for rescue versus reaching default alive independently.
