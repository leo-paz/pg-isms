# Managing Runway and Survival

Classify the survival state from cash evidence, then preserve enough time and capability to reach a credible economic or product-type milestone. Static runway is a sensitivity, not proof of default alive.

## Route the binding state

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md) and return its filled record in no more than 1,200 words.

Make this skill primary when observed trends are default-dead, cleared cash and dated obligations bind, the history is too sparse to support a survival extrapolation, or spending and hiring must be tested against runway. Set assumed financing to zero until cash clears; treat pipeline, unsigned rounds, nonbinding letters, and receivables according to their actual certainty and timing.

Route a healthy default-alive company's round size, investor selection, dilution, governance, terms, and process to `raising-and-governing-capital`; return only the bounded runway handoff. Route payer, price, package, and normal monetization to `designing-business-models`; channel execution to `acquiring-and-growing-users`; role design and hiring execution to `building-and-evolving-organizations`. Survival sets the cash envelope and switch date before those workflows execute.

## Establish the state

Record each input once as `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`. Give every consequential unknown an owner, artifact, due date, threshold, and decision effect. Require sufficient, reconciled history spanning the relevant operating and collection cycle before calling a company default alive. If history is sparse, pre-revenue, structurally changing, or capital-heavy, use calendar scenarios and funded milestones instead.

Separate cash on hand, timed collections, outflow, net burn, revenue, margin, churn, obligations, and planned step-changes. Build downside, base, and upside cash consequences. Use a daily-to-weekly 13-week cash calendar for acute cash; use a dated milestone calendar for long-latency or regulated products. Match evidence cadence to when trustworthy results can appear.

## Act without indiscriminate austerity

Give every collection, revenue, cost, hiring, or bounded-spend action an owner, date, cash effect or cap, evidence artifact, safeguard, and review date. Protect essential customer, product, operational, data, safety, regulatory, and team capabilities. Pause state-inappropriate hiring; invest reversibly when a proven engine has an absorbable return and a preserved cash floor.

Close with the state-specific branch set from the runtime contract. Each branch needs a non-overlapping observable predicate, cash or time consequence, next action, owner, decision date, resulting route, and preserved option or handoff. Trigger current qualified finance, tax, employment, legal, insurance, insolvency, regulatory, or clinical owners before changing protected obligations or making irreversible decisions; do not invent professional conclusions.
