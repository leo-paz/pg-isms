# Making Calibrated Decisions

Turn mixed evidence into a dated, reversible-where-possible commitment without converting prestige, contrarianism, history, or one outcome into proof.

## Route before synthesizing

Use this skill only for the explicit cross-domain or source-calibration job in the description. If one specialist owns the concrete decision, route there and carry only the inherited guards: stage and survival state, reversibility and error cost, evidence latency, product type, truth and user effects, current-context revalidation, and optionality.

Route direction to `choosing-startup-opportunities`; demand evidence to `learning-from-users`; release to `shipping-and-iterating-products`; architecture to `engineering-for-leverage`; growth to `acquiring-and-growing-users`; pricing or capital model to `designing-business-models`; competition or platform position to `competing-and-positioning`; founder partnership to `forming-founder-partnerships`; organization to `building-and-evolving-organizations`; focus to `operating-with-focus-and-morale`; survival to `managing-runway-and-survival`; financing to `raising-and-governing-capital`; acquisition to `navigating-acquisitions-and-exits`; selection to `evaluating-startups-and-founders`; support to `advising-and-supporting-founders`; community governance to `governing-platforms-and-communities`; active-team location to `preparing-to-found`; and regional intervention to `building-startup-ecosystems`. Route open-ended discovery to `thinking-through-writing` and audience adaptation of settled claims to `communicating-clearly`.

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md). Select exactly one mode: route-away, source-calibration, or cross-domain decision. Return only that mode's record in no more than 1,200 words.

## Establish the record

In cross-domain decision mode, name the decision, owner, deadline, company stage, survival state, product type, evidence latency, irreversible downside, and current binding constraint. Classify every material proposition as observed, claimed, inferred, or unknown. Attach each consequential unknown to an owner, primary artifact or qualified source, due date, observable threshold, and decision effect.

State competing hypotheses and rival explanations. In source-calibration mode, stop after separating the durable mechanism from dated parameters, confidence, contrary evidence, and verification needs. Treat status, repetition, rejection, consensus, and nonconsensus as inputs rather than proof. Record a prediction only when its outcome and resolution date permit a fair credibility update.

## Choose the next commitment

Compare viable options on hard constraints, survivability, accountable user effects, expected upside and payoff shape, reversibility, preserved options, evidence cost, and cost of delay. Do not hide incompatible evidence behind an unanchored score or infer decision quality from outcome alone.

When evidence is weak and the next move is cheap and reversible, choose the smallest discriminating test whose result can arrive before the deadline. Precommit the threshold and falsifier. When exposure is irreversible or can harm safety, trust, control, legal standing, cash, or a community, require stronger evidence, qualified review, staged exposure, and a credible alternative.

In cross-domain decision mode, end with mutually exclusive `proceed`, `adapt`, `defer`, and `stop-or-route` branches. For each, state the predicate, owner, date, action, preserved option, switch condition, and specialist handoff. Hold the goal more firmly than the tactic; update the record when evidence resolves.
