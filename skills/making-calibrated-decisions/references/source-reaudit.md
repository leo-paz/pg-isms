## Boundaries and source limitations

- Specialist-owned decisions remain specialist-owned. This skill synthesizes explicit cross-domain records and calibrates sources; it does not become a generic evidence or strategy trigger.
- Article `025` is retained only as historical/source-context evidence. Its dated counterattack example is not operating guidance.
- Article `004` is collapsed into one source line in the corpus. Its canonical `10-10` range is mechanically valid but not granular; do not cite it as precise sentence-level support.
- Early-release and fast-feedback mechanisms apply only when errors are cheap and evidence can arrive quickly. Enterprise, hardware, biotech, regulated, and high-harm work require their own latency and review gates.
- Contrarian claims, prestige, rejection, consensus, and nonconsensus are hypotheses or bias signals, not proof.
- Revalidate current law, regulation, finance, tax, safety, instruments, markets, locations, platform policy, and technical feasibility with qualified sources before action.
- Power-law logic applies only when the payoff distribution and the actor's exposure justify it; a diversified selector and a concentrated founder do not share the same risk model.
