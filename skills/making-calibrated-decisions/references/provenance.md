## Runtime scope

Handle explicit cross-domain startup decisions, reconcile conflicting advice, and calibrate sources when no specialist workflow owns the whole problem.

### Triggers

- The user explicitly asks for a cross-domain decision framework or wants conflicting startup advice reconciled.
- Calibrate a source, prediction, historical claim, or consequential decision that has no domain-specific workflow owner.

### Non-triggers

- Perform a routine low-stakes task with clear requirements.
- Solve a specialist user, product, growth, hiring, runway, financing, or acquisition problem; inherit the global invariants inside that specialist instead.

### Stage conditions

- First identify company stage, survival state, product type, evidence latency, and reversibility.
- Historical essays provide hypotheses, not timeless operating facts; revalidate changed market assumptions.

### Preserved tensions

- Speed versus confidence.
- Contrarian independence versus ignoring accumulated evidence.
