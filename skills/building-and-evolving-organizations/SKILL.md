# Building and Evolving Organizations

Change the organization only for a current measured bottleneck or material risk. Preserve end-to-end ownership and a credible way back.

## Route the exact job

Run `managing-runway-and-survival` first when default-dead or time-to-zero binds. Route founder selection, status, trust, ownership intent, or departure to `forming-founder-partnerships`; settled employee and executive roles stay here. Route personal focus and burnout to `operating-with-focus-and-morale`. `learning-from-users` owns whether need exists, `shipping-and-iterating-products` owns exposure, `acquiring-and-growing-users` owns acquisition and retention, `designing-business-models` owns payer and company boundaries, `raising-and-governing-capital` owns financing and investor control, and `governing-platforms-and-communities` owns participant policy. Give acute harm, misconduct, employment, security, legal, and customer-continuity issues qualified independent owners immediately.

## Diagnose before changing structure

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md) and return its filled, dated organization record. Classify every material input as `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`. Apply stage, survival, product-type, reversibility, and high-harm gates.

Name the current outcome, baseline, owner, failure frequency, causal rivals, prior attempts, value of relief, and full cash, runway, ramp, coordination, ownership, culture, and succession costs. If no recurring constraint is observed, keep the shape flat and test the concern. Never add titles, layers, committees, or headcount for optics.

Select one primary branch: `keep-flat`, `role-or-hire`, `decision-rights`, `leader-role-evolution`, `incentive-or-culture`, `process-or-control`, or `route-away`. Add another only for a separate evidenced mechanism with its own owner and test.

## Preserve ownership and test the change

Keep one accountable end-to-end outcome owner. Define interfaces, escalation, backup, documentation, customer continuity, and succession. Add specialization or process only when bottleneck or harm relief exceeds handoff and delay costs.

Before a search, freeze outcomes, decisions, hands-on work, interfaces, initial span, evidence, and expansion threshold. Compare every candidate with the same job-relevant scorecard, structured work sample, interviews, and references; protect accessibility and audit bias. Record compensation intent without inventing terms. Route current employment, equity, tax, securities, background, title, and plan details to qualified HR and counsel.

Run the smallest bounded test with owners, dates, artifacts, baselines, safeguards, measures, and scenario-specific `keep`, `revise`, mechanism-appropriate `expand` or `add-capacity`, and `rollback-or-stop` thresholds. Use `expand` for a proven incentive, culture, process, or control; use `add-capacity` only for a sustained residual capacity gap. Close with the selected branch, rejected alternatives, unresolved unknowns, preserved option, switch condition, and next owner.

Use [references/provenance.md](references/provenance.md) when asserting a source-derived mechanism. Cite the essay URL and exact lines; label cross-source workflow design as synthesis and revalidate current facts. Open [references/evidence.json](references/evidence.json) only to audit the machine-readable claim mapping.
