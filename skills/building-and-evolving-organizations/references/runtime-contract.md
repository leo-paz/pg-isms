# Runtime contract: building and evolving organizations

Use this contract to produce a decision artifact, not generic management advice or a blank worksheet. Fill only fields material to the selected branch, but never omit evidence class, owner, date, measure, threshold, continuity, and next owner.

## Provenance boundary

The organization mechanisms in `provenance.md` are essay-supported. Route precedence, the evidence ledger, branch names, safeguards, test schema, and decision-record shape are cross-source and taxonomy synthesis. Do not present them as a Paul Graham recipe. Historical team sizes, grant math, salaries, laws, markets, offices, hours, location assumptions, or named-company practices require current verification.

## 1. Route and state gates

Apply in order:

1. If default-dead or time-to-zero binds, make `managing-runway-and-survival` primary. Organization work implements role, workload, communication, and continuity consequences only after the survival envelope is chosen.
2. Give fraud, harassment, retaliation, safety, security, privacy, money movement, evidence preservation, employment, legal, regulatory, or acute customer-continuity risk a qualified independent current owner. Do not relabel misconduct as culture.
3. Route unsettled founder selection, status, trust, commitment, ownership intent, departure, or continuation-affecting founder agreements to `forming-founder-partnerships`.
4. Continue here only when employee/executive hiring, authority, roles, management, coordination, incentives, internal culture, process, or scale is the unresolved job.
5. Otherwise select `route-away` and return the exact decision, exact canonical primary skill identifier, exact composed skill identifiers in execution order, evidence ledger, safeguards, handoff date, later organization trigger, preserved option, and switch condition.

Record company stage, survival state, product/release state, product type, team shape, change speed, evidence latency, reversibility, error cost, binding constraint, and current facts or laws requiring verification.

Product type changes the gate. Capital-light software may use short reversible tests when harm is bounded. Enterprise work includes procurement, implementation, security, switching, and support. Hardware includes fabrication, certification, reliability, inventory, and physical risk. Biotech, energy, and capital-heavy work use scientific, regulatory, infrastructure, partner, and specialist milestones. Marketplace/community work separates internal organization from participant liquidity and governance. High-harm work retains current qualified review, appropriate separation of duties, monitoring, human review, evidence preservation, incident response, and safe containment or rollback.

Startup speed never authorizes removing a material protection. Replace the protection before changing its control.

## 2. Evidence ledger

Classify each material input:

- `observed`: dated, attributable records or directly verified behavior. A scheduled assessment is observed-as-scheduled; its result remains unknown.
- `claimed`: interpretations, forecasts, promises, candidate plans, self-reports, investor advice, prestige signals, confidence, titles, and culture labels.
- `unknown`: unverified causes or future results, including hidden rework, sustainable load, leadership capacity, candidate stage fit, metric gaming, best decision boundary, and role evolution.

For every decision-relevant unknown, name evidence owner, method or artifact, due date, result threshold, and how each result changes the decision.

## 3. Current-bottleneck record

Fill:

1. Outcome at risk or opportunity delayed.
2. Dated baseline, evidence interval, frequency, severity, duration, and affected users or work.
3. Current owner, decision path, interfaces, and operating shape.
4. Proposed causal mechanism and at least one rival.
5. Attempts already made without a role, layer, committee, or broad reorganization.
6. Whether the work is current and essential or speculative.
7. Benefit of relief and evidence that would falsify the bottleneck.
8. Full cost: cash, runway, compensation, recruiting, ramp, management, communication, handoffs, interfaces, ownership dilution, culture, bias, incentives, continuity, succession, and reversibility.

If recurring delay, failure, overload, capability gap, or material risk exceeds the full cost of the smallest intervention, choose that mechanism. If the shape is healthy or the bottleneck is only claimed, keep it flat and test the concern. Calendar age, headcount, titles, investor optics, and large-company precedent are not bottlenecks.

## 4. Select one primary branch

### `keep-flat`

Use when ownership, latency, interfaces, continuity, and sustainable load show no recurring bottleneck or only an unverified concern exists. Preserve the current shape. Run the smallest monitoring or delegation test. State observable `keep-flat`, `change-process`, and `open-role` predicates.

### `role-or-hire`

Use when a capability or capacity gap remains after cheaper priority, ownership, tooling, process, or delegation options and relief exceeds full role cost. Before search, freeze a role charter with current outcomes, decisions, hands-on work, interfaces, initial span, 30/60/90-day evidence, cost, evolution, and expansion gate.

Compare all candidates against the same job-relevant scorecard, structured questions, bounded accessible work sample, targeted references, evidence definitions, and critical threshold. Collect independent written ratings before discussion. Select exactly one: `hire`, `change-scope`, `continue-search`, or `no-hire`.

Do not use protected traits, demographic proxies, pedigree, polish, charisma, shared worldview, communication speed, tool fashion, confidence, or willingness to accept low cash as ability or character proxies. Retain credentials when current licensure, regulated practice, safety, sparse direct evidence, or long evidence latency makes them relevant. Time-bound work samples, accommodate candidates, and pay work that creates company value. Have current HR/counsel own discrimination, accessibility, background, classification, and title effects; audit disagreement and pass rates for recreated prestige or style bias.

### `decision-rights`

Use for repeated queues, ambiguous authority, duplicate ownership, late commitments, or unsafe escalation. Per domain, name outcome owner, decision limits, consultation/review, notification, escalation trigger, destination and deadline, backup, succession, and audit artifact. Retain founder preapproval only for predeclared irreversible, strategic, fiduciary, safety, legal, or exceptional-risk decisions; review routine delegated outcomes instead.

### `leader-role-evolution`

Use when stage-required planning, hiring, coaching, information flow, external work, or succession exceeds a leader-centered shape. Define retained and delegated decisions, time allocation, product/customer contact, coaching, information flow, staged transfer, backup, succession, and rollback. Personal focus, morale, or health without a role or authority mechanism routes to `operating-with-focus-and-morale`.

### `incentive-or-culture`

Use when metrics, rewards, status, norms, or authority plausibly shape observed behavior and outcomes. Record the behavior/outcome baseline, incentive pressure, rivals, gaming, workload, invisible work, shared outcome, prospective change, continuity, and current HR/legal review. Culture is reinforced behavior, not a values label. Preserve respectful dissent, psychological safety, inclusion, and independent handling of misconduct.

State compensation intent only: outcome, horizon, cash/equity/variable categories, employee downside and liquidity, internal consistency, runway, and gaming risk. Do not invent terms. Current HR/employment counsel owns classification, wage/hour, pay equity, notice, consent, accommodations, titles, and prospective plan changes. Current tax/securities/equity counsel owns instruments, valuation, tax, vesting, exercise, dilution, documents, and cap-table effects. Historical equity formulas are assumption checks, not grant recommendations.

### `process-or-control`

Use when stable repeated work or material risk needs consistency, review, or memory, or an existing check costs more than its protection. Record the harm prevented; direct, delay, exclusion, opportunity, idea, retention, and ownership costs; smallest control; owner; response time; exception; escalation; expiry; and review. Standardize stable interfaces and high-consequence checks while keeping reversible exploratory work directly owned.

## 5. Preserve ownership, interfaces, and succession

For every affected unit, name customer/company outcome, one accountable owner, scope and decisions, interfaces and service expectations, dependencies and standards, information artifact, backup, absence coverage, succession, bus-factor trigger, and customer-continuity owner.

Specialization must preserve one end-to-end outcome owner. A specialist owns a coherent result or a bounded review/advisory interface; narrow functional ownership must not orphan the customer outcome.

## 6. Run the bounded change

Record hypothesis and rival; baseline; change and evidence owners; start, intermediate, and final dates; affected unit; artifacts; measures; customer, employee, safety, legal, and continuity safeguards; confounders; and preserved option. Set numeric or directly observable scenario-specific branches:

- `keep`: critical outcome, ownership, continuity, and harm thresholds pass at the current scope and trustworthy interval.
- `revise`: the mechanism is supported but one boundary, interface, measure, or workload assumption misses; change one variable and set one final date.
- `expand`: a bounded incentive, culture, process, or control test passes its outcome, continuity, fairness, and harm thresholds; extend only the proven mechanism to the next named scope with its own owner and review.
- `add-capacity`: after priority, decision, process, and interface causes are addressed, a sustained residual capacity gap still exceeds full role cost; open only the prewritten charter or add the smallest proven capacity.
- `rollback-or-stop`: a harm, legal, bias, continuity, accountability, or critical-outcome limit is crossed; restore the prior safe state, preserve evidence, and hand consequences to the qualified owner.

Use `expand` for mechanism rollout and `add-capacity` only for residual capability or capacity. For an incentive or process pilot, the required outcome set is `keep`, `revise`, `expand`, and `rollback-or-stop`; do not substitute hiring for successful rollout.

“Better,” “faster,” and “more aligned” are not thresholds. Match review cadence to the shortest interval in which trustworthy evidence can appear.

## 7. Return the filled organization decision record

1. Header: date, exact decision, horizon, primary route and branch, active routes, accountable owners, intermediate and final reviews.
2. State and gates: company/product/survival state, product type, evidence latency, reversibility, error cost, current-review gates, and protected outcomes.
3. Evidence ledger: observed, claimed, and unknown rows with evidence owners, methods, dates, thresholds, and decision effects.
4. Current bottleneck: baseline, mechanism, rival, prior attempts, relief, full cost, falsifier, and conclusion.
5. Selected change: selected and rejected branches, ownership, decisions, interfaces, escalation, succession, continuity, and any role, hiring, or compensation module.
6. Test and decision: artifacts, measures, safeguards, `keep`/`revise`/mechanism-appropriate `expand` or `add-capacity`/`rollback-or-stop` thresholds, decision now, unresolved unknowns, preserved option, switch condition, and next owner per result.
7. Lineage: source-derived mechanisms with essay URL and exact lines; cross-source/taxonomy synthesis; current external facts and qualified reviewers.
