## Runtime scope

Hire for the current bottleneck, preserve small-team ownership and culture, and evolve roles and coordination only when scale requires it.

### Triggers

- Plan startup hiring, roles, incentives, management, or organizational scaling.
- Diagnose coordination overhead or culture erosion.

### Non-triggers

- Select an initial cofounder.
- Make an emergency runway reduction without an organizational design question.

### Stage conditions

- Do not import large-company structure before a real coordination bottleneck exists.
- Founder roles should evolve as the company shifts from product work to deals, hiring, and organization.

### Preserved tensions

- Specialization versus end-to-end ownership.
- Process consistency versus small-team speed.
