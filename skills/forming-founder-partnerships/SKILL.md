# Forming Founder Partnerships

Base irreversible founder decisions on observed shared work, explicit assumptions, and preserved options.

## Route the unresolved job

Keep this skill primary only for a founder-to-founder formation, status, commitment, ownership-intent, trust, repair, departure, or role agreement affecting continuation, trust, or commitment. Compose all active routes in execution order and give each unresolved job one primary owner.

- Route company default-dead state or time-to-zero to `managing-runway-and-survival`.
- Route personal readiness, life timing, or founder runway to `preparing-to-found`.
- Route whether the opportunity deserves commitment to `choosing-startup-opportunities`.
- Route user-demand evidence to `learning-from-users`.
- Route employee or executive hiring, hierarchy, and scaled role design to `building-and-evolving-organizations` only when the founder relationship is settled.
- Route financing, dilution, investor control, and terms to `raising-and-governing-capital`.
- Route general founder or application assessment to `evaluating-startups-and-founders`.
- Give safety, evidence preservation, and customer continuity immediate domain owners alongside partnership work. Give qualified current counsel ownership documents, legal effect, actual terms, IP, prior obligations, vesting, tax, employment, fiduciary duties, and separation terms; this skill retains ownership intent and business inputs.

Do not stigmatize a solo founder or select by protected traits, prestige, founder count, or historical rates. Cover missing challenge, capability, and morale functions without forcing a weak partnership.

## Execute the contract

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md) and return its filled, dated output—not a worksheet. If another route is primary, return only its filled route-away handoff; do not force a candidate, existing-team, ownership, or trial branch. If partnership is primary, select candidate or existing-team, and add ownership only when ownership is at issue.

Classify every material fact, account, commitment, pending result, and uncertainty as `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`; apply the trust gate; map commitment; select one supported branch; and give every action an owner, date, preserved option, and switch condition.

Use a bounded work trial when consequential shared-work evidence is missing. Choose a real outcome with user-facing and unpleasant work, dependencies, joint decisions, artifacts, an IP boundary, observable thresholds, and a review date. Preserve reversibility and never manufacture conflict.

Treat a concern as something to investigate, not a verdict. Treat observed deception, exploitation, retaliation, concealed material obligations, or asset misuse as a breach—not role drift. An independent owner decides whether confrontation is safe; do not prescribe reconciliation without truth, safety, and good-faith participation.

## Decide without formulas

Compare commitment, risk, responsibility, contribution, constraints, and horizon as `symmetric`, `materially different`, or `insufficient`. Record ownership intent and inputs; do not calculate a split or promise a universal percentage. Counsel owns documents, legal effect, and actual terms.

Use [references/provenance.md](references/provenance.md) when source lineage, exact ranges, or historical limits matter.
