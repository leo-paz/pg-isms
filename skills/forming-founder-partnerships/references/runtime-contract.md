# Founder partnership runtime contract

Return one filled, dated output, not a blank template. Label assumed dates. Base each selected branch on scenario evidence, and give every action an owner, date, preserved option, and observable switch condition.

## Synthesis boundary

The essays support choosing through shared work, maintaining founder relationships, surfacing commitment and departure risk, and stopping on trust warnings. The decision schema, trial fields and thresholds, trust-versus-role diagnostic, ownership categories, and repair, exit, and separation branches below are cross-source or taxonomy synthesis. The essays supply no fixed recipe, ownership split, or current legal terms.

## Compose routes before partnership analysis

List all active routes in execution order. Assign exactly one primary owner to each unresolved job, and choose one primary route for the exact decision.

- `managing-runway-and-survival`: company default-dead state or company time-to-zero only.
- `preparing-to-found`: personal readiness, life timing, personal founder runway, or whether to found.
- `choosing-startup-opportunities`: whether the opportunity merits commitment.
- `learning-from-users`: user-demand evidence.
- `building-and-evolving-organizations`: employee or executive hiring, hierarchy, or scaled role design, but only when the founder relationship is settled.
- `raising-and-governing-capital`: financing, dilution, investor terms, or investor control.
- `evaluating-startups-and-founders`: general founder or application selection.
- `partnership-primary`: founder selection or status, commitment, ownership intent, departure, founder-to-founder trust or repair, or a founder-to-founder role agreement affecting continuation, trust, or commitment.

Safety, evidence preservation, access control, and customer continuity receive immediate independent, security, operations, legal, or customer owners alongside partnership work. They do not become survival-route ownership unless company default-dead state or time-to-zero also binds.

Qualified current counsel owns ownership documents, legal effect, actual terms, IP, prior obligations, vesting, tax, employment, fiduciary duties, and separation terms. The partnership workflow retains ownership intent, factual inputs, and business questions.

### Route-away handoff

When `partnership-primary` is not the primary route, return only the filled route-away handoff. Do not fill candidate, existing-team, ownership, or work-trial branches.

The handoff must state: exact decision; primary route and primary decision owner; all active routes in execution order; every material past fact, person's account, future commitment, pending result, and uncertainty classified as `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`; evidence owners; process owner; decision and review dates; preserved option; observable switch condition; and HR or counsel questions where relevant. A scheduled assessment's existence and date are `observed` (`observed-as-scheduled`); its result remains `unknown` until the dated assessment is completed. Reopen the founder-partnership issue only if founder status, control, departure, trust, commitment, ownership intent, or a founder-to-founder role agreement affecting continuation becomes genuinely unsettled.

## Fill a partnership-primary record

Select exactly one primary mode: `candidate` or `existing-team`. Add an ownership branch only when ownership intent or inputs are at issue. Add a work trial only when consequential shared-work evidence is missing and a trial is safe and relevant.

1. **Decision:** Exact question, decision date, horizon, company stage, company survival state, active routes in order, and one primary owner per unresolved job.
2. **People and status:** Members, relationship history, founder status, existing commitments, constraints, and why candidate formation or existing-team continuation is the primary mode.
3. **Evidence ledger:** Include every material past fact, each person's account or interpretation, every future commitment, each pending result, and each uncertainty. Mark each `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`; date observations and name an evidence owner and due date for each important unknown. A scheduled assessment's existence and date are `observed` (`observed-as-scheduled`); its result is `unknown` until completed on the recorded date. Include character, follow-through, determined adaptability, competence, candor, user-facing and unpleasant work, joint decisions, conflict behavior, and respect when material. Friendship, liking, credentials, and complementarity are inputs, not proof of trust.
4. **Trust gate:** Persistent misgivings; inconsistent accounts; deception; exploitation; retaliation; concealed material obligations; asset misuse; evidence status; independent safety owner; current safeguards; and safeguard-expansion triggers.
5. **Commitment map:** Each founder's time, duration, outside work, personal constraints, cash needs, risk, responsibilities, opportunity belief, motivation, and exit expectations. Do not turn caregiving, health, immigration, or another real constraint into a character judgment.
6. **Optional work trial:** If selected, fill the complete trial contract below. Otherwise cite the shared-work evidence or reason it is irrelevant or unsafe.
7. **Operating intent:** Roles now, decision domains, founder-to-founder role agreement, escalation, information access, check-in cadence, compensation and runway assumptions, ownership intent and inputs, expected evolution, departure continuity, and questions for counsel.
8. **Outcome:** The supported mode branch, optional ownership branch, decisive observed evidence, unresolved unknowns, owner and date, preserved option, review date, and exact switch condition.
9. **Provenance:** Article number, URL, exact lines, and mechanism for material source-derived claims. Label the synthesis named above as synthesis.

## Apply the trust gate

Classify a misgiving or inconsistent account without verified harmful conduct as `concern-to-investigate`. Define a narrow evidence check, its owner and date, and the reversible option being preserved. Do not convert intuition into a character verdict.

Classify verified deception, exploitation, retaliation, concealed material obligations, or asset misuse as `observed-breach`. An independent board member, committee, or qualified counsel—not an implicated founder—decides whether and when confrontation is safe. Prohibit confrontation while it could compromise evidence, assets, personal safety, access control, or customer continuity.

First preserve evidence, assets, access, safety, customer continuity, and non-retaliation; then route governance and legal consequences to independent current review. Expand safeguards when evidence suggests broader scope, evidence loss or tampering, retaliation, unusual access or transfers, new affected people or customers, a continuity dependency, noncooperation, or an independent reviewer or counsel directs expansion. Do not trade trust for scarce capability or choose repair without truth, safety, and good-faith participation.

## Run an optional reversible work trial

Fill: real outcome; duration; availability; owners; user-facing task; unpleasant task; joint decisions; dependencies; artifacts; confidentiality and current IP boundary; review date; and scenario-specific `pass`, `change`, `extend`, and `stop` thresholds.

- `pass`: predeclared critical behaviors and outcome evidence are observed.
- `change`: trust remains intact, but evidence supports a different scope, role, or dependency plan.
- `extend`: a predeclared dependency prevented a fair test; name the new evidence and one final date.
- `stop`: an observed breach, material mismatch, refusal of reversible terms, or failure of a critical threshold occurs.

Do not manufacture conflict. Use real uncertainty, dependencies, user contact, difficult work, and joint decisions. Before work begins, have current counsel resolve material employment, confidentiality, and IP uncertainty. Do not promise permanent title, stock, or company-IP transfer before the gate.

## Select one candidate branch

- `reject-now`: observed breach, known incompatibility, or refusal of a safe reversible process; preserve solo operation or search.
- `run-work-trial`: consequential shared-work evidence is missing and no breach blocks a trial.
- `extend-trial`: a specified dependency left a material unknown; extend once with a bounded target.
- `formalize`: observed evidence clears thresholds, material unknowns are acceptable, commitments align, and counsel can document current terms.

When no trustworthy candidate exists, `solo-and-search` preserves owned customer work while reversible advisers, contractors, or employees cover missing challenge, capability, or morale. Never add a cofounder for optics, fundraising pressure, or founder-count convention.

## Select one exclusive existing-team branch

Apply these predicates in order so exactly one branch is primary:

1. `trust-breach-separation`: an observed breach requires containment, continuity, independent review, and counsel-governed separation.
2. `planned-founder-exit`: reset minimums are refused, unavailable, or fail at the dated gate; protect operations and send terms to counsel.
3. `commitment-reset`: time, risk, or responsibility has materially changed; set prospective commitments, coverage, and a gate. If resentment or stalled decisions also exist, attach a relationship-repair subplan without changing the primary branch.
4. `relationship-repair`: commitments are aligned, truth and safety are intact, and a repairable interaction pattern is the main issue; name reciprocal actions, evidence owner, and failure condition.
5. `ordinary-role-reset`: commitments are aligned and the main issue is changed responsibilities, capability, or company needs; update responsibilities and decision domains.

Role mismatch is not automatically breach, and breach is not cured by relabeling it role mismatch.

## Add ownership input only when ownership is at issue

Compare time, duration, cash and opportunity risk, responsibilities, critical contributions, outside work, constraints, and exit expectations. Select `symmetric-inputs`, `materially-different-inputs`, or `insufficient-inputs`, and explain the evidence. Symmetry does not imply a percentage.

The partnership record owns ownership intent and inputs. Every ownership branch ends in `counsel-review`; qualified current counsel owns documents, legal effect, and actual terms. Do not calculate a split, reclaim ownership unilaterally, equate compensation with earned ownership, or provide a universal percentage.

## Close with accountability

Return only supported branches. State selected outcome, observed evidence, accountable owners, decision and review dates, preserved option, unresolved unknowns, and exact switch condition. State alternative predicates only to show what new evidence would change the decision.
