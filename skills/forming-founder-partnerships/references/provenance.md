## Runtime scope

Find and test cofounder fit, make ownership choices, and establish a durable working relationship before conflicts become existential.

### Triggers

- Choose, evaluate, or trial a potential cofounder.
- Resolve founder ownership, commitment, trust, or relationship concerns.

### Non-triggers

- Design a later-stage management hierarchy.
- Hire an individual contributor for a defined role.

### Stage conditions

- Test relationships through demanding shared work before making irreversible commitments.
- Revisit roles as evidence changes, while treating trust failures as different from ordinary role evolution.

### Preserved tensions

- Complementary skills versus proven trust.
- Equal partnership versus contribution and commitment differences.
