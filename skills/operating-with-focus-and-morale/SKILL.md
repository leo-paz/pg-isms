# Operating with Focus and Morale

Choose the company-changing constraint, then run one evidence loop protecting judgment, users, and sustainable execution.

## Route the actual state first

Read [references/runtime-contract.md](references/runtime-contract.md) and return its compact, filled, dated record without blank or merged fields. Route default-dead, payroll-threatening, or fatal-pinch states first to `managing-runway-and-survival`. Route a live financing process with dated investor decisions first to `raising-and-governing-capital`, and a live buyer process to `navigating-acquisitions-and-exits`. Route persistent unclear rights, unowned decisions, or role redesign first to `building-and-evolving-organizations`; keep a short approval queue caused by acute overload here when rights and owners are already clear. Use this skill as supporting cadence after another primary constraint has an owner and bound.

Keep acute health, safety, security, legal, fiduciary, misconduct, and continuity risks with qualified owners. Do not relabel them as focus or morale problems.

## Establish what is true

Inventory every decision-relevant prompt fact once as `observed`, `claimed`, or `unknown`; do not summarize away a stakeholder interpretation, constraint, deadline, or continuity fact. A crowded calendar, low morale, competitor move, visible effort, prestige, and confidence may be context; none proves demand, priority, or seriousness. Give every consequential unknown its own owner, artifact, date, threshold, and decision effect.

Select one outcome and one current tactic that can change the company's state within the shortest trustworthy evidence interval. Give the plan a dated baseline, target, affected users or work, owner, mechanism, rival, latency, and falsifier. Give every named competing initiative a `cancel`, `delegate`, `defer`, or `bound` disposition while preserving essential obligations.

## Protect execution and morale

Protect recurring contiguous maker time and direct user contact. Bound meetings and availability. State a weekly limit, recovery, bounded exceptional push, primary and backup coverage for each essential continuity class, and an escalation destination plus response window. Never invent missing coverage; control it as an unknown. Never use nights, continuous availability, public pressure, or raw hours as determination proxies.

Attach morale to controllable progress, credible user evidence, ownership, supportive peers, realistic duration, recovery, and health. Keep a valid outcome steadier than its tactics. Change tactics when evidence misses; reopen the outcome itself only when repeated discriminating evidence undermines its premise. Do not confuse a hard phase with invalidation or persistence with denial.

Close with separate `continue`, `adapt`, `reopen-goal`, and `stop` rows. Every row needs its own observable threshold, next action, owner, and date. For a route-away state, use that route's complete branch set and add an explicit pause or end path plus a dated handoff back here. Each check records result, confounder, safeguard status, and branch.
