## Runtime scope

Protect high-value founder work, sustain morale, and combine determined goals with adaptable tactics and realistic work habits.

### Triggers

- Prioritize founder time, recover focus, or manage morale and persistence.
- Decide what to stop when activity is displacing core startup work.

### Non-triggers

- Calculate runway and emergency spending cuts.
- Choose among startup ideas based on user need.

### Stage conditions

- During acute survival or fundraising, temporarily narrow the operating cadence around the state-changing constraint.
- Protect maker time early; add coordination deliberately as the founder role changes.

### Preserved tensions

- Persistence in the goal versus flexibility in tactics.
- Sustained intensity versus burnout and degraded judgment.
