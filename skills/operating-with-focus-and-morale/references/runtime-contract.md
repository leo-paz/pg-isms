# Runtime contract: operating with focus and morale

Produce a filled decision artifact, not generic productivity advice, a morale speech, or a blank worksheet. Keep the answer proportional and compact. Never merge required fields or leave them implicit: an unfilled owner, artifact, date, threshold, action, backup, or decision effect is a missing control, even when the prose sounds sensible. Never turn a nearby role into unsupplied backup coverage. If a required backup is not supplied, record it as an unknown with owner, artifact, date, threshold, and decision effect.

## Provenance boundary

The mechanisms in `provenance.md` are essay-supported. Route precedence, evidence classes, branch names, record structure, safety gates, and combined decision thresholds are cross-source and taxonomy synthesis; do not present them as a Paul Graham recipe. Revalidate current medical, employment, legal, fiduciary, financing, security, safety, market, and product facts.

## 1. Route and bound the state

Apply in order:

1. If cash math makes the company default-dead, threatens near payroll, or creates a fatal pinch, make `managing-runway-and-survival` primary. Choose the survival envelope before ordinary calendar or morale work. This skill may protect humane workload and essential continuity inside that envelope.
2. If an active fundraising process has current diligence, term, reference, investor-selection, negotiation, or closing decisions with dates, make `raising-and-governing-capital` primary. If a live buyer process or sale decision binds, use `navigating-acquisitions-and-exits`. Bound the external-decision sprint, insulate product and users, and define its close, pause, or stop condition before returning here. Optional investor, media, or conference activity without a live dated decision remains an attention-allocation question.
3. Make `building-and-evolving-organizations` primary only when an observed recurring queue is caused by unclear or structurally wrong rights, duplicate or absent ownership, interfaces, role design, or a leader-centered shape. A recent approval wait or missed commitment during a bounded overload spike does not prove an organization mechanism. When rights, owners, escalation, and backups are clear and the wait began with acute overload or degraded judgment, keep this skill primary, remove load, install temporary backup coverage, and measure recovery. Switch to organization work if the queue persists past that dated relief test or reveals an authority or interface defect. Reserve focus blocks only from capacity actually released.
4. Give acute health, self-harm, safety, misconduct, retaliation, security, privacy, legal, fiduciary, evidence-preservation, or customer-continuity risk a qualified current owner and escalation path.
5. Continue here when attention allocation, competing work, degraded judgment, sustainable effort, morale, or persistence-versus-adaptation is the unresolved company constraint. Otherwise choose `route-away`; name the exact primary and supporting skill identifiers, its immediate state-changing priority, continuity and workload safeguards, owner, checkpoint, and observable dated handoff condition back here.

Record the date, company and product stage, runway or survival state, financing or buyer state, organization state, current constraint, evidence latency, reversibility, error cost, essential obligations, and any current facts requiring verification. For an approval queue, include age and frequency under normal load, explicit rights, owners and backups, onset relative to overload, and a dated persistence threshold. Explicitly state the selected primary route and the concrete facts that exclude each plausible adjacent primary route; do not merely name the winner.

When another workflow is primary, return only a filled **Route-away handoff**; do not fill ordinary focus-priority, morale, or persistence branches. Include: exact decision and canonical primary and supporting skills in execution order; why focus is not primary; the complete evidence ledger; the primary route's immediate decision; dispositions for every named alternative or initiative; continuity and workload safeguards; and the route-outcome matrix defined below.

## 2. Build the evidence ledger

Classify every decision-relevant input:

- `observed`: dated records or directly verified behavior and outcomes. Examples include hours, calendar categories, user contacts, defects, adoption, rollbacks, decision waits, cash, signed terms, and scheduled deadlines.
- `claimed`: interpretations, forecasts, motives, confidence, prestige benefits, competitor narratives, promises, and statements that all commitments matter or more hours prove seriousness.
- `unknown`: unverified causes and future effects, including customer impact, task value, repeatability, workflow friction, minimum viable event value, decision capacity, tactic causality, investor fit, and whether the goal premise still holds.

For each consequential unknown, create a separate row with evidence owner, method or artifact, due date, result threshold, and how the result changes the priority, tactic, or goal. Preserve each material stakeholder interpretation in `claimed`, even when it is weak or contradicted. Do not promote a claim to a fact by repetition.

## 3. Choose one state-changing priority

Write a single outcome and select one current tactic or evidence plan, not an activity list or a continuing comparison among candidates. It must address the current constraint and be testable within the shortest interval in which trustworthy evidence can appear. Record:

1. Outcome and why it can change the company state now.
2. Dated baseline, target, horizon, accountable owner, and affected users or work.
3. Proposed causal mechanism and at least one rival.
4. Evidence latency and the earliest honest checkpoint.
5. Falsifier and the condition that ends the cycle.
6. Essential obligations that remain protected.
7. A named `cancel`, `delegate`, `defer`, or tightly `bound` disposition, owner, and effective date for every material competitor named in the input.

Prefer product and user outcomes during normal early operation. Do not choose an event, redesign, visibility campaign, meeting reduction, productivity tool, generic morale reset, or total hours unless evidence makes it the state-changing mechanism. One priority can contain the minimum linked user contact and build work needed to resolve the same uncertainty; it is not permission for parallel initiatives.

Increase review and safeguards with safety, security, regulation, money movement, customer harm, or irreversibility. Hardware, scientific, regulated, and slow systems may require longer evidence intervals than ordinary software; do not manufacture a short deadline that cannot reveal the result.

For long-latency work, state both an intermediate decision date and the final evidence date. If a trial has not started, give a dated start-by deadline and a conservative latest review date; never write only "after the trial completes."

## 4. Protect the execution environment

Create a calendar and coverage envelope around the priority:

- recurring contiguous maker or analysis blocks;
- recurring direct user contact and a named user-continuity owner;
- bounded meetings, media, events, investor activity, internal status work, notifications, and availability;
- a work mix matched to the founder's actual role, replacing a maker-heavy calendar when management, hiring, or coordination has become the current job;
- named essential product, support, incident, payroll, legal, fiduciary, and teammate obligations;
- primary and backup coverage for every essential continuity class, including user or customer continuity, incident response, and decision authority when relevant;
- escalation destination, concrete response window, and handoff artifact;
- a sustainable weekly workload boundary, recovery periods, and an end date for any exceptional push.

Do not stack protected blocks on top of unchanged approval load. Delegate or remove work and establish backup coverage first. Deep work never overrides timely user, teammate, incident, safety, legal, or customer-continuity coordination.

When customer evidence informs the decision, reserve recurring direct-user evidence for the accountable decision owner; delegated continuity ownership is not a substitute. If direct contact is impossible, state why and name the proxy, artifact, and cadence.

If sustained hours, sleep loss, reversals, rollbacks, skipped users, approval waits, conflict, errors, or health symptoms indicate degraded judgment, establish recovery and decision backup immediately. Check acute risk within days. Do not prescribe compulsory nights, permanent availability, humiliation, coerced hours, or public accountability. Route medical or acute mental-health concerns to qualified help.

## 5. Diagnose morale without turning it into a verdict

Treat morale as an operating signal. Record the observed behaviors or outcomes behind the label and test plausible mechanisms:

- stalled or invisible progress;
- weak or delayed user evidence;
- low ownership or blocked agency;
- isolation, conflict, or lack of supportive peers;
- an implausible duration or unclear finish line;
- workload, recovery, or health degradation;
- a tactic repeatedly failing while the goal remains untested;
- credible evidence that the goal premise is wrong.

Restore controllable progress, real user contact, clear ownership, a trusted peer or advice channel, honest duration, recovery, and health protections. Keep accountability private and useful rather than humiliating or performative. A competitor announcement, a difficult month, low confidence, or a single miss is not proof the market does not care. Equally, mission, effort, or prior investment is not proof the goal remains valid.

## 6. Separate persistence from denial

Write the goal premise and current tactic separately.

- `continue`: the priority produces the target signal within the trustworthy interval and safeguards hold.
- `adapt`: the goal premise remains plausible, but the tactic, segment, workflow, scope, or channel misses a precommitted threshold. Change one material variable and set a new bounded test.
- `reopen-goal`: repeated discriminating tests across plausible tactics or segments undermine the premise, or survival, capability, harm, or opportunity-cost evidence changes the objective. State what evidence is still missing and who decides.
- `stop`: the premise is invalidated, a hard safety/legal/survival boundary is crossed, or the preserved option dominates under the precommitted rule.

Give every branch its own observable threshold, next action, accountable owner, and calendar date; never share a single owner or review date implicitly across rows. Record the selected tactic now and an honest final decision date now, even when the result arrives later. If another workflow is primary, replace these four branches with that workflow's complete branch set, including explicit proceed or continue, change or counter, pause, and end paths as applicable. Add columns for the primary route after each branch and its handoff state. A proceed or continue branch keeps the specialist route primary under a new bounded phase. A pause, stop, decline, end, or actual close hands cadence back here only when the live specialist decision is closed and continuity and workload safeguards are stable. Never write only "handoff after close"; give the dated predicate and next owner.

Do not persist indefinitely with an unchanged tactic. Do not abandon a valid user outcome merely because morale is low or a competitor appears. Record how many attempts or what evidence suffices before reopening the goal; avoid moving the threshold after seeing results.

## 7. Run the owned cadence

For the priority, attention allocation, user contact, company outcome, and workload health, record an owner, dated baseline, target or limit, evidence source, and review date. Include leading and outcome evidence without using hours as a determination score.

Set intermediate checks at the shortest honest evidence interval and a final decision date. Give the cadence an explicit checkpoint-content row: every named check records observed result, confounder or rival status, safeguard status, and the precommitted branch. Use daily or within-days checks only for acute risk, recovery, incidents, or near deadlines; use weekly checks when evidence supports them. Match longer product cycles to their real latency.

Any temporary crisis or financing cadence needs a named end, availability bounds, backup coverage, minimum product/user motion, and an explicit handoff. Close the state before declaring an ordinary focus reset.
