This skill's owned essay evidence is cataloged in `provenance.md`. It also inherits repository-wide rules that are intentionally synthesized once rather than reassigned to every skill.

- Source of inheritance: `research/taxonomy-source.json:3` states that every runtime skill inherits the global invariants.
- Evidence latency and product selection: `research/taxonomy-source.json:27-40` requires the shortest trustworthy evidence interval and selection of the applicable product-type row before reusing advice across categories.
- Historical revalidation and reversibility: `research/taxonomy-source.json:18-24,53-66` raises gates with error cost, rechecks dated parameters, and preserves options until evidence supports commitment.
- Product-type matrix: `research/taxonomy-source.json:69-123` defines the software, enterprise, hardware, biotech/energy, marketplace/community, and high-harm rows used by `SKILL.md`.
- Learning-to-growth gate: `research/taxonomy-source.json:127-130` keeps the question and interpretation with learning until one segment demonstrates real use, payment, or a predefined equivalent.

The latency mechanism also appears directly in essay `213`, [What I've Learned from Users](https://paulgraham.com/users.html), line `78`: the useful decision interval depends on how long the problem takes to produce results, ranging from days to months. The matrix details remain canonical synthesis, not claims that every product type is discussed in essay `213`.
