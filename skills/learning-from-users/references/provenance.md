## Runtime scope

Identify a narrow user, observe real behavior, validate demand, and turn direct service and feedback into trustworthy product learning.

### Triggers

- Plan user discovery, demand validation, interviews, or support-driven learning.
- Choose an initial niche and evidence that would confirm a real need.

### Non-triggers

- Optimize a proven acquisition channel at scale.
- Implement a product feature from an already validated specification.

### Stage conditions

- Early on, user learning owns the hypothesis, sample, evidence standard, and interpretation; growth may execute recruitment or beachhead seeding only as a subordinate step in that plan.
- For products with long evidence latency, match the test interval to when credible behavior can appear.

### Preserved tensions

- Listening to requests versus inferring the underlying problem.
- A narrow delighted niche versus superficial approval from a broad audience.
