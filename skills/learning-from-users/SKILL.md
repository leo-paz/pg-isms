# Learning from Users

Own the question, sample, threshold, and interpretation while need remains uncertain.

## Route by ownership

- Route direction generation, comparison, or pivots to `choosing-startup-opportunities`.
- Route prototypes or validated specifications to `shipping-and-iterating-products`; learning states the uncertainty.
- Test willingness to pay here; route pricing to `designing-business-models`.
- Compose with `advising-and-supporting-founders` when an existing founder's bottleneck is uncertain.
- Hand settled findings to `communicating-clearly` without upgrading weak evidence.

Before validation, growth may recruit the sample; no channel optimization. Learning owns hypothesis, threshold, and interpretation. After repeated use, payment, or a predefined equivalent, growth owns execution. Route unresolved expansion to opportunity choice.

## Write the Learning Record before recruiting

Return a filled record, never a template. Use scenario facts; choose and label assumed calendar dates. Record:

1. product type, stage, target segment, and sample rationale;
2. one specific actor-workflow-consequence claim, causal rivals such as visibility, authority, notification, or process delay, and disconfirming evidence;
3. behavior and requests already observed, including contradictions;
4. recruitment owner, inclusion rule, consent, risks, and exclusions;
5. test, comparison, evidence threshold, calendar review date(s), and stop rule; and
6. next owner for each result: continue learning, build through shipping, validated to growth, or failed back to opportunity choice.

Avoid convenience, enthusiasm, or access-only samples.

## Seek behavior, not approval

Ask about recent events, sequence, workaround, consequence, and who acts or pays. Observe work where safe. Do not pitch, lead witnesses, or treat feature requests as diagnoses.

Evidence strengthens from interest to costly behavior: workflow or data access, repeated use, deployment, switching, a signed commitment, or payment. Praise, signups, clicks, subsidized activity, and undeployed pilots do not independently validate demand.

Use manual service, support, or a concierge workflow to expose hidden steps. Log repetitions; do not let custom work silently become the product or consume survival runway.

If requests conflict with behavior, investigate the underlying job, context, authority, workaround, and consequence before implementation. If they agree, test the clearest falsifiable claim; agreement is not validation.

## Match evidence to product and latency

- Software: reversible use over days or weeks.
- Enterprise: workflow access, deployment, procurement, switching, and integration.
- Hardware: built units and observed physical use over weeks or months.
- Biotech or energy: scientific, regulatory, partner, and outcome milestones over months or years.
- Marketplaces: density before interpreting liquidity or retention.
- High-harm products: separate performance, workflow, and safety hypotheses, plus procurement when applicable; sandbox, seek expert review, run bounded monitored pilots with rollback.

Use the shortest trustworthy cadence; revalidate dated assumptions and keep claims proportional.

## Close the loop

Prefer a narrow segment with repeated use or payment over praise. Record observations, confounders, harms, and the decision. If the threshold passes, hand growth the segment and evidence. If not, continue the specified test or return preserved learning to opportunity choice; never pivot silently.
