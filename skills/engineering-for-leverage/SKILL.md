# Engineering for Leverage

Own internal structure—not need, exposure, policy, staffing, or commerce.

If default-dead or acute survival binds, address it before architecture.

## Route by decision

- `learning-from-users` owns need uncertainty, sampling, and interpretation.
- `shipping-and-iterating-products` owns artifact/exposure; existing owner keeps routine code, tests, and technical rollback. Invoke only for a binding structural constraint, before shipping.
- After one target segment's real use, payment, or equally discriminating predeclared commitment, `acquiring-and-growing-users` owns channels, activation, retention, liquidity, and expansion; only technical bottlenecks return before shipping exposure.
- `governing-platforms-and-communities` owns participant effects, recourse, transparency, and ranking/moderation policy; engineering owns implementation/evaluation/adversarial machinery; shipping owns exposure.
- `building-and-evolving-organizations` owns staffing, team topology, reporting, incentives, and process; engineering supplies capability costs/code boundaries.
- `designing-business-models` owns payer, price, vertical integration, company/partner boundary; engineer afterward.

## Fill the Technical Decision Record

Use facts; label assumptions. Record:

1. company stage, survival/product state, product type, evidence latency, reversibility, error cost, binding constraint;
2. technical constraint, capable familiar baseline, candidates, and predecessor limitation each removes;
3. falsifiable leverage: capability, delivery time, concepts, defects, reliability, cost, or team size;
4. libraries/interoperability, proficiency, hiring, debugging, deployment, observability, security, procurement, maintenance, migration, vendor/exit costs;
5. bounded representative latency/throughput/recovery/operability workload; owner, decision date, thresholds, portability/fallback seam, branches per candidate or none;
6. when abstracting/decomposing: product/data seams, shared invariants, change frequency, repeated stable vocabulary; an owner able to test/operate/change end-to-end surface; interface, conformance/invariant tests, behavior-preserving migration/fallback, incident responsibility, and retreat if duplication, coordination, call-site, or conceptual cost rises; and
7. choice, rejected alternatives, uncertainty, preserved option, switch condition, next owner, dated review.

For adversarial/asymmetric-error work, also record protected outcome, attacker/probe loop, authorization/ordering/idempotency/audit/recovery invariants, trust boundary, least privilege/blast radius, evaluation data/unequal errors, ordinary/hostile failures, adaptation, collateral harm, monitoring, human review, code/incident owners, and fallback. Add reconciliation or deterministic replay when interruption/reordering requires it.

## Apply the predicates

Choose unusual technology only when evidence shows product-specific net advantage over a capable familiar option after lifecycle/ecosystem costs. Revalidate current conditions.

Abstract only repeated, stable, understood semantics when a primitive reduces conceptual, duplication, call-site, or coordination cost. Keep speculation concrete; prefer fewer concepts, connections, side effects, and defects to line-count cleverness.

Map seams, shared invariants, and change frequency before assigning owners/interfaces. Make the smallest reversible change; preserve delivery. At a dated review, use lead time, handoffs, conflicts, incidents, and recovery to keep, redraw, separate, or retreat.

For adversarial machinery, assume the method is known; choose the simplest testable design meeting its invariants, validate inputs, and inspect harmful failures. For reordering/interruption, require simulation, fault injection, representative replay, and precommitted pass/redesign thresholds. Before high-harm exposure require shadow or sandbox assurance plus current security, privacy, and legal/compliance review. Engineering owns machinery and technical rollback; shipping owns exposure, monitoring, support, rollback gates, incident response, and restitution for moved funds or credible harm.

For non-reversible software, enterprise, hardware, capital-heavy, marketplace/community, or high-harm work, apply [runtime gates](references/runtime-contract.md). Open [provenance](references/provenance.md) or [evidence](references/evidence.json) only for source questions.
