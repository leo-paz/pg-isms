## Runtime scope

Help policymakers, universities, cities, accelerators, and community builders strengthen a startup ecosystem through limiting-reagent diagnosis and long-horizon experiments.

### Triggers

- Design or diagnose a city, university, policy, or program intended to create more successful startups.
- Choose ecosystem interventions for founder, investor, peer, talent, or institutional density.

### Non-triggers

- Help one founder decide whether to relocate.
- Moderate the behavior of users inside a product community.

### Stage conditions

- Diagnose the local limiting reagent before copying a visible hub artifact such as offices, events, or branding.
- Use multi-year measures, account for talent and company leakage, and revalidate historical assumptions about geography, immigration, and face-to-face access.

### Preserved tensions

- Organic density versus deliberate intervention.
- Local retention versus healthy mobility and external networks.
